About 2026-05 · Eli Lilly · Obesity

Met Retatrutide TRIUMPH-1

The triple agonist retatrutide achieved about 28% average weight loss in people with obesity, approaching bariatric surgery. In TRIUMPH-4, in knee osteoarthritis, weight loss was 28.7% with marked reductions in joint pain.

About 2026-04 · Boehringer Ingelheim / Zealand · Obesity

Met Survodutide SYNCHRONIZE-1

The GLP-1/glucagon dual agonist survodutide produced up to 16.6% weight loss at 76 weeks in people with obesity, with marked reductions in visceral and liver fat.
