Target brief

Why it matters

HIF-2α (EPAS1) is a core hypoxia-response transcription factor. When VHL is lost, HIF-2α stays stable and drives VEGF, cyclin D1 and other targets, making it the main oncogenic pathway in clear cell RCC. EPAS1 gain-of-function mutations also occur in pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma. Belzutifan's approval for VHL disease-associated tumors validated the target.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The record tracked here is thin. The one efficacy test, adding belzutifan to Keytruda plus Lenvima (NCT05239728), missed. The NKT2152 stop (NCT05935748) was a portfolio decision and says nothing about the mechanism. Belzutifan Phase 1/2 RCC trials NCT04626518 and NCT04626479, past primary completion, are next to report.

Where the openings are

  • Among programs tracked here, only one addresses pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma and VHL disease. Genetically defined groups with EPAS1 mutations or VHL-driven disease remain a hypothesis worth testing.
  • All four programs are small molecules. Degraders or RNA-based agents might get around acquired resistance mutations in the drug-binding pocket, and no program tracked here has tried them.
  • After the triplet miss, it is still unclear which partners actually add benefit. HIF-2α plus CDK4/6 (the cyclin D1 axis) went untested because NCT05935748 stopped for business reasons. Casdatifan's Phase 3 work may show whether stronger target inhibition makes a difference.
