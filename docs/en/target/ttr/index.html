Missed Eplontersen CARDIO-TTRansform

The TTR antisense oligonucleotide eplontersen did not significantly reduce cardiovascular events in ATTR-CM. 57% of patients were already on a TTR stabiliser at baseline and another 24% started one during the trial; in the prespecified subgroup not on a stabiliser the hazard ratio was 0.71 (nominally significant).
