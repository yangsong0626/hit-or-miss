Target brief

Why it matters

5-HT2A is a Gq-coupled serotonin receptor highly expressed on cortical pyramidal neurons. Classic psychedelics such as psilocybin act through it to induce neuroplasticity, supporting rapid, durable antidepressant effects after one or a few doses. Blocking the same receptor is part of how many atypical antipsychotics work.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin: no readouts or failures are tracked here yet. The first real tests will be CYB003's phase 3 MDD trial NCT06564818 and AbbVie's GM-2505 phase 2 MDD trial NCT06236880. Both have passed their primary completion dates, and results are still pending.

Where the openings are

  • Among programs tracked here, work centers on MDD and TRD. PTSD and adjustment disorder have only a few programs, and other anxiety-related indications are largely open. These could test whether 5-HT2A agonism works across diagnoses.
  • All tracked programs are small-molecule agonists, mostly psilocybin or its analogs. An open question is whether newer molecules (CYB003, GM-2505, bretisilocin) can set themselves apart from psilocybin on session length, durability or tolerability.
  • There are no biomarkers yet for choosing patients or predicting response, and functional unblinding makes controlled trials hard to design. Whether NCT06564818 reproduces the earlier signals in phase 3 will be a key test of how robust the effect is.
