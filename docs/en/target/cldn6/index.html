Target brief

Why it matters

CLDN6 is a tight-junction protein expressed in embryonic epithelia but largely silent in healthy adult tissue, and re-expressed in testicular germ cell tumours, ovarian cancer and subsets of lung, gastric and endometrial cancers. That near-tumour-restricted pattern suits ADCs, T-cell engagers and CAR-T; the main design challenge is selectivity over closely related claudins such as CLDN9, CLDN3 and CLDN4.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Among programs tracked here the clinical record is still thin: no readouts, no reported failures, and no trials reaching primary completion in the next six months. Of 8 programs at 7 companies most are in phase 1; Qilu's phase 3 and BioNTech's phase 2 CAR-T are furthest along.

Where the openings are

  • Germ cell tumours carry the highest CLDN6 expression, yet testicular and extragonadal germ cell tumours each appear for only one tracked program. Relapsed, refractory disease is a small but biologically clean setting to test whether CLDN6 targeting yields deep responses.
  • Outside ovarian cancer coverage is sparse: NSCLC and gastric cancer have one tracked program each and endometrial cancer none. IHC-based selection of CLDN6-high subsets in these tumours, with a validated expression cutoff, is an open question.
  • ADCs, bispecifics and CAR-T each have two programs, so no format leads yet. Which one gives the widest window against CLDN9/3/4 cross-reactivity, and whether Qilu's phase 3 (modality not specified here) sets the benchmark, remain to be shown.
