Met 匹妥布替尼（Pirtobrutinib） BRUIN CLL-313

The non-covalent BTK inhibitor pirtobrutinib cut the risk of progression by 80% in treatment-naive CLL, with an objective response rate of 94% vs 81%. The FDA approved the indication on October 2.
