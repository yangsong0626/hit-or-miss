Met Admilparant ALOFT-IPF

The 60 mg dose of the oral LPA1 antagonist admilparant was significantly better than placebo on FVC decline at 52 weeks; decline was also slower with 120 mg but not statistically significant. Discontinuations for adverse events were similar to placebo in both doses. The company did not disclose the differences.
