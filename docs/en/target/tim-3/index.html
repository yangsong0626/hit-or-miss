Target brief

Why it matters

TIM-3 (HAVCR2) is co-expressed with PD-1 on exhausted CD8+ T cells and is also found on Tregs, myeloid cells and leukemic stem cells in AML/MDS. Its ligands include galectin-9, phosphatidylserine and CEACAM1. In preclinical studies, blocking PD-1 and TIM-3 together restores T-cell function, which made TIM-3 a candidate for overcoming PD-1 resistance.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin. The only readout with a formal test, phase 2/3 COSTAR Lung (NCT04655976), missed OS with cobolimab plus dostarlimab in NSCLC. Incyte's NCT04370704 reported safety only. The cobolimab melanoma trial NCT06521567 is the next test.

Where the openings are

  • Myeloid cancers rest on a different rationale: TIM-3 on leukemic stem cells, not only T-cell exhaustion. Among programs tracked here, only Novartis's sabatolimab addresses MDS/CMML. Whether this path holds up regardless of the NSCLC miss is still open.
  • No readout tracked here has tested biomarker enrichment. Selecting patients for TIM-3/PD-1 co-expression on infiltrating T cells, or for ligand expression, is an untested hypothesis. The upcoming melanoma readout may show whether tumor type matters.
  • All five programs tracked here are monoclonal antibodies. New formats that limit TIM-3 blockade to PD-1+ exhausted T cells, or that act on the myeloid and dendritic cells that also express TIM-3, could show whether the problem was the mechanism or the drug format.
