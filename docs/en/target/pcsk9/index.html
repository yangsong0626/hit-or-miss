Target brief

Why it matters

PCSK9 binds the hepatic LDL receptor and sends it for lysosomal degradation, which raises plasma LDL-C. Gain-of-function mutations cause familial hypercholesterolemia. Loss-of-function variants bring lifelong low LDL-C and lower coronary risk, so the human genetic validation is strong. The antibodies evolocumab and alirocumab have been approved for years. Competition now turns on modality and dosing frequency.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • LDL-C lowering holds up across modalities. The oral peptide MK-0616 (NCT05952856), the fusion protein lerodalcibep in HeFH (NCT04797104) and tafolecimab as monotherapy (per Innovent) all met their primary endpoints. Lipid efficacy alone no longer sets programs apart.
  • Evolocumab's large outcomes trial VESALIUS-CV (NCT03872401) met its composite of CHD death, MI or ischemic stroke. This adds support that lowering LDL-C through PCSK9 leads to fewer cardiovascular events.
  • The only early stop tracked here, NCT06700720, was a small phase 2 plaque-imaging study ended at the sponsor's request (business reasons). No program here stopped for efficacy or safety, so attrition reflects strategy, not the mechanism.
  • The pipeline is moving toward easier dosing. siRNA accounts for 6 of 15 programs, and oral agents (MK-0616, AZD0780, a Salubris small molecule) are in phase 2-3. Many Chinese sponsors, including Qilu, Akeso and Dinovate, are active.

Where the openings are

  • It is still unproven that oral agents improve adherence and long-term event outcomes. AZD0780's phase 3 trials in HeFH (NCT07000136) and cardiovascular disease (NCT07000123), plus MK-0616's NCT07216482, should supply the next data.
  • There is little evidence in the acute setting or at the plaque level. Inclisiran trials in ACS (NCT07102628) and coronary artery disease (NCT05360446) can test early intensive lowering. With NCT06700720 stopped, plaque-imaging endpoints remain an open question.
  • Tracked programs cluster in hypercholesterolemia and ASCVD. Homozygous FH, pediatric and statin-intolerant patients do not appear as separate indications here. Longer dosing intervals or combinations with targets such as Lp(a) are possible ways to stand out in a crowded field.
