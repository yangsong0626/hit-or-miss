Curated readouts

2026-09-22 · Amgen · Moderate-to-severe systemic Sjögren's (ESSDAI ≥ 5)

Met Dazodalibep OASIZ 301

The CD40L antagonist fusion protein dazodalibep significantly improved 48-week disease activity (ESSDAI) in moderate-to-severe systemic Sjögren's, with onset from week 4. No imbalance in thromboembolic events or opportunistic infections. The company did not disclose the effect size.
