Target brief

Why it matters

GIPR is the class B GPCR for the incretin GIP. It is found on beta cells, in fat tissue and in the brain. It boosts glucose-dependent insulin secretion and affects fat storage and appetite. Adding it to GLP-1 agonism, as in tirzepatide, increases glucose and weight lowering. Human GIPR loss-of-function variants are linked to lower BMI, which also supports antagonism.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Weight loss with GIPR-containing multi-agonists is now well supported. Retatrutide reported positive phase 3 results in TRIUMPH-1, -2 and -3, and all nine programs tracked here are in phase 3. The question is no longer whether it works but how to stand out.
  • Weight loss does not automatically move organ-specific endpoints. A phase 2 retatrutide study (NCT05936151) missed its primary endpoint, change in measured GFR at week 24. It is the only efficacy failure tracked here. Kidney claims may need longer, dedicated designs.
  • Agonism and antagonism have both reached phase 3. Amgen's maridebart cafraglutide, an antibody that blocks GIPR and carries GLP-1 agonist peptides, runs alongside eight peptide agonist programs. Since both directions produce weight loss, GIPR's exact role remains unresolved.
  • Cardiovascular outcome evidence still needs interpretation. SURPASS-CVOT (NCT04255433), with over 13,000 participants, is recorded here as having no formal hypothesis test, so it counts as neither a hit nor a miss. Only two programs each address ASCVD and HFpEF.

Where the openings are

  • Few programs cover OSA, liver disease or neurology. Hengrui's two HRS9531 OSA phase 3 trials (NCT06994650, NCT06974851) will test whether GIPR co-agonism adds value in obesity complications.
  • Type 1 diabetes is largely untested. Two tirzepatide phase 3 trials (NCT06962280, NCT06914895) will test its value as an add-on to insulin. Because GIP stimulates glucagon release, hypoglycemia safety deserves close watching.
  • Modalities and patient selection are narrow: eight of nine programs are injectable peptides and one is an antibody, with no oral small molecule. Stratifying by GIPR variants, or mechanistic studies comparing agonism with antagonism, could show which patients benefit most from the GIPR component.
