Target brief

Why it matters

CLDN18.2 is the stomach-specific splice isoform of the tight-junction protein claudin-18. In normal tissue it is confined to differentiated gastric mucosa and buried within tight junctions. After malignant transformation its epitopes become exposed on gastric, gastroesophageal junction and pancreatic tumour cells, which makes it a fairly selective surface target for antibodies, ADCs and CAR-T.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • No registry readouts or failures are tracked yet. A press release reports that sonesitatug vedotin met overall survival in later-line gastric cancer, with data not yet released. The first real tests are AZD0901 in gastric cancer (NCT07143604), zolbetuximab in pancreatic cancer (NCT03816163) and phase 3 NCT06206733 in GEJ adenocarcinoma.

Where the openings are

  • Pancreatic cancer is the main indication outside gastric/GEJ among programs tracked here. NCT03816163 will show whether a naked antibody works in pancreatic disease. If it does not, ADC payloads or CAR-T may be needed there.
  • ADCs are the most crowded modality (10 programs), with several in phase 3 for gastric cancer. One testable way to differentiate is reaching patients with moderate or patchy CLDN18.2 expression, where bystander payloads may help, and setting expression cutoffs for each modality.
  • T-cell engagers are sparse (two bispecifics, including AZD5863), and cell therapy is moving toward dual-target CAR-NK designs. It is still unknown whether these designs can overcome patchy antigen expression and the toxicity expected from hitting normal gastric mucosa.
