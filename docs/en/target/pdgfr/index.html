Target brief

Why it matters

PDGFRα is a receptor tyrosine kinase that drives proliferation and migration of mesenchymal, smooth-muscle and fibroblast cells. Activating PDGFRA mutations drive a subset of GIST. In PAH, PDGF signalling drives pulmonary vascular smooth-muscle proliferation and remodelling. Imatinib studies in PAH suggested the pathway is druggable, but systemic dosing was poorly tolerated.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The PDGFR clinical record tracked here is still thin. There are only two Phase 3 small-molecule programs (GB002 and Ripretinib) and no logged readouts or failures. The first real test is GB002's Phase 3 PAH trial NCT06274801, with primary completion expected in December 2026.

Where the openings are

  • NCT06274801 will answer a key question: can a PDGFR-targeted small molecule deliver efficacy in PAH with acceptable tolerability? Delivery route or kinase selectivity may decide whether it avoids the safety limits of systemic TKIs.
  • No program tracked here addresses fibrotic disease (e.g. IPF, systemic sclerosis), even though fibroblast activation depends heavily on PDGF signalling. This is a hypothesis worth testing.
  • Every program tracked here is a small molecule. Enrolling GIST patients by PDGFRA mutation subtype (e.g. D842V), or trying other modalities such as antibodies or degraders, remains open.
