Target brief

Why it matters

AXL is a TAM-family receptor tyrosine kinase activated by GAS6. It drives epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition, invasion and an immunosuppressive tumor microenvironment. High AXL expression is linked to poor prognosis in several solid tumors and is a known bypass route of resistance to EGFR inhibitors, chemotherapy and immunotherapy. That makes it a candidate target for reversing resistance.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Among programs tracked here, AXL has only one: a Phase 1 small molecule from Xuanzhu Biopharmaceutical in advanced solid tumors and NSCLC. It has no readouts yet, and no trials reach primary completion in the next six months, so the clinical record is too thin to draw lessons from.

Where the openings are

  • Test AXL inhibition in NSCLC that has progressed on EGFR inhibitors and shows high AXL expression or an EMT signature. This would show whether biomarker enrichment turns the resistance hypothesis into a measurable benefit.
  • No antibodies, ADCs or degraders are tracked here. AXL sits on the cell surface and goes up in resistant tumors, so an ADC might kill AXL-high cells without depending on kinase signaling.
  • Because AXL suppresses immune responses, combinations with PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors are worth exploring. So are indications beyond NSCLC, such as blood cancers or fibrosis. No tracked programs address either yet.
