Met Aficamten ACACIA-HCM

The cardiac myosin inhibitor aficamten significantly improved symptom scores and exercise capacity in non-obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. LVEF fell below 50% in 10.5% of treated patients (0.8% on placebo), requiring echocardiographic monitoring.
