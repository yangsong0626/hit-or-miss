Target brief

Why it matters

Cardiac myosin is the sarcomere's motor, hydrolysing ATP to drive contraction. Mutations in MYH7, MYBPC3 and other sarcomere genes cause hypercontractility and are the main genetic basis of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM). Inhibitors reduce actin-myosin cross-bridges and relieve outflow obstruction, and mavacamten is approved for obstructive HCM. Activators aim to boost contraction in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Obstructive HCM is where the evidence for this mechanism is clearest: aficamten's MAPLE-HCM (NCT05767346) met its pVO2 endpoint. Outside obstruction, results split. ACACIA-HCM improved symptoms and exercise capacity in non-obstructive HCM, while mavacamten's ODYSSEY-HCM (NCT05582395) missed on both KCCQ and pVO2.
  • Over-suppressing contraction is a safety issue across the class. In ACACIA-HCM, LVEF fell below 50% in clearly more treated patients than placebo patients, so echo monitoring is needed. Dose titration and monitoring burden may be what mainly separates drugs in this class.
  • Extending myosin inhibition to skeletal muscle has not worked yet. Sevasemten's pivotal trial in Becker muscular dystrophy (NCT06066580) missed for efficacy, so reducing muscle damage has not yet produced functional benefit.
  • BMS-986435 (NCT05556343) was stopped for business reasons, which says nothing against the mechanism. Among programs tracked here, 9 programs sit with just 3 companies, so the field is highly concentrated.

Where the openings are

  • Why ACACIA-HCM and ODYSSEY-HCM diverged in non-obstructive HCM could be traced to genotype, baseline LVEF, biomarkers such as NT-proBNP, or titration strategy. Data from aficamten's non-obstructive trial NCT06081894 will test whether the positive result holds up.
  • No activator readout is tracked yet. Omecamtiv mecarbil is in phase 3 for heart failure, and whether specific subgroups, such as patients with very low ejection fraction, benefit is still open.
  • Pediatric HCM has only one tracked program, and no one has tested whether inhibiting early in genotype-positive patients slows disease. In Becker, phase 2 NCT05291091 will show whether any subgroup or endpoint signal remains after the pivotal miss.
