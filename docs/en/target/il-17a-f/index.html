Target brief

Why it matters

IL-17A and IL-17F are released by Th17 cells, γδ T cells and ILC3s under IL-23 drive. Signalling through IL-17RA/RC, they inflame keratinocytes, synovium and entheses and recruit neutrophils. Secukinumab and ixekizumab validated the pathway in psoriasis, PsA and axial spondyloarthritis. IL-17F is also raised in lesions such as hidradenitis suppurativa, which is the rationale for blocking A and F together.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Sonelokimab met its primary endpoint in two phase 2 trials, in PsA (NCT05640245) and hidradenitis suppurativa (NCT05322473). That supports dual IL-17A/F blockade in both joint and skin disease, and the program has moved into phase 3.
  • Tibulizumab missed in a phase 2 skin-disease trial (NCT06843239). Moving IL-17 blockade outside the core psoriasis, spondyloarthritis and HS indications, or combining it with another mechanism, does not reliably carry the efficacy over.
  • ZL-1102's vehicle-controlled phase 2 was stopped by sponsor decision (NCT06380907). That is a business call, not evidence against the mechanism, so non-injectable or local delivery is still untested.
  • Eleven of the 13 programs tracked here are mAbs, clustered in psoriasis, PsA, HS and axial SpA. With netakimab, picankibart and a secukinumab biosimilar entering, competition is shifting toward indication breadth and price.

Where the openings are

  • HS is the next real test. Results are pending from bimekizumab (NCT04901195, NCT06921850), sonelokimab phase 3 (NCT06768671) and tibulizumab (NCT06993610). They should show whether nanobody and IgG formats differ. Stratifying patients by lesional IL-17F or tunnel burden is worth exploring.
  • Among programs tracked here, there is only one non-antibody program, and it stopped for business reasons. Oral or topical IL-17 inhibitors for mild-to-moderate or needle-averse patients remain open, provided enough drug reaches the tissue.
  • Few programs address juvenile PsA, enthesitis-related arthritis or palmoplantar pustulosis. Along with bimekizumab's axial SpA phase 3 (NCT04436640), pediatric and niche indications are a way to stand apart from the leading products.
