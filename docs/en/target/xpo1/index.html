Missed Selinexor XPORT-EC-042

Weekly oral selinexor maintenance extended median PFS by 5.3 months over placebo, but the hazard ratio's confidence interval crossed 1 and it was not statistically significant. The company then cut its endometrial cancer investment and shifted to myelofibrosis and multiple myeloma.
