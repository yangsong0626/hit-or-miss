Target brief

Why it matters

B7-H4 (VTCN1) is a B7-family co-inhibitory ligand that dampens T-cell proliferation and cytokine release. It is highly expressed in ovarian, breast and endometrial cancers with limited normal-tissue expression, and often does not overlap with PD-L1, making it a candidate antigen for ADCs and other targeted payload delivery.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin: all three programs tracked here are in phase 1-2, none has read out, and no trial reaches primary completion in the next six months. Hansoh's phase 2 ADC is the likeliest first real test.

Where the openings are

  • Among programs tracked here, work centres on advanced solid tumours and ovarian/fallopian tube cancer; breast cancer (including TNBC) and endometrial cancer, where B7-H4 is often highly expressed, have no dedicated program.
  • Test whether B7-H4 IHC thresholds identify ADC responders; because B7-H4 and PD-L1 expression often diverge, PD-L1-negative tumours or PD-1 combinations are a hypothesis worth testing.
  • Every tracked program with a known modality is an ADC; B7-H4×CD3 or ×4-1BB bispecifics and CAR-T are not represented here, leaving its value as a checkpoint or T-cell-redirecting target untested.
