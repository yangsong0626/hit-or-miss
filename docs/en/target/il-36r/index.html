Target brief

Why it matters

IL-36R (IL1RL2) pairs with IL-1RAcP to signal for IL-36α/β/γ, activating keratinocytes and recruiting neutrophils into pustular inflammation. Loss-of-function IL36RN mutations disable the natural antagonist IL-36Ra and cause generalized pustular psoriasis (GPP), giving genetic validation. Spesolimab is approved for GPP flares.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin. The only readout tracked here, spesolimab in hidradenitis suppurativa (NCT06241573), had a safety primary endpoint with no formal test and is logged as stopped early with no reason given. It neither shows efficacy nor counts against the mechanism. No tracked readouts are due in the next six months.

Where the openings are

  • Whether the mechanism reaches beyond GPP into other neutrophilic dermatoses is still untested. Hidradenitis suppurativa and pyoderma gangrenosum have one tracked program each, and efficacy-powered trials could settle it.
  • Patient selection is an open question. Enrolling by IL36RN, CARD14 or AP1S3 variants, or by a lesional IL-36 signature, could test whether these subgroups respond better outside GPP.
  • Both tracked programs are monoclonal antibodies (spesolimab and the phase 2 HB0034). Among programs tracked here, there is no oral small molecule, topical or bispecific yet, which leaves room in long-term maintenance or flare prevention.
