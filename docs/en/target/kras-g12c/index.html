Target brief

Why it matters

KRAS G12C swaps glycine for cysteine at codon 12. This impairs GTP hydrolysis, so KRAS stays active and drives RAS–MAPK signaling. It occurs in a notable share of lung adenocarcinomas and in smaller fractions of colorectal and pancreatic cancers. The mutant cysteine let covalent inhibitors reach a target once called undruggable, and the approvals of sotorasib and adagrasib validated the approach.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Recent exits have been commercial. Novartis stopped JDQ443 in phase 3 (NCT05132075) and with trametinib (NCT05358249) for business reasons and stated these were not safety-related. Seven phase 3 programs are tracked here, and these stops are not evidence against the mechanism.
  • Single-agent activity has limits. Sotorasib's phase 2 CodeBreaK201 in NSCLC (NCT04933695) was stopped for futility. The colorectal phase 3 CodeBreak300 (NCT05198934) has no formal test recorded here, so the registry record does not yet show a clear win.
  • Of 13 tracked programs, 11 are small molecules. Late-stage work is led by newer covalent agents (olomorasib, divarasib, JAB-21822, D-1553), and the RAS(ON) inhibitor elironrasib is in early development. Reaching the target is no longer the hurdle. Programs now have to stand out on potency, tolerability and combination partners.

Where the openings are

  • Pancreatic cancer has only three tracked programs, against ten in NSCLC. G12C is rare in pancreatic cancer, but the tumor depends heavily on KRAS. It could test whether deeper, more durable suppression from RAS(ON) inhibitors or combinations leads to benefit.
  • Every tracked program with a known modality is a small molecule. Degraders, vaccines or TCR therapies aimed at G12C neoantigens do not appear here. Trials that select patients by co-mutations such as STK11 or KEAP1 are another open design question.
  • Adagrasib in NSCLC (NCT05609578) and advanced cancer (NCT05853575), and JAB-21822 in NSCLC (NCT05009329), have passed primary completion with no results posted. They are the next data on whether activity holds across treatment lines and tumor types.
