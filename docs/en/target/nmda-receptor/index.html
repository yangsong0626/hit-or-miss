Target brief

Why it matters

NMDA receptors are glutamate-gated calcium channels built from GluN1 and GluN2A-D subunits and are central to synaptic plasticity and memory. Ketamine's rapid antidepressant effect, the approvals of esketamine for treatment-resistant depression and memantine for Alzheimer's disease, plus GRIN gene variants and anti-NMDAR encephalitis, give clinical, genetic and immunological validation.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Oral low-affinity channel blockers have not reproduced ketamine-like rapid antidepressant effects: REL-1017 missed in MDD in NCT04855747, and the sister study NCT06011577 was stopped early after the same interim sample-size re-estimation.
  • Both MDD studies were stopped after sample-size re-estimation, which suggests the true effect was smaller than the design assumed. When effects are this modest, dose choice and placebo-response control may decide outcomes as much as the mechanism does.
  • AXS-05 missed the CMAI endpoint in Alzheimer's agitation in ADVANCE-2 (NCT05557409). The companion long-term safety study NCT06736509 closed because program objectives were complete. Its data are descriptive and are not evidence of efficacy.
  • Every failure recorded here is coded as efficacy (three unique trials), with no safety or business stops. Among programs tracked here, the main risk for this target is the size of the effect, not tolerability.

Where the openings are

  • Subunit-selective and allosteric approaches are still early: radiprodil (GRIN, Phase 1/2, GluN2B-selective) and zelquistinel (Syndeio, Phase 2, positive modulator) could test whether non-blocking mechanisms avoid the efficacy ceiling seen with broad blockers.
  • Immune-mediated neuropsychiatric indications are barely covered. Among programs tracked here, only one antibody program (Arialys) targets neuropsychiatric lupus. Selecting patients by anti-NMDAR antibody status is a hypothesis worth testing.
  • No primary completions are tracked in the next six months. The two J&J esketamine Phase 3 programs and the Phase 2 studies of EG-501 and zelquistinel could use target-engagement markers such as EEG to guide dosing and reduce uncertainty about effect size.
