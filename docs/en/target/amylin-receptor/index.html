Curated readouts

2026-10-07 · Zealand Pharma / Roche · Overweight or obesity with type 2 diabetes

Met Petrelintide ZUPREME-2

The weekly long-acting amylin analogue petrelintide produced 7.4–9.2% weight loss at 28 weeks in obesity with diabetes, with lower HbA1c. Gastrointestinal discontinuations were 1.9%, similar to placebo.
