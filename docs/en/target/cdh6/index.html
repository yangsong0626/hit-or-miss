Target brief

Why it matters

CDH6 (K-cadherin) is a type II cadherin expressed mainly in fetal kidney. Adult normal tissues express little of it, but serous ovarian cancer, renal cell carcinoma and other tumours overexpress it, and it internalises. That gap between tumour and normal tissue, plus internalisation, makes it a good fit for ADCs and other therapies that target surface antigens.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin: this site has no tracked CDH6 readouts or failures yet. All three Phase 3 programs centre on Daiichi Sankyo/MSD's raludotatug deruxtecan (R-DXd). The first real test is the Phase 2 gastrointestinal cancer trial NCT06864169. Its listed primary completion date is 2026-08-18, and this site has not tracked results yet.

Where the openings are

  • Among programs tracked here, ovarian and broad solid-tumour indications dominate, and no program focuses on renal cell carcinoma. Given that CDH6 is expressed in kidney cancer, that indication looks worth testing.
  • None of the programs tracked here is a bispecific, CAR-T or radioconjugate. Later entrants such as Hansoh, Qilu and Phrontline could set themselves apart from DXd-based ADCs with a different payload, linker or modality.
  • Whether efficacy depends on how much CDH6 a tumour expresses is still unknown. NCT06864169 may give a first signal on whether lower-expressing tumours outside ovarian cancer respond, and whether patients need to be selected by expression level.
