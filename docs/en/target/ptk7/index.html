Target brief

Why it matters

PTK7 is a catalytically inactive pseudokinase receptor in Wnt/planar cell polarity signalling. It is overexpressed in ovarian cancer, triple-negative breast cancer and non-small cell lung cancer, is enriched on tumour-initiating cells, and has limited expression in most normal adult tissue. That profile makes it a candidate for ADCs and other therapies aimed at surface antigens.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin. All three programs tracked here are phase 1 ADCs: Whitehawk's HWK-007 and one unnamed drug each from Day One and Kivu. None has reported a readout, and no trial reaches primary completion in the next six months, so no efficacy or safety pattern can be drawn yet.

Where the openings are

  • Tracked programs focus on ovarian, endometrial and all-comer solid tumours. PTK7 is also reported to be highly expressed in triple-negative breast cancer and NSCLC, yet no program tracked here is dedicated to either. These are indication hypotheses worth testing.
  • Every tracked program is an ADC. No bispecific, CAR-T or radioconjugate approaches appear yet. Because PTK7 is enriched on tumour-initiating cells, one hypothesis is that these modalities could reach cell subsets that ADCs clear poorly.
  • It is still unclear how three similar phase 1 ADCs will differentiate. Selecting patients by PTK7 expression level, and the choice of payload and linker, are likely key questions for dose-expansion cohorts.
