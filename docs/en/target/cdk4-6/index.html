Target brief

Why it matters

CDK4 and CDK6 bind cyclin D and phosphorylate RB. This releases E2F and moves cells from G1 into S phase. Estrogen signalling drives cyclin D1, so HR+/HER2- breast cancer depends heavily on this pathway, and palbociclib, ribociclib or abemaciclib plus endocrine therapy is standard first-line care. RB loss and cyclin E–CDK2 activation are the main resistance routes.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The three early stops tracked here (NCT05935748, NCT05573555, NCT05358249) all combined an approved CDK4/6 inhibitor with a new partner (HIF-2α, an ER degrader, KRAS G12C). All were ended for portfolio reasons. NCT05573555 carries an efficacy code, but the sponsor says the stop was unrelated to efficacy.
  • Development is moving toward CDK4-selective agents. Among selective programs tracked here, atirmociclib is furthest along, at phase 3. Its earlier trials (NCT06206837, NCT06465368) used DLTs, response rate and complete cell-cycle arrest at day 14 as descriptive endpoints, so no formal efficacy comparison exists yet.
  • Outside breast cancer, the readouts are dose-finding or descriptive only: Ewing sarcoma (NCT03709680) and ccRCC (NCT05935748). Neither tested a hypothesis. Three programs list mCRPC, but in this record, extension to other cancers is still unproven.
  • Biomarker work is advancing. BioItaLEE (NCT03439046) linked PFS to early ctDNA changes at cycle 1 day 15 and ended early once it had enough data. NCT03671330 reported PFS by menopausal cohort. Both are descriptive, so neither counts as a hit or a miss.

Where the openings are

  • Hypothesis to test: sparing CDK6 widens the therapeutic window enough to allow fuller target coverage. Complete cell-cycle arrest at day 14, the endpoint used in NCT06465368, could serve as an early go/no-go marker, but randomized comparisons with first-generation agents are still needed.
  • Patients who progress after CDK4/6 therapy remain largely unaddressed. Only one degrader is tracked here (NiKang's NKT5097, which targets CDK2), so adding CDK2 inhibition in cyclin E-high tumors, and CDK4/6 degraders, are both little explored.
  • Only one readout is due in the next six months: NCT05909397, a phase 3 palbociclib breast cancer trial with primary completion on 2026-12-30. Outside breast cancer, selecting patients by RB status or CCND1 amplification could test whether benefit in mCRPC or sarcoma is real.

Class and pathways

Class: Kinases › Cell-cycle kinases
Same family: CDK2 · CDK7 · CDK9 · WEE1 · PKMYT1 · Aurora A · PLK1 · CDC7

Hormone-driven cancers · upstream ER (ESR1) / AR
KAT6 → ER (ESR1) / AR → CDK4/6
KAT6 controls ER transcription; ER drives breast cancer (via cyclin D and CDK4/6) and AR drives prostate cancer.
Cell cycle · downstream CDK2 / CDC7
CDK4/6 → CDK2 / CDC7 → WEE1 / PKMYT1 → Aurora A / PLK1 / KIF18A
CDK4/6 and CDK2 push G1/S; WEE1 and PKMYT1 gate G2/M; Aurora A, PLK1 and KIF18A run mitosis.
