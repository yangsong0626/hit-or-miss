Target brief

Why it matters

TROP2 (TACSTD2) is a transmembrane glycoprotein that relays calcium signals and drives tumour proliferation and invasion. It is overexpressed in many epithelial cancers, including breast, lung, urothelial and endometrial tumours. It is internalised after antibody binding, which makes it well suited to ADCs. Sacituzumab govitecan has been approved for metastatic triple-negative breast cancer since 2020, which shows the approach works.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Of the 17 programs tracked here, 11 are ADCs, and all three phase 3 assets are ADCs with topoisomerase-I payloads. Antibody, bispecific and cell-therapy approaches are still in phase 1-2, so clinical validation of this target comes almost entirely from ADCs.
  • Benefit depends on tumour type. In TROPiCS-04 (NCT04527991), sacituzumab govitecan missed overall survival in pretreated urothelial carcinoma. A press release says sacituzumab tirumotecan improved OS and PFS in endometrial cancer after platinum and PD-(L)1 therapy (TroFuse-005). Broad TROP2 expression alone does not predict a survival gain.
  • All tracked failures are for efficacy, and all come from one urothelial phase 3. No stops for safety or business reasons appear. The urothelial phase 2 NCT05327530 had no formal hypothesis test, so it neither rescues the urothelial case nor weakens it further.

Where the openings are

  • Patient selection is still open. The tracked trials are organised by tumour type, not by TROP2 level. One hypothesis to test: quantitative or membrane-localised TROP2 scoring separates responders. That could help explain the urothelial miss and improve trial designs in weaker indications.
  • Several phase 3s will show whether the endometrial result extends to other tumours: sacituzumab tirumotecan in NSCLC (NCT06448312, NCT06711900) and gastroesophageal cancer (NCT06356311), and sacituzumab govitecan in breast cancer (NCT04639986). All reach primary completion by end-2026.
  • Formats beyond topoisomerase-I ADCs are barely explored. Only one bispecific (AK146D1) and one NK cell therapy (EB-NK-301) are tracked. Different payloads or non-ADC formats could be tested in patients who progress on a TROP2 ADC. Non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NCT06637423) is another early direction.
