Target brief

Why it matters

PD-1 on activated T cells binds PD-L1 on tumor and myeloid cells to dampen T-cell activity, which tumors exploit to escape immune attack. Blocking the axis restores anti-tumor immunity; pembrolizumab, nivolumab and others are long-established standards across many cancers. With 231 programs from 170 companies tracked here, it is the most crowded checkpoint target.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Perioperative benefit is not universal: neoadjuvant pembrolizumab in TNBC (NCT03036488) and adjuvant atezolizumab in muscle-invasive bladder cancer (NCT04660344) met their endpoints, while neoadjuvant atezolizumab plus nab-paclitaxel in NSCLC missed EFS (NCT03456063).
  • Selection and bispecifics are raising the bar: pembrolizumab monotherapy beat chemotherapy on PFS in first-line dMMR endometrial cancer (KEYNOTE-C93), and ivonescimab plus chemotherapy improved OS over durvalumab plus chemotherapy in first-line biliary cancer (HARMONi-GI1).
  • Most attrition is commercial: of 42 coded stops, 24 were business-driven and 10 efficacy-driven, including acasunlimab (NCT05117242), botensilimab plus balstilimab (NCT05529316) and small-molecule INCB099280 (NCT05888844). This reflects crowding, not evidence against the mechanism.
  • Gains on top of the backbone are incremental: lenvatinib plus pembrolizumab in HCC only partly met its primary endpoints (NCT04246177), and subcutaneous nivolumab was assessed on pharmacokinetics (NCT04810078), showing lifecycle competition shifting to formulation and convenience.

Where the openings are

  • About 30 bispecifics are tracked here; whether a second arm beyond VEGF beats a PD-1 antibody is unsettled. The volrustomig (PD-1/CTLA-4) phase 3 in NSCLC (NCT05984277) and tobemstomig in renal cancer (NCT05805501) are near-term tests.
  • Patient selection remains open: dMMR status predicted a monotherapy win, but colorectal cancer (22 programs) has no formal efficacy test here, with NCT04952753 and NCT05589597 stopped early. Biomarkers separating monotherapy responders from those needing a bispecific or combination are a gap.
  • Outside oncology the field is nearly empty, with only 2 immune/inflammation trials tracked. PD-1 agonism to restrain autoreactive T cells, and oral small molecules (20 programs) for convenience or combination dosing, remain little-tested hypotheses among programs tracked here.
