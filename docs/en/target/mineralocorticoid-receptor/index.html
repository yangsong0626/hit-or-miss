Target brief

Why it matters

The mineralocorticoid receptor is a nuclear receptor activated by aldosterone. Overactivation in kidney, heart and vessels drives sodium retention, inflammation and fibrosis, which push hypertension, heart failure and CKD forward. Spironolactone and eplerenone have long been used in heart failure. Nonsteroidal finerenone added kidney and cardiovascular protection in diabetic CKD, but hyperkalemia limits use of the class.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The record tracked here is still thin: just two finerenone readouts, both using UACR as a surrogate endpoint. FINE-ONE (NCT05901831) met its endpoint in CKD. CONFIDENCE (NCT05254002) ran no formal test of its combination comparison. The next real test is the Phase 3 CKD trial NCT05196035, due April 2027.

Where the openings are

  • Among programs tracked here, AstraZeneca's balcinrenone is the only Phase 3 MR program besides finerenone. A hypothesis to test: can a newer nonsteroidal MR modulator widen the potassium safety window enough to stand apart, especially in combination regimens?
  • Most activity is in diabetic CKD. Finerenone is also being studied in type 1 diabetes and pediatric heart failure/LV dysfunction. Starlight's spironolactone in glioblastoma is the only oncology program; biomarkers could help select MR-dependent patients.
  • Every readout so far uses UACR. Still open: does albuminuria reduction lead to hard-outcome benefit in type 1 diabetic and non-diabetic CKD, and can predictors of hyperkalemia risk be found?

Class and pathways

Class: Nuclear receptors and transcription factors › Hormone nuclear receptors
Same family: ER (ESR1) · AR · GR (glucocorticoid receptor) · THR-β · FXR

RAAS and blood pressure · upstream AT1 receptor / Aldosterone synthase (CYP11B2)
Renin / AGT → AT1 receptor / Aldosterone synthase (CYP11B2) → Mineralocorticoid receptor / Endothelin receptor
Renin cleaves AGT to make angiotensin acting on AT1; aldosterone synthase makes aldosterone acting on the mineralocorticoid receptor; endothelin is a separate vasoconstrictor axis.
