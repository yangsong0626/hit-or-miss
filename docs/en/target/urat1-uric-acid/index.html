Target brief

Why it matters

URAT1 (SLC22A12) reabsorbs filtered urate in the proximal tubule, and most gout patients under-excrete urate. Loss-of-function SLC22A12 variants cause renal hypouricemia, which is genetic validation. Xanthine oxidase produces urate and is the target of allopurinol and febuxostat. Keeping serum urate below target dissolves urate crystals and reduces flares.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin. The only registry readout tracked here is a Dotinurad PK study, NCT06056570, with no formal test. AR882's positive Phase 3 comes only from a company announcement, with no registry readout yet. The first real test will be the Tigulixostat Phase 3 NCT07414394, primary completion April 2027.

Where the openings are

  • Every program tracked here is an oral small molecule aimed at urate lowering in gout. None tracked here pairs a URAT1 inhibitor with a xanthine oxidase inhibitor for patients who miss target on one drug. That combination is a testable hypothesis.
  • Measuring fractional urate excretion to separate under-excreters from over-producers could guide the choice between a uricosuric and an XO inhibitor. Kidney safety in patients with CKD has long been a concern for uricosurics and needs dedicated study.
  • Tracked programs use serum urate as the endpoint. Tophus resolution, flare rates and kidney or cardiovascular outcomes are still open questions. NCT07414394 will give a first answer on whether a newer XO inhibitor can stand apart from existing drugs.
