Target brief

Why it matters

Cleaving C5 releases C5a, which recruits and activates neutrophils through C5aR1, and C5b, which starts the membrane attack complex (C5b-9). In PNH, red cells lack CD55/CD59, so terminal complement drives intravascular hemolysis. Complement-mediated injury also underlies aHUS, AChR-positive gMG and AQP4-positive NMOSD. Eculizumab and ravulizumab have long been approved for PNH.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • In PNH, where complement directly causes disease, C5 blockade works reliably: ravulizumab (NCT06578949) and eculizumab (NCT05886244) both met LDH endpoints. Both trials were small and look more like population-extension or bridging studies than new tests of the mechanism.
  • Moving into heterogeneous, unselected injury settings failed. Ravulizumab missed in TMA (NCT04543591), and ARTEMIS (NCT05746559) was stopped early for lack of efficacy. In both settings complement is only one of several drivers.
  • Crovalimab's aHUS readouts, COMMUTE-a (NCT04861259) and COMMUTE-p (NCT04958265), were single-arm and descriptive with no formal hypothesis test. They are supportive evidence, not confirmation.
  • Among programs tracked here, competition is mostly about dosing and format: subcutaneous antibodies, a peptide, siRNA and oral small molecules. BioCryst's oral PNH program REDEEM-1 (NCT05116774) stopped by sponsor decision, a business stop that says nothing against the mechanism.

Where the openings are

  • The TMA and AKI misses suggest patient enrichment is needed outside classic indications. One hypothesis to test: selecting patients by complement-activation markers such as sC5b-9, or by complement gene variants, could reveal a benefit in heterogeneous populations.
  • PNH is crowded, while gMG and NMOSD have fewer programs. Readouts for gefurulimab (NCT06607627), zilucoplan (NCT04225871, NCT06055959) and eculizumab in NMOSD (NCT06724809) will test whether long-acting or self-administered dosing sets programs apart.
  • NCT05133531 compares pozelimab with ravulizumab and eculizumab in PNH and will show whether a new regimen gives deeper or more durable C5 blockade. C5 inhibition does not address C3-mediated extravascular hemolysis, and few programs tracked here target C5a/C5aR1 selectively while sparing the MAC.
