Class and pathways

Class: Nuclear receptors and transcription factors › Hormone nuclear receptors
Same family: AR · GR (glucocorticoid receptor) · Mineralocorticoid receptor · THR-β · FXR

Hormone-driven cancers · upstream KAT6 · downstream CDK4/6
KAT6 → ER (ESR1) / AR → CDK4/6
KAT6 controls ER transcription; ER drives breast cancer (via cyclin D and CDK4/6) and AR drives prostate cancer.
