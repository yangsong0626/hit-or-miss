Target brief

Why it matters

JAK kinases (JAK1/2/3, TYK2) relay STAT signalling from receptors for IL-6, interferons, γc cytokines and EPO/TPO. Activating JAK2 mutations such as V617F drive myeloproliferative neoplasms including myelofibrosis, while cytokine loops sustain autoimmune and inflammatory skin disease. Long-standing approvals of ruxolitinib and tofacitinib provide clinical validation.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin: only TRuE-AD4 (NCT06238817, ruxolitinib in atopic dermatitis) met its primary endpoint, and the other stops were for enrollment or business reasons. The ruxolitinib hidradenitis suppurativa Phase 3s (NCT06958211, NCT06959225) will be the next real test.

Where the openings are

  • Only two hidradenitis suppurativa programs are tracked here. NCT06958211 and NCT06959225 will test whether JAK inhibition reaches this neutrophil-driven follicular inflammation, which opens a path to biomarkers that pick out responders.
  • Of the 20 programs tracked here, 19 are small molecules, with no degraders and no locally or tissue-restricted agents. Such designs might keep local efficacy while reducing the class safety risks of systemic JAK inhibition.
  • Myelofibrosis combinations remain unsettled. RVU120 plus ruxolitinib stopped on a sponsor decision (NCT06397313), which says nothing against the mechanism, and NCT02158858 had no formal test. Selecting patients by mutation or anemia status could test whether combinations go beyond spleen response.
