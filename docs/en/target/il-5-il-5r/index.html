Target brief

Why it matters

IL-5 drives eosinophil differentiation, activation and survival, and IL-5Rα is expressed mainly on eosinophils. Neutralizing IL-5, or depleting eosinophils through IL-5Rα, treats eosinophil-driven diseases such as eosinophilic asthma, EGPA and HES. Approvals of mepolizumab and benralizumab in severe eosinophilic asthma have validated the mechanism clinically.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin. The only readout tracked here is NATRON (NCT04191304), where benralizumab met its primary endpoint of time to first HES worsening. The next tests are depemokimab in EGPA (NCT05263934), benralizumab in asthma (NCT05692180) and depemokimab in asthma (NCT07671001).

Where the openings are

  • Among programs tracked here, every program is a Phase 3 monoclonal antibody from one of two companies. No oral small molecules or bispecifics (for example, pairing IL-5 with TSLP or IL-13) appear. For patients with overlapping type 2 pathways, these designs remain testable hypotheses.
  • COPD appears in the condition list of only one program. Eosinophilic COPD, with patients selected by blood eosinophil count, is barely covered in the tracked pipeline and could be a biomarker-led direction to explore.
  • It is still unclear whether neutralizing IL-5 (depemokimab) or depleting eosinophils via IL-5Rα (benralizumab) works better in systemic eosinophilic disease. Reading the EGPA results of NCT05263934 alongside NATRON may show whether a long-interval neutralizing antibody can match a depleting one.
