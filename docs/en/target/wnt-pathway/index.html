Met Remigromig（MK-3000） BRUNELLO

Remigromig, a trispecific antibody that activates the Wnt pathway to repair the blood–retinal barrier, was non-inferior to ranibizumab on vision. But the treatment arm had more proliferative diabetic retinopathy, vitreous haemorrhage and discontinuations for adverse events, and the company is analysing further.
