Target brief

Why it matters

Wnt/β-catenin signalling controls stem-cell renewal, tissue repair and fibrosis. CTNNB1 mutations drive a subset of hepatocellular carcinoma, and Norrin/FZD4 signalling maintains the blood-retina barrier. Mutations in that axis cause familial exudative vitreoretinopathy. So both blocking and activating the pathway are plausible therapeutic strategies.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • Both late-stage small-molecule studies tracked here missed their primary endpoints: lorecivivint on week-12 pain in a phase 3 knee osteoarthritis trial (NCT05603754), and topical SM04554 on hair count in androgenetic alopecia (NCT03742518). All recorded failures are coded as efficacy.
  • The only positive signal so far comes from a targeted antibody. Remigromig, a tri-specific Wnt-activating antibody, was non-inferior to ranibizumab on vision in BRUNELLO. Retinal Norrin/FZD4 genetics support this indication.
  • Activating the pathway brings safety liabilities. The remigromig arm had more proliferative diabetic retinopathy, vitreous haemorrhage and discontinuations due to adverse events. Non-inferiority alone does not set it apart from existing drugs, and the benefit-risk balance is still under analysis.
  • The field is small: 5 programs from 4 companies are tracked here, and no trials reach primary completion in the next six months. New data to test these patterns is unlikely soon.

Where the openings are

  • Mediar's MTX-463 (phase 2) is the only idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis program tracked here. Whether an antibody acting on Wnt-related signalling can improve functional endpoints in a fibrotic organ has not yet been tested.
  • Oncology has only one phase 1 program, in advanced hepatocellular carcinoma. A testable hypothesis: enrolling patients by CTNNB1 mutation or β-catenin activation could make a signal easier to detect.
  • After BRUNELLO, the open question in the eye is whether more receptor-selective agonists, such as Surrozen's phase 1 antibody, can separate barrier repair from proliferative disease. Selecting patients by baseline retinopathy grade, or combining with anti-VEGF, are angles worth testing.
