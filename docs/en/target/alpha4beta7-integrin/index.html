Target brief

Why it matters

α4β7 integrin on gut-homing memory T cells binds MAdCAM-1 on intestinal vascular endothelium and steers lymphocytes into the gut. Blocking this interaction dampens intestinal inflammation while leaving systemic immune surveillance intact. Vedolizumab's long-standing approval in ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease validated this gut-selective approach.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record tracked here is still thin. Only one vedolizumab Phase 3 study, NCT04738942, has read out, and it was descriptive with no formal hypothesis test. A news report says an oral α4β7 inhibitor met its Phase 2 UC endpoint. No trials tracked here complete in the next six months, so no near-term test is scheduled.

Where the openings are

  • Oral small molecules are the main gap. MORF-057, the only small molecule tracked here, is in Phase 2, and another oral inhibitor plans to enter Phase 3. The open question is whether oral agents can come close to antibody efficacy while offering easier dosing.
  • Indications cluster in UC and Crohn's disease, with only one program in pouchitis. Post-surgical or other niche IBD populations may be under-studied.
  • No tracked program selects patients by biomarker. One hypothesis to test is whether stratifying by MAdCAM-1 expression or α4β7 receptor occupancy picks out patients who respond better.
