Target brief

Why it matters

Endothelin-1 acting through the ETA receptor drives vasoconstriction, podocyte injury, mesangial proliferation, inflammation and fibrosis, all of which push up proteinuria and speed kidney function loss. Endothelin receptor antagonists are long validated in pulmonary arterial hypertension (bosentan, ambrisentan). Development has now shifted to proteinuric kidney diseases such as IgA nephropathy and FSGS, and to resistant hypertension.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record is still thin. The only readout tracked here is Novartis's small phase 2 ASSIST trial (NCT05834738), where atrasentan met its primary endpoint of proteinuria reduction at week 12 in IgA nephropathy. That is a surrogate signal. The first real test will come from two trials in CKD with high proteinuria: phase 2 NCT06942910 (primary completion has passed, results not yet tracked) and phase 3 NCT06087835 (due February 2027).

Where the openings are

  • Among programs tracked here, FSGS has two programs but no readouts yet. It is still open whether ETA blockade works beyond IgA nephropathy in podocytopathies, and whether patients need to be selected by cause or genetic background.
  • CKD with high proteinuria regardless of cause is a broader indication. NCT06087835 may show whether combining with RAS or SGLT2 inhibitors keeps the proteinuria benefit while controlling fluid retention, and whether harder endpoints such as eGFR slope support it.
  • Only one program tracked here targets resistant hypertension: CSPC Zhongnuo's phase 3 aprocitentan. Pharmazz's peptide programs, including sovateltide, use different receptor pharmacology and are worth assessing on their own as a way to stand apart.

Class and pathways

Class: GPCRs › Cardiovascular and renal receptors
Same family: AT1 receptor · Bradykinin B2 receptor · P2Y12

RAAS and blood pressure · upstream AT1 receptor / Aldosterone synthase (CYP11B2)
Renin / AGT → AT1 receptor / Aldosterone synthase (CYP11B2) → Mineralocorticoid receptor / Endothelin receptor
Renin cleaves AGT to make angiotensin acting on AT1; aldosterone synthase makes aldosterone acting on the mineralocorticoid receptor; endothelin is a separate vasoconstrictor axis.
