Target brief

Why it matters

α-synuclein is the main component of Lewy bodies; misfolded aggregates are thought to spread between neurons in a prion-like way, driving Parkinson's disease and other synucleinopathies. SNCA point mutations and gene multiplications cause familial Parkinson's, and GWAS link the SNCA locus to sporadic disease, giving direct genetic support.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • The clinical record tracked here is still thin: only Roche's antibody prasinezumab has reached Phase 3, plus a newly registered, unnamed XingImaging Phase 1 program with no modality stated. There are no readouts, no failures and no primary completions due in the next six months, so it is too early to say where the mechanism works.

Where the openings are

  • Programs tracked here all target Parkinson's disease. Multiple system atrophy and dementia with Lewy bodies are also synucleinopathies, and no programs for them appear here, so they could serve as additional settings to test the mechanism.
  • Among programs tracked here, there are no ASO or siRNA programs to lower SNCA, no small-molecule aggregation inhibitors and no active vaccines. These could test whether cutting protein production works better than clearing extracellular aggregates.
  • α-synuclein seed amplification assays (SAA) could be used to enrol early-stage patients with confirmed pathology. This would make trial populations less mixed and could set a program apart from existing antibody trials.
