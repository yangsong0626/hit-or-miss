Target brief

Why it matters

BCL-2 is an anti-apoptotic protein that binds BH3-only proteins and BAX/BAK, which holds back mitochondrial cell death. CLL (13q14 miR-15/16 loss), follicular lymphoma (t(14;18)) and AML often depend on it to survive. Venetoclax, a selective BH3 mimetic, validated the target with approvals in CLL and AML.

What the clinic has taught so far

  • None of the four readouts tracked here had a formal hypothesis test. Both early stops (NCT04161885 VIALE-T, NCT06508489) were for strategic reasons, not efficacy or safety. The first real tests will come from venetoclax in myeloma (NCT03539744) and CLL (NCT03836261), and sonrotoclax in CLL (NCT06637501).

Where the openings are

  • Four small molecules tracked here are in Phase 3 (venetoclax, sonrotoclax, lisaftoclax, TQB3909). Later entrants may need to stand out by overcoming resistance mutations like BCL2 G101V, simplifying dose ramp-up, or running fixed-duration BTK-inhibitor combinations. NCT06637501 may offer early signals.
  • Beyond CLL and AML, few programs here address myeloma (NCT03539744) or Waldenström macroglobulinemia (NCT05952037). Biomarkers such as t(11;14) could pick out BCL-2-dependent patients. VIALE-T (NCT04161885) stopped for strategic reasons, so post-transplant AML maintenance remains unanswered.
  • Non-small-molecule approaches tracked here have only reached Phase 1 (e.g., Bio-Path's antisense BP1002). No programs here test degraders, or combinations with MCL-1 or BCL-xL blockade to counter adaptive resistance. Both are open hypotheses.
