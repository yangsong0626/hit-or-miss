KVD824-201 NCT05055258

A Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Different Doses of KVD824 for Prophylactic Treatment of HAE Type I or II

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “The Rate of Investigator-confirmed HAE Attacks During the Tr”: HAE rate ratio (Active vs. Placebo) 0.45 (95% CI 0.18–1.14), p=0.0918
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=33
  • Serious adverse events: 300 mg KVD824 BID 14%; 600 mg KVD824 BID 12%; 900 mg KVD824 BID 29%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Sample size only 33, limited power to detect a moderate effect
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 29 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Why it stopped

Safety · “Terminated by Sponsor due to adverse events reported”
