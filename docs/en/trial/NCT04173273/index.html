Hit or Miss / Trials / CULTIVATE

Pfizer · Phase 2/3 · Immunology / inflammation

CULTIVATE NCT04173273

A Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Oral Etrasimod in the Treatment of Adult Participants With Moderately to Severely Active Crohn's Disease

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants With Endoscopic Response by SES-C”: Percentage Difference 2.50 (90% CI -6.10–11.10), p==0.3147
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=379
  • Serious adverse events: SSA: Placebo in Induction Period 0%; SSA: Etrasimod 2 mg in Induction Period 5%; SSA: Etrasimod 3 mg in Induction Period 2%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 100 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. In CULTIVATE, etrasimod's endoscopic response in Crohn's disease induction was almost identical to placebo, and the whole study stopped after sub-study 1 lacked efficacy. S1P modulators working in ulcerative colitis but repeatedly failing in Crohn's is common to this class.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The study was prematurely discontinued due to a lack of efficacy in sub-study 1”
