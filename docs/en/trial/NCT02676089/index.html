Chiesi Farmaceutici · Phase 3 · Immunology / inflammation

TRIGGER NCT02676089

TRIple in Asthma hiGh strenGth vErsus Ics/Laba hs and tiotRopium (TRIGGER)

Partly met

  • Primary endpoint “1_Change From Baseline in Pre-Dose Forced Expiratory Volume ”: Least square mean difference 0.07 (95% CI 0.03–0.12), p=0.003
  • Primary endpoint “2_Moderate and Severe Asthma Exacerbation Rate Over the 52-W”: Adjusted rate ratio 0.88 (95% CI 0.75–1.03), p=0.110
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1437
  • Serious adverse events: CHF 5993 200/6/12.5 µg 5%; CHF 1535 200/6 µg 6%; CHF 1535 200/6 µg + Tiotropium Respimat 2.5 µg 5%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo

Editor’s analysis

Partly met. In TRIGGER, high-dose triple inhalation improved FEV1 but the exacerbation rate ratio of 0.88 was not significant. Compared with TRIMARAN, adding a LAMA on top of high-dose ICS adds less on exacerbations.
