A Study of Efruxifermin in Subjects With Compensated Cirrhosis Due to Nonalcoholic Steatohepatitis (NASH) (Symmetry)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Main: Change From Baseline in Fibrosis With no Worsening Ste”: Point estimate percentage difference 7.80 (95% CI -7.45–23.04), p=0.2868
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=213
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo (Main Study) 18%; EFX 28 mg (Main Study) 26%; EFX 50 mg (Main Study) 24%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 26 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
