PROMIS-II NCT03460704

Trial in Non-cystic Fibrosis Bronchiectasis Patients With Chronic Lung Infections Treated With Colistimethate Sodium.

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=287
  • Serious adverse events: CMS (Colistimethate Sodium) (SAF) 18%; Placebo (SAF) 13%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Enrollment / operations”

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “The study was brought to an early close in March 2022 due to major ongoing recruitment issues primarily related to the COVID-19 pandemic and concerns regarding the continued randomization and exposure of subjects to placebo in the study.”
