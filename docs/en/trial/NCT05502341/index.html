ARTISTRY-1 NCT05502341

Study to Compare Bictegravir/Lenacapavir Versus Current Therapy in People With HIV-1 Who Are Successfully Treated With a Complicated Regimen (ARTISTRY-1)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Phase 2: Percentage of Participants With Human Immunodeficie”: Difference in Percentage 1.90 (95% CI -12.20–10.70), p=1.000
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=689
  • Serious adverse events: Phase 2: Treatment Group 1: BIC 75 mg + LEN 25 mg 6%; Phase 2: Treatment Group 2: BIC 75 mg + LEN 50 mg 4%; Phase 2: Treatment Group 3: SBR 8%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 10 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
