Phase III Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety in Patients With Ankle Sprains.

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Sum of Pain Intensity Difference (SPID) on Pain-on-movement ”: Mean Difference (Net) 471 (95% CI 280–662), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=70
  • Serious adverse events: TK-254RX 0%; Placebo Patch 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
