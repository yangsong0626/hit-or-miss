MARIPOSA NCT05071612

Parallel Arm Trial of AD109 and AD504 In Patients With OSA

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change in Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI4) From Baseline: Combine”: Least Squares Mean Difference -7.17 (95% CI -10.31–-4.04), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=294
  • Serious adverse events: AD109 75/5mg 0%; AD109 75/2.5mg 0%; AD504 75/100mg 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
