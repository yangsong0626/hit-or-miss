Phase 3 Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of Batoclimab as Induction and Maintenance Therapy in Adult Participants With Generalized Myasthenia Gravis

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=233

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Sponsor decision / strategy”

Editor’s analysis

Strategic decision. Immunovant closed its batoclimab trial in myasthenia gravis to advance the next-generation FcRn inhibitor IMVT-1402. Batoclimab lowers albumin and raises LDL, which the next molecule aims to fix.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Sponsor Decision”
