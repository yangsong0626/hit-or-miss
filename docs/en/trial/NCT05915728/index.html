Quanti OBR NCT05915728

A Study to Compare How Well Gadoquatrane Works and Its Safety With an Already Available Contrast Agent for MRI in People With Any Known or Suspected Problems of the Body (Except Brain or Spinal Cord-related Problems)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Visualization Parameter Contrast Enhancement Assessed by Sep”: Difference in mean average score 2.28 (95% CI 2.17–2.40), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Visualization Parameter Delineation Assessed by Separate Bli”: Difference in mean average score 2.16 (95% CI 2.04–2.28), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=410
  • Serious adverse events: Gadoquatrane 0%; SoC GBCAs 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust

Editor’s analysis

Met. Bayer's next-generation gadolinium agent gadoquatrane beat an approved macrocyclic agent on image-enhancement scores in body MRI. The selling point is a lower gadolinium dose; the crossover design and blinded reading make the result fairly reliable.
