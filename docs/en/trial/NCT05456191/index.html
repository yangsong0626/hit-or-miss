Novartis · Phase 3 · Oncology

ASC4START NCT05456191

A Study to Investigate Tolerability and Efficacy of Asciminib (Oral) Versus Nilotinib (Oral) in Adult Participants (≥18 Years of Age) With Newly Diagnosed Philadelphia Chromosome Positive Chronic Myelogenous Leukemia in Chronic Phase (Ph+ CML-CP)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Time to Discontinuation of Study Treatment Due to Adverse Ev”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.45 (95% CI 0.25–0.81), p=0.004
  • Primary endpoint “Time to Discontinuation of Study Treatment Due to Adverse Ev”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.46 (95% CI 0.27–0.76), p=0.001
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=568
  • Serious adverse events: Asciminib 17%; Nilotinib 15%; All Participants 16%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased

Editor’s analysis

Met. ASC4START's primary endpoint was time to discontinuation for adverse events; asciminib cut it by about 55% vs nilotinib. A tolerability-driven trial proving "better tolerated" rather than an efficacy endpoint, used to support first-line asciminib.
