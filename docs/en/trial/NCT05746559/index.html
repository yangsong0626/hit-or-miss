ARTEMIS NCT05746559

ARTEMIS: Ravulizumab to Protect Patients With CKD From CSA-AKI and MAKE

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Estimated Percentage of Participants Who Experienced Major A”: Rate Difference 1.80 (95% CI -6.40–10.00), p=0.6651
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=555
  • Serious adverse events: Ravulizumab 48%; Placebo 52%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. ARTEMIS tested the complement C5 inhibitor ravulizumab for preventing acute kidney injury after cardiac surgery and stopped in April 2026 for lack of efficacy in the overall population. Acute kidney injury has complex causes and complement is only one pathway, so a single-mechanism intervention struggles to change outcomes.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The ALXN1210-CSA-AKI-318 trial was discontinued on 29-Apr-2026 due to lack of efficacy in this overall study universe / patient population.”
