Ascendis · Phase 2/3 · Rare / genetic

ApproaCH NCT05598320

A Clinical Trial to Evaluate Efficacy and Safety of TransCon CNP Compared With Placebo in Children With Achondroplasia

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Annualized Growth Velocity (AGV) at Week 52”: Least Square Mean Difference 1.49 (95% CI 1.05–1.93), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=84
  • Serious adverse events: Navepegritide 5%; Placebo for Navepegritide 11%; Open-Label Extension Period: Navepegritide 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 11 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
