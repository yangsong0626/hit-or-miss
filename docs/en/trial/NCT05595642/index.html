ARNASA NCT05595642

A Study to Evaluate Astegolimab in Participants With Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Annualized Rate of Moderate and Severe COPD Exacerbations Ov”: Adjusted Rate Ratio 0.82 (95% CI 0.69–0.97), p=0.0239
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1380
  • Serious adverse events: Astegolimab Q2W 24%; Astegolimab Q4W 27%; Placebo 24%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
