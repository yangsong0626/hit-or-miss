A Study of Seltorexant as Adjunctive Therapy to Antidepressants in Adult and Elderly Participants With Major Depressive Disorder With Insomnia Symptoms Who Have Responded Inadequately to Antidepressant and Long-term Safety Extension Treatment With Seltorexant

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Double Blind (DB) Treatment Phase: Change From Baseline to D”: Least square (LS) mean difference -2.60 (95% CI -4.53–-0.74), p==0.007
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=588
  • Serious adverse events: Double Blind (DB) Treatment Phase: Placebo 0%; DB Treatment Phase: Seltorexant 20 mg 0%; OL Treatment Phase: Placebo to Seltorexant 20 mg 6%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 6 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. The orexin-2 receptor antagonist seltorexant as adjunctive therapy in major depression with insomnia symptoms improved MADRS about 2.6 points more than placebo. A moderate effect; mechanistically it improves sleep and mood together, suited to the insomnia subgroup.
