A Study to Check the Safety of Dexlansoprazole and Learn If it Can Heal Erosive Esophagitis (EE) and Keep it Healed in Children 2 to 11 Years Old

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “EE Healing Period: Percentage of Participants With Healing o”: Difference in percentage of participants 4.20 (95% CI -28.10–37.10), p==1
  • Primary endpoint “Maintenance of Healed EE Period: Percentage of Participants ”: Difference in percentage of participants -5.60 (95% CI -43.80–35.00), p==1
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=24
  • Serious adverse events: EE Healing Period: Dexlansoprazole 30 mg 0%; EE Healing Period: Dexlansoprazole 60 mg 0%; Maintenance of Healed EE Period: Dexlansoprazole 1 11%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • Sample size only 24, limited power to detect a moderate effect
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 11 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
