MK-7684A With or Without Other Anticancer Therapies in Participants With Selected Solid Tumors (MK-7684A-005) (KEYVIBE-005)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Objective Response Rate (ORR) Per Response Evaluation Criter”: Difference in Percentage 4.50 (95% CI -7.20–16.30), p=0.2215
  • Primary endpoint “Progression-Free Survival (PFS) Per RECIST 1.1 as Assessed b”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.99 (95% CI 0.70–1.38), p=0.4787
  • Design: randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=611
  • Serious adverse events: Cohort A1 MK-7684A 51%; Cohort A1 Pembrolizumab 40%; Cohort A2 MK-7684A 52%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 58 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
