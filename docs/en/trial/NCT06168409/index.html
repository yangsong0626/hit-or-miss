Bax24 NCT06168409

A Study to Investigate the Effect of Baxdrostat on Ambulatory Blood Pressure in Participants With Resistant Hypertension

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Least Square Mean Difference for Change From Baseline in Amb”: Least-squares mean difference -14.00 (95% CI -17.20–-10.80), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=218
  • Serious adverse events: Baxdrostat 2 mg 1%; Placebo 1%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
