Hit or Miss / Trials / KALOS

AstraZeneca · Phase 3 · Immunology / inflammation

KALOS NCT04609878

Study to Assess PT010 in Adult and Adolescent Participants With Inadequately Controlled Asthma (KALOS)

Partly met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in FEV1 AUC0-3 (L) at Week 24”: LS Mean Difference 0.07 (95% CI 0.03–0.11), p=<0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Pooled (KALOS/LOGOS): Rate of Severe Asthma Exacerbations”: Rate ratio 0.88 (95% CI 0.75–1.03), p=0.120
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=2274
  • Serious adverse events: BGF MD I320/14.4/9.6 μg BID 5%; BGF MDI 320/28.8/9.6 μg BID 5%; BFF MDI 320/9.6 μg BID 6%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo

Editor’s analysis

Partly met. In KALOS, budesonide/glycopyrronium/formoterol triple inhalation improved FEV1, but the severe exacerbation rate ratio pooled with LOGOS was 0.88 with the confidence interval crossing 1. A clear lung-function benefit with insufficient evidence of fewer exacerbations, which will limit triple therapy's role in asthma.
