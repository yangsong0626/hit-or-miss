CADENCE NCT04945460

A Study of Sotatercept for the Treatment of Cpc-PH Due to HFpEF (MK-7962-007/A011-16)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Base Study: Change From Baseline in Pulmonary Vascular Resis”: Hodges-Lehmann Location Shift -81.51 (95% CI -144–-18.61), p=0.004
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=165
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo-Controlled Treatment Period: Sotatercept 0 20%; Placebo-Controlled Treatment Period: Sotatercept 0 33%; Placebo-Controlled Treatment Period: Placebo 22%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 14 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
