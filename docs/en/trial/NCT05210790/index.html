VERIFY NCT05210790

A Study of Rusfertide in Adults With Polycythemia Vera (PV)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Part 1a: Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Response”: Odds Ratio (OR) 6.42 (95% CI 3.81–10.80), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=293
  • Serious adverse events: Part 1a: Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Period: Pl 5%; Part 1a: Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Period: Ru 3%; Part 1b: Open-Label Treatment Phase 3%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
