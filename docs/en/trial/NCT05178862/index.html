MARIO NCT05178862

A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-blind Study for Patients With Invasive Candidiasis Treated With IV Echinocandin Followed by Either Oral Ibrexafungerp or Oral Fluconazole

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “All-cause Mortality”: IV Echinocandin Followed by Oral Ibrexafungerp (SC 28.00 Participants; IV Echinocandin Followed by Oral Fluconazole or Be 28.00 Participants
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=68
  • Serious adverse events: IV Echinocandin Followed by Oral Ibrexafungerp (SC 56%; IV Echinocandin Followed by Oral Fluconazole or Be 62%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Strategic decision not related to safety or efficacy concerns for Ibrexafungerp.”
