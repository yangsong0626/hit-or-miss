BRUIN-CLL-313 NCT05023980

A Study of Pirtobrutinib (LOXO-305) Versus Bendamustine Plus Rituximab (BR) in Untreated Patients With Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (CLL)/Small Lymphocytic Lymphoma (SLL)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Arm A Compared to Arm B: Progression-free Survival (PFS), As”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.20 (95% CI 0.11–0.37), p=<.0001
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=282
  • Serious adverse events: Arm A - Pirtobrutinib 31%; Arm B - Bendamustine Plus Rituximab 31%; Arm B - Crossover to Pirtobrutinib 6%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 26 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
