A Trial of Setmelanotide in Acquired Hypothalamic Obesity

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Pivotal Cohort: Mean Percent Change From Baseline in Body Ma”: Least square mean difference -19.83 (95% CI -24.55–-15.10), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=143
  • Serious adverse events: Setmelanotide 28%; Placebo 6%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 21 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
