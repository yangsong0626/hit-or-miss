A Study of Lebrikizumab in Adolescent Participants With Uncontrolled Asthma Who Are on Inhaled Corticosteroids and a Second Controller Medication

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Rate of Asthma Exacerbations During 52-Week Placebo Controll”: Placebo 0.43 events per p; Lebrikizumab 37.5 mg 0.26 events per p; Lebrikizumab 125 mg 0.21 events per p
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=346
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 5%; Lebrikizumab 37.5 mg 5%; Lebrikizumab 125 mg 7%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

A knock-on of programme termination. Lebrikizumab's adult asthma phase 3 results were inconsistent (one positive, one negative), so Roche stopped asthma development and this adolescent trial ended with it. The drug was later approved in atopic dermatitis.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Sponsor decision to discontinue lebrikizumab development for the treatment of asthma.”
