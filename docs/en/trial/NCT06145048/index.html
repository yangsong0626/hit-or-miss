VISUALIZE NCT06145048

Phase 2B Safety and Efficacy Study of VGT-309 in Subjects With Cancer in the Lung.

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Clinically Significant Events (CSE)”: single group proportion 0.45 (95% CI 0.34–0.56), p=<.0001
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=89

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Single-arm trial; the p value compares against baseline or a preset threshold, not a control arm, so the evidence is weaker than a randomised trial
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
