A Phase 3 Study to Evaluate Efficacy and Safety of AL001 in Frontotemporal Dementia (INFRONT-3)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Part 1 Double Blind - Evaluation of Efficacy of AL001 Compar”: Mean Difference (Final Values) 1.51 (95% CI -0.96–3.98), p=0.2278
  • Primary endpoint “Part 1 Double Blind (Co-Primary US Endpoint) - Evaluation of”: Geometric Mean Ratio of Fold Change 2.65 (95% CI 2.35–2.98), p=<.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=119
  • Serious adverse events: Part 1 Blinded - AL001 15%; Part 1 Blinded - Placebo 7%; Part 2 (OLE Treatment) - AL001 6%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 10 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. Latozinemab (AL001, an anti-sortilin antibody that raises progranulin) in GRN-mutant frontotemporal dementia missed the co-primary endpoint of slowing clinical progression. Biomarker changes did not translate into clinical benefit, a classic dilemma for neurodegeneration drugs.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The trial did not meet the clinical co-primary endpoint of slowing FTD-GRN progression, as measured by the Clinical Dementia Rating® plus National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center Frontotemporal Lobar Degeneration Sum of Boxes (CDR® plus NACC FTLD-SB)”
