Almirall · Phase 3 · Immunology / inflammation

TirbAKare NCT06135415

A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tirbanibulin Ointment in Adult Participants With Actinic Keratosis

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in Lesion Count at Day 57”: Least-squares (LS) mean difference -38.92 (95% CI -48.20–-29.64), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=280
  • Serious adverse events: Vehicle Ointment 2%; Tirbanibulin 10 mg/g 5%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
