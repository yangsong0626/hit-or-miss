RILECSU NCT05107115

Rilzabrutinib for the Treatment of Chronic Spontaneous Urticaria in Patients Who Remain Symptomatic Despite the Use of H1 Antihistamine

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Weekly Urticaria Activity Score (UAS”: LS Mean Difference -6.75 (95% CI -12.23–-1.26), p=0.0159
  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Weekly Itch Severity Score (ISS7) at”: LS Mean Difference -3.44 (95% CI -6.25–-0.62), p=0.0168
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=160
  • Serious adverse events: DB Period: Placebo TID 2%; DB Period: Rilzabrutinib 400 mg QPM + Placebo 0%; DB Period: Rilzabrutinib 400 mg BID + Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 11 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
