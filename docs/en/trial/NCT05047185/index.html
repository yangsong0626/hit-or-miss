Pharvaris Netherlands · Phase 2 · Immunology / inflammation

HAE CHAPTER-1 NCT05047185

Dose-ranging Study of Oral PHA-022121 for Prophylaxis Against Angioedema Attacks in Patients With Hereditary Angioedema Type I or Type II

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Investigator-confirmed Hereditary Angioedema (HAE)”: Percent Reduction 84.46 (95% CI 53.83–94.77), p=0.0008
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=34
  • Serious adverse events: Part 1: Placebo 0%; Part 1: Deucrictibant 10 mg BID 0%; Part 1: Deucrictibant 20 mg BID 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 7 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Why it stopped

Regulatory · “Sponsor decision. All remaining participants (21 total) were rolled over from Part 2 to study PHA022121-C307 (NCT06679881).”
