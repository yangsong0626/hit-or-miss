Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline on Log-transformed Mixed Meal Tolerance”: p=0.368

Likely reasons

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The DSMB concluded that there was a low probability the study would meet it is primary endpoint as designed.”
