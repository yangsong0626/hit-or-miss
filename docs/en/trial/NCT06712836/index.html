VESPER-1 NCT06712836

A Phase 2b Study to Examine the Safety and Efficacy of Once-Weekly MET097 in Adults With Obesity or Overweight

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in Body Weight at Week 28: Part”: LS Mean Difference -8.09 (95% CI -10.58–-5.59), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=239
  • Serious adverse events: Part A: Placebo QW 0%; Part A: Berobenatide 0.4 mg QW 4%; Part A: Berobenatide 0.6 mg QW 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
