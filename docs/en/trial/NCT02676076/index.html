Met

  • Primary endpoint “1_Change From Baseline in Pre-Dose Forced Expiratory Volume ”: Adjusted mean difference 0.06 (95% CI 0.01–0.10), p=0.008
  • Primary endpoint “2_Moderate and Severe Asthma Exacerbation Rate Over the 52-W”: Adjusted rate ratio 0.85 (95% CI 0.72–0.99), p=0.033
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1155
  • Serious adverse events: CHF 5993 100/6/12.5 µg 5%; CHF 1535 100/6 µg 4%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful

Editor’s analysis

Met. TRIMARAN added a LAMA (single-inhaler triple) in asthma poorly controlled on medium-dose ICS/LABA; FEV1 and exacerbations both improved, but the exacerbation rate ratio of 0.85 had a confidence interval close to 1. A clear lung-function benefit, with a smaller reduction in exacerbations.
