EPCORE DLBCL-1 NCT04628494

A Phase 3 Trial of Epcoritamab vs Investigator's Choice Chemotherapy in Relapsed/Refractory (R/R) Diffuse Large B-cell Lymphoma (DLBCL)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival (OS)”: Stratified hazard ratio 0.96 (95% CI 0.77–1.20), p=0.7285
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=483
  • Serious adverse events: Epcoritamab 69%; Investigator's Choice (IC) 32%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 37 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
