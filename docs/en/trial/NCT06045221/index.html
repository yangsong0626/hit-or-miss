Hit or Miss / Trials / ACHIEVE-3

Eli Lilly · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

ACHIEVE-3 NCT06045221

A Study of Orforglipron (LY3502970) Compared With Semaglutide in Participants With Type 2 Diabetes Inadequately Controlled With Metformin

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) [Non-inferior”: Least Squares Mean difference -0.71 (95% CI -0.86–-0.55), p=<.001
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1698
  • Serious adverse events: 12 mg Orforglipron 5%; 36 mg Orforglipron 9%; 7 mg Semaglutide 4%

Likely reasons

  • This is a non-inferiority test; meeting it means no worse than the comparator, not better
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased

Editor’s analysis

Met (non-inferiority test). ACHIEVE-3 was a head-to-head trial of orforglipron vs oral semaglutide, with about 0.7 points more HbA1c reduction. The primary endpoint was designed for non-inferiority, but the direction and size of the difference already suggest superiority to oral semaglutide, important data in the oral GLP-1 race.
