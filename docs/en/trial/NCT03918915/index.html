Sydnexis · Phase 3 · Ophthalmology

STAR NCT03918915

The Safety and Efficacy of SYD-101 in Children With Myopia

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Myopic Progression <-0.75 D (Diopters)”: p==0.0226
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=852
  • Serious adverse events: Part 1: SYD-101 0.01% 1%; Part 1: SYD-101 0.03% 4%; Part 1: Vehicle 3%

Likely reasons

  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. Low-dose atropine eye drops SYD-101 reduced the share of children with rapidly progressing myopia, p=0.023. A moderate effect; another entrant in the low-dose atropine race, differentiated mainly by formulation stability.
