A Study of Ociperlimab With Tislelizumab Compared to Pembrolizumab in Participants With Untreated Lung Cancer

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival (OS) in Arms A and B”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.97 (95% CI 0.76–1.23), p=0.4050
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=669
  • Serious adverse events: Arm A: Ociperlimab Plus Tislelizumab 52%; Arm B: Pembrolizumab Plus Placebo 39%; Arm C: Tislelizumab Plus Placebo 47%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 18 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Missed. AdvanTIG-302 compared the TIGIT antibody ociperlimab + tislelizumab with pembrolizumab in first-line PD-L1-high non-small cell lung cancer; OS HR 0.97, no difference, and the trial stopped early. Serious adverse events were 13 percentage points higher in the combination arm. Together with tiragolumab's repeated failures, TIGIT plus PD-1 in lung cancer is essentially disproven.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “This decision was conducted by the sponsor based on lack of efficacy and not driven by safety concerns.”
