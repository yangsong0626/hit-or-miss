OPTIMIZE-2 NCT06128369

A Phase 3 Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of OCS-01 Opthalmic Suspension Compared to Vehicle for the Treatment of Ocular Inflammation and Pain Following Cataract Surgery

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Absence of Anterior Chamber Cells at Visit 6.”: OCS-01 40.00 Participants; Vehicle 15.00 Participants
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=117
  • Serious adverse events: OCS-01 0%; Vehicle 0%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

Administrative reasons. The OCS-01 (dexamethasone ophthalmic suspension) trial for post-cataract inflammation and pain closed because a third-party administrative error made the results unanalysable, unrelated to product quality or safety.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Sponsor decision to close study due to third-party administrative error preventing analysis of trial results. There was no issue in terms of quality of the investigational product, and no safety risk to any study participant.”
