CabOSTar NCT06341712

Effects of Maintenance Cabozantinib+BSC Versus BSC in Children and AYA With Osteosarcoma

No statistical test

  • Design: randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=10
  • Serious adverse events: Cabozantinib + BSC 40%; BSC Alone 0%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “The sponsor has decided to terminate the CabOSTar study due to ongoing recruitment challenges”
