Add-on Reparixin in Adult Patients With ARDS

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change in Oxygenation Index (OI) From Baseline to Day 7 of T”: Adjusted mean difference 1.30 (95% CI -2.10–4.70), p=0.735
  • Primary endpoint “Ventilator-Free Days (VFD)”: Adjusted mean difference -0.90 (95% CI -5.60–3.80), p=0.625
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=66
  • Serious adverse events: Reparixin 57%; Placebo 55%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • Sample size only 66, limited power to detect a moderate effect
