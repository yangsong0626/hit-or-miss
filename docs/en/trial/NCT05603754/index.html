Safety and Efficacy of Lorecivivint (SM04690) for the Treatment of Knee Osteoarthritis (STRIDES)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline OA Pain in the Target Knee as Assessed ”: Mean Difference (Final Values) -0.12 (95% CI -0.53–0.28), p=0.543
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=499
  • Serious adverse events: Lorecivivint 1%; Vehicle 1%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high

Editor’s analysis

Missed. Intra-articular lorecivivint, a Wnt pathway inhibitor, for knee osteoarthritis had a pain-score difference of -0.12, almost no different from vehicle. Placebo (intra-articular injection) responses are high in osteoarthritis pain trials, and structural improvement may take longer than the trial to translate into less pain, a common problem for disease-modifying osteoarthritis drugs.
