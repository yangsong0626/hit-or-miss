A Study of Nipocalimab in Adults With Primary Sjogren's Syndrome (pSS)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Clinical European League Against Rhe”: Mean Difference (Final Values) -2.65 (90% CI -4.03–-1.28), p==0.002
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=163
  • Serious adverse events: Group 1: Placebo IV q2w 5%; Group 2: Nipocalimab 5 mg/kg q2w 8%; Group 3: Nipocalimab 15 mg/kg q2w 7%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
