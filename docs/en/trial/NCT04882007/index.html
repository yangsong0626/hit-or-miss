OSE Immunotherapeutics · Phase 2 · Immunology / inflammation

CoTikiS NCT04882007

Study of OSE-127 vs Placebo in Patients With Moderate to Severe Active Ulcerative Colitis

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Modified Mayo Score (MMS) at Week 10”: Least Square Mean Difference -1.16 (95% CI -2.12–-0.19), p=0.019
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=136
  • Serious adverse events: Lusvertikimab 850 mg Induction Period 6%; Lusvertikimab 450 mg Induction Period 8%; Placebo Induction Period 6%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
