A Study to Learn About Zavegepant as the Acute Treatment of Migraine in Asian Adults

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants With Pain Freedom at 2 Hours Post”: Difference in Percentage 9.20 (95% CI 5.00–13.30), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants With Freedom From Most Bothersome”: Difference in Percentage 12.80 (95% CI 7.50–18.10), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1414
  • Serious adverse events: Zavegepant: On-Treatment Period 0%; Placebo: On-Treatment Period 0%; Zavegepant: Follow-up Period 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. The nasal CGRP receptor antagonist zavegepant in Asian migraine patients raised 2-hour pain freedom by about 9 points and freedom from the most bothersome symptom by about 13 points, consistent with pivotal trials in Western populations and supporting filings in Asia.
