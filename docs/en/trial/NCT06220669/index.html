A Study of LY3541860 in Adult Participants With Relapsing Multiple Sclerosis

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Cumulative Number of New T1 Gadolinium-Enhancing (GdE) Lesio”: Relative reduction estimate from AIPW 0.29 (90% CI -0.74–0.71), p=0.531
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=65
  • Serious adverse events: LY3541860 High Dose 9%; Placebo / LY3541860 Low Dose 6%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • Sample size only 65, limited power to detect a moderate effect
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The interim analysis data failed to demonstrate the intended treatment effect.”
