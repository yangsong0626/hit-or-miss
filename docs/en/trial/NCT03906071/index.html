SAPPHIRE NCT03906071

Phase 3 Study of Sitravatinib Plus Nivolumab vs Docetaxel in Patients With Advanced Non-Squamous Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

Missed

  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=577
  • Serious adverse events: Nivolumab + Sitravatinib 46%; Docetaxel 37%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Other”

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. SAPPHIRE tested the multi-target TKI sitravatinib + nivolumab against docetaxel in non-small cell lung cancer progressing after immunotherapy; overall survival missed the primary endpoint. The idea of "using a TKI to remodel the immune microenvironment and reverse immune resistance" was not confirmed in phase 3.

Why it stopped

Other · “Inability to meet protocol objectives”
