AstraZeneca · Phase 3 · Oncology

DUO-O NCT03737643

Durvalumab Treatment in Combination With Chemotherapy and Bevacizumab, Followed by Maintenance Durvalumab, Bevacizumab and Olaparib Treatment in Advanced Ovarian Cancer Patients

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Progression-free Survival (PFS) by Investigator Assessment U”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.63 (95% CI 0.52–0.76), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Progression-free Survival (PFS) by Investigator Assessment U”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.49 (95% CI 0.34–0.69), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1407
  • Serious adverse events: Global Non-tBRCAm: SoC 35%; Global Non-tBRCAm: SoC + Durvalumab 44%; Global Non-tBRCAm: SoC + Durvalumab + Olaparib 40%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 13 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. DUO-O in newly diagnosed advanced ovarian cancer added durvalumab and olaparib to chemotherapy + bevacizumab; PFS HR 0.63 in the non-BRCA-mutant population. The benefit comes mainly from olaparib, and the PD-L1 antibody's own contribution is limited, consistent with several negative immunotherapy results in ovarian cancer.
