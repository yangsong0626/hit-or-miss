Study of OCA in Combination With BZF Evaluating Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability in Participants With PBC

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)”: Least square mean difference -44.70 (95% CI -72.60–-16.70), p=0.003
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=72
  • Serious adverse events: DB Period: Placebo + BZF 100 mg 0%; DB Period: OCA 5 mg + BZF 100 mg 0%; DB Period: Placebo + BZF 400 mg 16%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 16 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
