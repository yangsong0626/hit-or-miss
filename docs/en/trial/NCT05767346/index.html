MAPLE-HCM NCT05767346

Phase 3 Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Aficamten Compared to Metoprolol Succinate in Adults With Symptomatic oHCM

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change in Peak Oxygen Uptake (pVO2) by Cardiopulmonary Exerc”: Least Squares Mean Difference 2.30 (95% CI 1.52–3.07), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=175
  • Serious adverse events: Aficamten 8%; Metoprolol 7%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
