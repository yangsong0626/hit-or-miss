Relacorilant in Combination With Nab-Paclitaxel in Advanced, Platinum-Resistant… NCT05257408

Relacorilant in Combination With Nab-Paclitaxel in Advanced, Platinum-Resistant, High-Grade Epithelial Ovarian, Primary Peritoneal, or Fallopian-Tube Cancer

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Progression-free Survival (PFS) as Assessed by Blinded Indep”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.70 (95% CI 0.54–0.91), p=0.0076
  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.65 (95% CI 0.51–0.83), p=0.0004
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=381
  • Serious adverse events: Relacorilant 150 mg With Nab-paclitaxel 80 mg/m^2 35%; Nab-paclitaxel 100 mg/m^2 24%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 11 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. ROSELLA in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer: the glucocorticoid receptor antagonist relacorilant + nab-paclitaxel vs nab-paclitaxel alone, PFS HR 0.70 and OS HR 0.65, both endpoints positive. Few new therapies show OS benefit in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer, so this matters.
