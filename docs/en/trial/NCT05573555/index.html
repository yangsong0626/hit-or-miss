TACTIVE-U: A Study to Learn About the Study Medicine (Vepdegestrant) When Given With Other Medicines in People With Advanced or Metastatic Breast Cancer (Sub-Study B)

Stopped

  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=20

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”
  • Only 20 patients enrolled at termination, too few to judge the drug itself

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Enrollment was permanently closed for strategic business reasons after 20 participants enrolled in Ph 1b. The decision was not related to safety, efficacy, benefit-risk, or participant welfare. The planned Ph 2 and DDI components were not initiated.”
