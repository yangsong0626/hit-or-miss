AtDventure NCT05999799

A Dose Finding Study to Investigate the Safety and Effectiveness of GSK1070806 in Adult Participants With Moderate to Severe Atopic Dermatitis

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline (CFB) in Eczema Area and Severi”: Placebo -44.53 Percent Chan; GSK1070806 Dose Level 1 -42.86 Percent Chan; GSK1070806 Dose Level 2 -69.95 Percent Chan
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=161
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 0%; GSK1070806 Dose Level 1 0%; GSK1070806 Dose Level 2 0%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Sponsor decision to early terminate the study based on the pre-defined futility criterion for efficacy. No safety concerns were identified.”
