TRANQUILITY NCT06362759

A Study to Evaluate TOUR006 in Patients With Chronic Kidney Disease and Elevated Hs-CRP

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Time-Averaged Percent Change From Baseline in High-Sensitivi”: Median Difference (Net) -59.82 (95% CI -79.69–-39.94), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=143
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 17%; TOUR006 - 25 MG 20%; TOUR006 - 50 MG 17%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 11 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
