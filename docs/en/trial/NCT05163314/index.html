A Study of Soticlestat as an Add-on Therapy in Children and Adults With Dravet Syndrome or Lennox-Gastaut Syndrome

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants With At Least One Treatment Emergent ”: Soticlestat 293 Participants
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=352

Likely reasons

  • Single-arm or uncontrolled design; the registry has only descriptive data, so success cannot be judged statistically and needs comparison with historical controls or similar drugs

Editor’s analysis

Stopped as a consequence of efficacy failure. Soticlestat (a cholesterol 24-hydroxylase inhibitor) was negative in the phase 3 SKYLINE and SKYWAY trials in Dravet and Lennox-Gastaut syndromes, so Takeda closed this supplementary study. Rare epilepsies already have several approved drugs; a new mechanism needs a clear efficacy advantage to matter.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Due to negative results from phase 3 SKYLINE and SKYWAY studies and unrelated to patient safety it has been determined that supplementary data from TAK-935-3003 study is no longer necessary. Therefore, Takeda has made a decision to close this study”
