A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Adjunctive Troriluzole in Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in the Y-BOCS Total Score in the Baseli”: Least squares mean difference 0.97 (95% CI 0.02–1.92), p=0.041
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=589
  • Serious adverse events: Troriluzole 1%; Placebo 1%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met, but the direction needs checking. Troriluzole as adjunctive therapy in obsessive-compulsive disorder registered a Y-BOCS difference of +0.97, p=0.041, with a lower confidence limit of 0.02 almost at 0. The sign depends on the subtraction direction used at registration; the effect is very small, and the programme's other phase 3 result should be read alongside.
