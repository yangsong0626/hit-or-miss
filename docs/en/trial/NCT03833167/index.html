Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) Versus Placebo Following Surgery and Radiation in Participants With Locally Advanced Cutaneous Squamous Cell Carcinoma (MK-3475-630/KEYNOTE-630)

Missed

  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=450
  • Serious adverse events: Pembrolizumab 25%; Placebo 19%; Pembrolizumab (Retreatment Phase) 0%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. KEYNOTE-630 gave adjuvant pembrolizumab after surgery and radiotherapy for locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma and stopped for futility. PD-1 works well in advanced cutaneous SCC but failed to reduce recurrence in the adjuvant setting, like failed adjuvant immunotherapy in liver and head and neck cancer.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The study stopped due to futility”
