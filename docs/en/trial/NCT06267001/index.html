SKYSCRAPER-15 NCT06267001

A Study of Tiragolumab Plus Atezolizumab Compared With Placebo Plus Atezolizumab in Participants With Completely Resected Non-small Cell Lung Cancer Who Have Received Adjuvant Platinum-based Chemotherapy

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants With Adverse Events (AEs)”: Atezolizumab ± Placebo 26.00 Participants; Tiragolumab + Atezolizumab 21.00 Participants
  • Design: randomised, open-label, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=56
  • Serious adverse events: Atezolizumab ± Placebo 18%; Tiragolumab + Atezolizumab 38%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Decision to terminate study was driven by results from preceding tiragolumab study (SKYSCRAPER-06;NCT04619797) \& sponsor's strategic priorities, not due to safety risks or quality reasons for any of molecules/combinations investigated in this study.”
