PINNACLE NCT05378763

A Study of Poziotinib in Previously Treated Participants With Locally Advanced or Metastatic NSCLC Harboring HER2 Exon 20 Mutations

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • planned enrollment n=268

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Enrollment / operations”

Editor’s analysis

Suspended, no patients enrolled yet. PINNACLE tests poziotinib in HER2 exon 20-mutant non-small cell lung cancer; the FDA earlier rejected the drug's accelerated approval application for insufficient data, and this confirmatory trial must renegotiate its design with the FDA.

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “The study has not enrolled any patients. The study design is under discussion and will likely be redesigned in consultation with the FDA.”
