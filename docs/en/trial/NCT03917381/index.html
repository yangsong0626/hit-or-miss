GEN1046 Safety Trial in Patients With Malignant Solid Tumors

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Dose Escalation: Number of Participants With Dose Limiting T”: Dose Escalation - GEN1046 25 mg 1.00 Participants; Dose Escalation - GEN1046 50 mg 0.00 Participants; Dose Escalation - GEN1046 80 mg 1.00 Participants
  • Design: open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=429
  • Serious adverse events: Dose Escalation - GEN1046 25 mg 50%; Dose Escalation - GEN1046 50 mg 40%; Dose Escalation - GEN1046 80 mg 67%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Genmab has decided to discontinue further clinical development of acasunlimab following strategic portfolio prioritization. The decision was not related to safety concerns.”
