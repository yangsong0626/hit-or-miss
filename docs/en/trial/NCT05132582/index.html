Pfizer · Phase 3 · Oncology

HER2CLIMB-05 NCT05132582

A Study of Tucatinib or Placebo With Trastuzumab and Pertuzumab for Metastatic HER2+ Breast Cancer

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Progression-Free Survival (PFS) as Assessed by Investigator ”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.64 (95% CI 0.51–0.80), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=654
  • Serious adverse events: Tucatinib + Trastuzumab + Pertuzumab 17%; Placebo + Trastuzumab + Pertuzumab 8%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. HER2CLIMB-05, in maintenance after first-line induction for HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer, added tucatinib to trastuzumab + pertuzumab; PFS HR 0.64. Longer progression-free time in maintenance has clear value, but serious adverse events doubled with tucatinib (17% vs 8%); watch diarrhoea and liver function.
