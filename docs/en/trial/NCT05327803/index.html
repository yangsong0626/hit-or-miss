Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Phase 2: Percentage of Participants Achieving Clinical Respo”: Risk Difference (RD) 13.90 (95% CI -6.80–33.80), p=0.1863
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=177
  • Serious adverse events: Epetraborole + OBR 14%; Placebo + OBR 18%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “EBO-301 truncated Phase 3 study (n=97) misses primary endpoint; results unable to confirm clinical efficacy observed in Phase 2 study.”
