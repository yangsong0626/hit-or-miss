THRIVE NCT05176639

A Safety, Tolerability, and Efficacy Study of Veligrotug (VRDN 001) in Participants With Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Proptosis Responder Rate (PRR) in the Study Eye As Measured ”: Risk Difference (RD) 64.74 (95% CI 51.97–77.51), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=113
  • Serious adverse events: Veligrotug 9%; Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
