Opus Genetics · Phase 3 · Ophthalmology

VEGA-3 NCT06542497

Evaluate Efficacy and Safety of POS to Improve Distance-corrected Near Visual in Participants With Presbyopia

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Subjects With ≥ 15 Letters of Improvement in B”: p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=569
  • Serious adverse events: 0.75% Phentolamine Ophthalmic Solution 1%; Phentolamine Ophthalmic Solution Vehicle 1%

Likely reasons

  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. 0.75% phentolamine eye drops for presbyopia significantly increased the share gaining ≥15 letters of near vision over vehicle. It increases depth of field by constricting the pupil, in the presbyopia-drop field (competing with pilocarpine products), with a possible advantage of no ciliary-muscle side effects.
