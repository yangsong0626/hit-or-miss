Alpelisib Plus Olaparib in Platinum-resistant/Refractory, High-grade Serous Ovarian Cancer, With no Germline BRCA Mutation Detected

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Progression Free Survival (PFS) Based on Blinded Independent”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 1.14 (95% CI 0.88–1.48), p=0.84
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=358
  • Serious adverse events: Alpelisib 200mg + Olaparib 200mg (On-Treatment) 54%; Paclitaxel or Pegylated Liposomal Doxorubicin (PLD 32%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 23 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Missed, and the trial stopped early. In platinum-resistant ovarian cancer without germline BRCA mutations, the PI3Kα inhibitor alpelisib + olaparib vs chemotherapy had PFS HR 1.14, a point estimate against the combination, with serious adverse events 21 points higher. The hypothesis that "PI3K inhibition induces HR deficiency and makes PARP inhibitors work" did not translate in an unselected population.
