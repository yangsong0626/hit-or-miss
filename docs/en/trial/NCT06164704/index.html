VIBRANT NCT06164704

A Study to Investigate the Efficacy and Safety of Verekitug (UPB-101) in Participants With Chronic Rhinosinusitis With Nasal Polyps

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Nasal Polyp Score (NPS) at Week 24”: LS Mean Difference -1.77 (95% CI -2.51–-1.03), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=81
  • Serious adverse events: Verekitug (UPB-101) 0%; Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
