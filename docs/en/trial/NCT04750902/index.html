A Clinical Trial to Assess the Safety and the Anti-caries Efficacy of COL 101 (Arginine) Non-fluoride Dentifrices in Comparison With 0.24% Sodium Fluoride (1100 Ppm F) Dentifrice Control in 10 to 14-year-old Children

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Incremental Subject-wise Decayed Missing or Filled Surfaces ”: LS Means Difference 0.49 (95% CI 0.06–0.92), p=0.0267
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=2025
  • Serious adverse events: 0.24% Sodium Fluoride Dentifrice 0%; 1.5% Arginine Dentifrice 0%; 4.0% Arginine Dentifrice 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
