Study of Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) Plus Chemotherapy vs Placebo Plus Chemotherapy… NCT03036488

Study of Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) Plus Chemotherapy vs Placebo Plus Chemotherapy as Neoadjuvant Therapy and Pembrolizumab vs Placebo as Adjuvant Therapy in Participants With Triple Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC) (MK-3475-522/KEYNOTE-522)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Pathological Complete Response (pCR) Rate Using the Definiti”: Difference in Percentage 9.20 (95% CI 2.80–15.60), p=0.00221
  • Primary endpoint “Event-free Survival (EFS) in All Participants”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.68 (95% CI 0.54–0.86), p=.0005943
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1174
  • Serious adverse events: Pembrolizumab + Chemotherapy (Neoadjuvant Phase) 40%; Placebo + Chemotherapy (Neoadjuvant Phase) 26%; Pembrolizumab (Adjuvant Phase) 7%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 39 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
