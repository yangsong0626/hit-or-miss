Clarion-CKD NCT04968184

Efficacy and Safety of KBP-5074 in Uncontrolled Hypertension and Moderate or Severe Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change in Seated Trough Cuff Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP) F”: Difference between adjusted LS means -0.80 (95% CI -3.70–2.00), p==0.572
  • Primary endpoint “Change in Seated Trough Cuff SBP From Week 48 to Week 52”: Difference between Adjusted LS means -0.50 (95% CI -4.40–3.30), p==0.789
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=666
  • Serious adverse events: KBP-5074 7%; Placebo 11%; KBP-5074 - OL 6%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 10 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Interim futility analysis showed primary endpoint not achieved”
