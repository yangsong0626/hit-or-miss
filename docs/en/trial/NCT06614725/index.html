A Study in India on the Immune Response and Safety of a Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) Older Adults (OA) Vaccine When Given to Older Adults 60 Years of Age and Above and Adults 50-59 Years of Age at Increased Risk (AIR) of Respiratory Syncytial Virus Lower Respiratory Tract Disease (RSV-LRTD)

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “RSV-A Neutralizing Titers Expressed as Geometric Mean Titers”: OA-RSV Group 1210 Titers; OA-Placebo Group 1317 Titers; Adults-AIR-RSV Group 1255 Titers

  • actual enrollment n=751
  • Serious adverse events: OA-RSV Group 0%; OA-Placebo Group 0%; Adults-AIR-RSV Group 0%

Likely reasons
