Study of ARO-ANG3 in Participants With Homozygous Familial Hypercholesterolemia (HOFH)

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in Fasting LDL-C at Week 24”: ARO-ANG3 200 mg/EXT ARO-ANG3 200 mg -36.79 percentage c; ARO-ANG3 300 mg / EXT ARO-ANG3 300 mg -40.10 percentage c

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Business decision, unrelated to safety concerns.”
