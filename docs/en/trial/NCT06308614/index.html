Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline to Week 12 in Feeling Concerned by Diff”: Least square mean difference -0.33 (95% CI -0.92–0.26), p=0.2684

  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=82
  • Serious adverse events: Estetrol 0%; Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Sample size only 82, limited power to detect a moderate effect
