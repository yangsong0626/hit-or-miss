CodeBreak300 NCT05198934

Sotorasib and Panitumumab Versus Investigator's Choice for Participants With Kirsten Rat Sarcoma (KRAS) p.G12C Mutation

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Progression-free Survival (PFS) Per Response Evaluation Crit”: Trifluridine and Tipiracil or Regorafenib 2.04 months; Sotorasib 240 mg + Panitumumab 3.91 months; Sotorasib 960 mg + Panitumumab 5.62 months
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=160
  • Serious adverse events: Trifluridine and Tipiracil or Regorafenib 29%; Sotorasib 240 mg + Panitumumab 32%; Sotorasib 960 mg + Panitumumab 26%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen
