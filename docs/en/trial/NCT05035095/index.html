Novo Nordisk · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

OASIS 1 NCT05035095

Research Study to Investigate How Well Semaglutide Tablets Taken Once Daily Work in People Who Are Overweight or Living With Obesity (OASIS 1)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage Change in Body Weight”: Treatment difference -12.74 (95% CI -14.15–-11.33), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Achieved Weight Loss Greater Than”: Odds Ratio (OR) 12.62 (95% CI 8.50–18.74), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=667
  • Serious adverse events: Oral Semaglutide 50 mg 10%; Placebo 9%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. In OASIS 1, 50 mg oral semaglutide produced about 13% more weight loss than placebo in obesity, close to the 2.4 mg injection. High oral doses need more active ingredient, the main limit on commercialisation.
