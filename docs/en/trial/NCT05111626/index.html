Bemarituzumab Plus Chemotherapy and Nivolumab Versus Chemotherapy and Nivolumab for FGFR2b Overexpressed Untreated Advanced Gastric and Gastroesophageal Junction Cancer.

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=515

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Sponsor decision / strategy”

Editor’s analysis

The registry says only "sponsor terminated". FORTITUDE-102 added bemarituzumab to nivolumab + chemotherapy in FGFR2b-overexpressing gastric cancer. The survival benefit in the sister trial FORTITUDE-101 faded with longer follow-up, which may have shaped the overall view of the programme, an inference.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Study was terminated by sponsor”
