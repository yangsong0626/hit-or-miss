DFU-002 NCT02264288

Efficacy and Safety of Intramuscular PDA-002 in Subjects With Diabetic Foot Ulcer With and Without PAD.

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Subjects With Complete Wound Closure of the In”: PDA-002 3 × 10^6 Cells 15.00 Participants; PDA-002 10 × 10^6 Cells 10.00 Participants; PDA-002 30 × 10^6 Cells 6.00 Participants

  • Serious adverse events: PDA-002 3 x 10^6 Cells 49%; PDA-002 10 x 10^6 Cells 41%; PDA-002 30 x 10^6 Cells 52%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “The study was terminated early due to a business decision.”
