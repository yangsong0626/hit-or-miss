AFFINITY NCT05071664

A Study of Guselkumab and Golimumab Combination Therapy in Participants With Active Psoriatic Arthritis

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Minimal Disease Acti”: Odds Ratio (OR) 1.40 (90% CI 0.60–3.30), p==0.557
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=91
  • Serious adverse events: Group 1: Guselkumab 100 mg q4w Plus Golimumab 50 m 7%; Group 2: Guselkumab 100 mg q4w Plus Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo
  • Sample size only 91, limited power to detect a moderate effect
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 7 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
