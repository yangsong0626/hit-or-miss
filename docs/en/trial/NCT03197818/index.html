TRIVERSYTI NCT03197818

Active Controlled Trial of CHF5993 Pressurized Metered-dose Inhaler ( pMDI) vs Symbicort®Turbuhaler® in Patients With Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease ( COPD) (TRIVERSYTI)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Pre-dose Forced Expiratory Volume Wi”: adjusted mean difference 0.06 (95% CI 0.04–0.09), p=< 0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in 2-hour Post-dose FEV1 at Week 24”: adjusted mean difference 0.11 (95% CI 0.09–0.14), p=< 0.001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=708
  • Serious adverse events: CHF 5993 100/6/12.5 µg - Safety Population 11%; Symbicort Turbuhaler 160/4.5 µg - Safety Populatio 17%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 6 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. In TRIVERSYTI, Trimbow triple vs Symbicort dual improved FEV1 by about 60–110 mL, with fewer serious adverse events. Mainly adds triple-inhaler evidence in Asian populations.
