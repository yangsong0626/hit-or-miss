A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Astegolimab in Participants With Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Annualized Rate of Moderate and Severe COPD Exacerbations Ov”: Adjusted Rate Ratio 0.84 (95% CI 0.71–1.00), p=0.0468
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1334
  • Serious adverse events: Astegolimab Q2W 25%; Astegolimab Q4W 29%; Placebo 31%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 6 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
