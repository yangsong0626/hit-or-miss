A Clinical Trial That Will Study the Efficacy and Safety of an Investigational… NCT04092686

A Clinical Trial That Will Study the Efficacy and Safety of an Investigational Drug in Acutely Psychotic People With Schizophrenia

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in PANSS Total Score at Week 6”: LS Mean Difference -3.80 (95% CI -7.50–-0.10), p=0.041
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=464
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 4%; SEP-363856 75mg 9%; SEP-363856 100mg 7%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 5 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. This ulotaront phase 3 met its primary endpoint with a PANSS difference of -3.8, but the upper confidence limit of -0.1 is almost at 0. Alongside another completely negative phase 3 (PANSS difference -0.3), TAAR1 agonists' antipsychotic efficacy looks weak and unstable, and the programme's prospects are poor.
