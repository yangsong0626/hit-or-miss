Bayer · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

OCEANIC-STROKE NCT05686070

A Study to Test Asundexian for Preventing a Stroke Caused by a Clot in Participants After an Acute Ischemic Stroke or After a High-risk Transient Ischemic Attack, a So-called Mini Stroke

Partly met

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Had an Ischemic Stroke”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.74 (95% CI 0.65–0.84), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Had ISTH Major Bleeding”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 1.10 (95% CI 0.85–1.44), p=0.4587
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=12327
  • Serious adverse events: Asundexian 19%; Placebo 20%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
