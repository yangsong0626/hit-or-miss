A Study of Vosoritide in Children With Noonan Syndrome With Inadequate Growth During or After Human Growth Hormone Treatment

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=18

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “BioMarin has decided to discontinue the study after assessing a number of factors, including study feasibility and the current treatment landscape for children with Noonan syndrome. This decision is not related to the safety or efficacy of vosoritide”
