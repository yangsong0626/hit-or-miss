Perioperative Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) Plus Cystectomy or Perioperative Pembrolizumab Plus Enfortumab Vedotin Plus Cystectomy Versus Cystectomy Alone in Participants Who Are Cisplatin-ineligible or Decline Cisplatin With Muscle-invasive Bladder Cancer (MK-3475-905/KEYNOTE-905/EV-303)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Event-Free Survival (EFS) Between Arm C: Enfortumab Vedotin ”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.40 (95% CI 0.28–0.57), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=595
  • Serious adverse events: Arm A: Pembrolizumab + Surgery 66%; Arm B: Surgery Alone 39%; Arm C: EV + Pembrolizumab + Surgery 58%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 27 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. KEYNOTE-905/EV-303 in cisplatin-ineligible muscle-invasive bladder cancer: perioperative enfortumab vedotin + pembrolizumab vs surgery alone, EFS HR 0.40, a very large benefit. ADC + PD-1 is already standard in advanced disease; this moves the combination to the operable setting.
