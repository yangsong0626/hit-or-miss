Sanofi · Phase 3 · Immunology / inflammation

AERIFY-2 NCT04751487

Study to Assess the Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of SAR440340/REGN3500/Itepekimab in Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Former Smoker Cohort: Annualized Rate of Adjudicated Moderat”: Rate ratio 0.88 (95% CI 0.71–1.08), p== 0.2176
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1234
  • Serious adverse events: Former Smoker Cohort: Placebo 22%; Former Smoker Cohort: Itepekimab 300 mg Q4W 25%; Former Smoker Cohort: Itepekimab 300 mg Q2W 23%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 5 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Missed. The IL-33 antibody itepekimab in former smokers with COPD had a moderate-to-severe exacerbation rate ratio of 0.88 with the confidence interval crossing 1. The concurrent phase 3 AERIFY-1 was positive; the inconsistency suggests the effect is small or highly sensitive to population (former vs current smokers, eosinophil levels). The overall view of the IL-33/ST2 pathway in COPD moves from "established" to "needs patient selection".
