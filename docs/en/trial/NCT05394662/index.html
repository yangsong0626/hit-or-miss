Safety and Effectiveness of Juveena™ Hydrogel System Following Transcervical Gynecologic Procedures (TCGP) At High-Risk for Intrauterine Adhesions

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Freedom From Intrauterine Adhesions (IUA)”: Risk Difference (RD) 17.30 (95% CI 0.53–34.14), p=0.017
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=150
  • Serious adverse events: Transcervical Gynecological Procedure + Juveena Hy 3%; Transcervical Gynecological Procedure Alone (Stand 4%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
