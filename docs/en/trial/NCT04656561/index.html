ARCHER NCT04656561

A Study Investigating the Efficacy and Safety of Intravitreal Injections of ANX007 in Patients With Geographic Atrophy

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Rate of Change (Slope) From Baseline to Month 12 in GA Lesio”: Slope 0.18, p=0.5503
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=270
  • Serious adverse events: ANX007 EM 24%; ANX007 EOM 17%; Sham 18%

Likely reasons

  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 6 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
