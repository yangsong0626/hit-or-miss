Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of AVP-786 for the Treatment of Negative Symptoms of Schizophrenia

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline (Week 3) to Week 15 in the Positive and”: Least Squares (LS) Mean Difference 0.20 (95% CI -1.09–1.47), p=0.768
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=136
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo (Run-in Period) 0%; AVP-786 3%; Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. AVP-786 (deuterated dextromethorphan + quinidine) for negative symptoms of schizophrenia; after an interim analysis the data monitoring committee recommended stopping for futility. Negative symptoms are the hardest part of schizophrenia to treat and almost all trials have failed.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Based on the Interim Analysis outcome and recommendation by the DMC, Otsuka approved termination of the study based on futility.”
