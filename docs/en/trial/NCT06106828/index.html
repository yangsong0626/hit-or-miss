SatraGO-2 NCT06106828

A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety, Pharmacokinetics, and Pharmacodynamics of Satralizumab in Participants With Thyroid Eye Disease

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants in the Active TED Population Who ”: Difference in Response Rates at Week 24 28.90 (95.03% CI 11.50–46.20), p=0.0011
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=127
  • Serious adverse events: Part I: Satralizumab 3%; Part I: Placebo 5%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
