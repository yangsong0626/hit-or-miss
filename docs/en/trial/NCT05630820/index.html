Study of Bepirovirsen in Nucleos(t)Ide Analogue-treated Participants With Chronic Hepatitis B (B-Well 2)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Achieving Functional Cure (FC) After ”: Risk Difference (RD) 13.30 (95% CI 10.40–16.10), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=857
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 5%; Bepirovirsen 8%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
