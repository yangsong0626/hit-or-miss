VAY RE-HIT NCT07039422

Study of Ianalumab in Adults With Primary Immune Thrombocytopenia (ITP) and Warm-antibody Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia (wAIHA) Who Have Previously Benefited From Ianalumab

Stopped

  • Design: non-randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”
  • The trial was withdrawn before the first patient was enrolled

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The study was discontinued due to evolving scientific \& feasibility considerations that could adversely affect recruitment, thereby reducing the likelihood of generating a sufficiently robust dataset to support reliable efficacy \& safety conclusions.”
