PSMAddition NCT04720157

An International Prospective Open-label, Randomized, Phase III Study Comparing 177Lu-PSMA-617 in Combination With Standard of Care (SoC), Versus SoC Alone, in Adult Male Patients With Metastatic Hormone Sensitive Prostate Cancer (mHSPC)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Radiographic Progression Free Survival (rPFS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.72 (95% CI 0.58–0.90), p== 0.002
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1144
  • Serious adverse events: On-treatment Period 177Lu-PSMA-617 + SoC 32%; On-treatment Period SoC Alone 29%; On-treatment Period Crossover 177Lu-PSMA-617 +/- S 30%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 32 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
