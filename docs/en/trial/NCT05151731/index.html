A Study to Investigate Vamikibart (RO7200220) in Diabetic Macular Edema

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Best-Corrected Visual Acuity (BCVA) ”: Difference in Adjusted Means -8.40 (90% CI -11.30–-5.40), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=394
  • Serious adverse events: Arm A: Vamikibart 0.25 mg Q8W 18%; Arm B: Vamikibart 1 mg Q8W 25%; Arm C: Vamikibart 1 mg Q4W 21%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 7 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
