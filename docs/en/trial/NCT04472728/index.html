COVA NCT04472728

Efficacy and Safety of BIO101 in the Prevention of Respiratory Deterioration in Hospitalized COVID-19 Patients

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Experiencing Negative Events”: Difference (%) -11.40 (95% CI -22.40–-0.40), p==0.0426
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=238
  • Serious adverse events: 350mg BIO101 25%; Placebo 33%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 8 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Enrollment difficulty. The trial of BIO101 in severe COVID-19 was stopped because falling case numbers left no eligible patients, unrelated to efficacy or safety.

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “Lack of eligible patients due to lower number of Covid-19 cases in all involved sites.”
