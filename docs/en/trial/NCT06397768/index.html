CORAL NCT06397768

Co-administration Study of an Investigational Live-Attenuated Respiratory Syncytial Virus Vaccine in Infants and Toddlers

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=2226

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Sponsor decision / strategy”

Editor’s analysis

The registry says only "sponsor decision". CORAL tested co-administering Sanofi's toddler RSV vaccine with routine childhood vaccines. RSV prevention in infants already has nirsevimab and maternal vaccines, so market size and development priority for a toddler vaccine may be the key consideration.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Sponsor decision”
