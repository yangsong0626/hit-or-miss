Novo Nordisk · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

STEP HFpEF DM NCT04916470

Research Study to Look at How Well Semaglutide Works in People Living With Heart Failure, Obesity and Type 2 Diabetes

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change in KCCQ (Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire) Cl”: Treatment difference 7.30 (95% CI 4.10–10.40), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Percentage Change in Body Weight”: Treatment difference -6.40 (95% CI -7.60–-5.20), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=617
  • Serious adverse events: Semaglutide 2.4 mg 18%; Placebo 29%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 11 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
