OPTIMA NCT05394116

A Study to Assess Safety, Tolerability and Efficacy of Garetosmab Versus Placebo Administered Intravenously (IV) in Adult Participants With Fibrodysplasia Ossificans Progressiva (FOP)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Number of New Heterotopic Ossification (HO) Lesions”: Exponential of estimates from model 0.10 (95% CI 0.01–0.76), p=0.0260
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=63
  • Serious adverse events: Low Dose Garetosmab 3 mg 5%; High Dose Garetosmab 10 mg 9%; Placebo 10%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
