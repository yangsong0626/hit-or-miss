A Safety, Tolerability and Efficacy Study of NC410 Plus Pembrolizumab in Participants With Advanced Unresectable or Metastatic Solid Tumors

Descriptive

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants With Treatment-emergent Adverse Event”: NC410 30mg and Pembrolizumab 3.00 Participants; 60mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 11.00 Participants; 100mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 62.00 Participants
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=97
  • Serious adverse events: NC410 30mg and Pembrolizumab 33%; 60mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 64%; 100mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 38%

Likely reasons

  • Single-arm or uncontrolled design; the registry has only descriptive data, so success cannot be judged statistically and needs comparison with historical controls or similar drugs

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Following completion of Phase 1b, the Sponsor decided not to initiate Part 2 of the trial.”
