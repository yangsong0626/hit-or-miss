STOP-HS2 NCT05620836

A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Povorcitinib (INCB054707) in Participants With Moderate to Severe Hidradenitis Suppurativa (HS)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Hidradenitis Suppura”: Odds Ratio (OR) 1.90 (95% CI 1.20–2.80), p=0.0033
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=619
  • Serious adverse events: PC Period: Povorcitinib 45 mg QD 2%; PC Period: Povorcitinib 75 mg QD 2%; PC Period: Placebo 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
