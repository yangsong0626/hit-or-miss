Phase I/II Study of Autologous T Cells to Express T-Cell Receptors (TCRs) in Subjects With Solid Tumors

Stopped

  • Design: non-randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=8
  • Serious adverse events: Assigned Dose Level 1 (Target 5 × 10⁹ TCR+ Cells; 100%; Assigned Dose Level 2 (Target 40 × 10⁹ TCR+ Cells; 29%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Enrollment / operations”
  • Only 8 patients enrolled at termination, too few to judge the drug itself

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “Sponsor halted enrollment on 14 Aug 2023 for business reasons after a Board review of funding needs and permanently discontinued TCR-T cell manufacturing. The study was not stopped for safety reasons.”
