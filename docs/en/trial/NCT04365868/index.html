Galectin · Phase 2/3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

NAVIGATE NCT04365868

Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Belapectin for the Prevention of Esophageal Varices in NASH Cirrhosis

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Proportion of Patients in the Belapectin Treatment Groups Wh”: Placebo 56.00 Participants; Belapectin 2 mg/kg Lean Body Mass (LBM) 45.00 Participants; Belapectin 4 mg/kg Lean Body Mass (LBM) 51.00 Participants
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=357
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 20%; Belapectin 2 mg/kg Lean Body Mass (LBM) 24%; Belapectin 4 mg/kg Lean Body Mass (LBM) 22%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

Design changed after regulatory discussion. NAVIGATE tested the galectin-3 inhibitor belapectin for preventing oesophageal varices in NASH cirrhosis, and analysed stage 1 as a separate trial on FDA advice. That stage missed its primary endpoint in the full population; the programme's prospects depend on whether subgroup results can persuade regulators.

Why it stopped

Regulatory · “Based on FDA feedback, Stage 1 of NAVIGATE was analyzed as a stand-alone trial. Study was not prematurely terminated for safety reasons.”
