AstraZeneca · Phase 3 · Oncology

NeoADAURA NCT04351555

A Study of Osimertinib With or Without Chemotherapy Versus Chemotherapy Alone as Neoadjuvant Therapy for Patients With EGFRm Positive Resectable Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Major Pathological Response (MPR) - IASLC Method”: Odds Ratio (OR) 19.82 (95% CI 4.60–85.32), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Major Pathological Response (MPR) - Chemotherapy Method”: Odds Ratio (OR) 19.17 (95% CI 4.45–82.66), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=358
  • Serious adverse events: Osimertinib + Chemo 20%; Osimertinib 11%; Placebo + Chemo 20%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. NeoADAURA in resectable EGFR-mutant non-small cell lung cancer: neoadjuvant osimertinib with or without chemotherapy, odds ratio for major pathological response about 19. Pathological response is a surrogate; whether it translates into event-free and overall survival awaits later data.
