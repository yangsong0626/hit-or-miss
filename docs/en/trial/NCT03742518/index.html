A Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of SM04554 Topical Solution in Male Subjects With Androgenetic Alopecia

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Absolute Non-vellus Hair Count in Target Area”: Rate ratio 1.02 (95% CI 0.95–1.10), p=0.540
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=675
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 1%; 0.15% SM04554 0%; 0.25% SM04554 1%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high

Editor’s analysis

Missed. The topical Wnt pathway modulator SM04554 for androgenetic alopecia had a hair-count ratio of 1.02, no different from vehicle. A point estimate of almost 1 means the drug is ineffective at this formulation and dose, not a power problem.
