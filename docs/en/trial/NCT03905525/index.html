Novartis · Phase 2 · Immunology / inflammation

TWINSS NCT03905525

Study of Safety and Efficacy of Multiple Doses of CFZ533 in Two Distinct Populations of Patients With Sjogren's Syndrome

Partly met

  • Primary endpoint “Cohort 1: Change in EULAR Sjögren Syndrome Disease Activity ”: LS Mean difference CFZ533-Placebo -3.00 (95% CI -4.90–-1.10), p=0.0025
  • Primary endpoint “Cohort 2: Change in EULAR Sjögren Syndrome Patient Reported ”: LS Mean difference CFZ533-Placebo -0.57 (95% CI -1.30–0.15), p=0.1210
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=273
  • Serious adverse events: Cohort 1 / Arm D (Period 1): Placebo 2%; Cohort 1 / Arm D1 (Period 2): CFZ533 600mg (From W 10%; Cohort 1/Arm C: CFZ533 150 mg 14%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 12 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
