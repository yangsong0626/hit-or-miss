Hit or Miss / Trials / FIGHT-302

Incyte · Phase 3 · Oncology

FIGHT-302 NCT03656536

A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Pemigatinib Versus Chemotherapy in Unresectable or Metastatic Cholangiocarcinoma

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Randomized Treatment Period: Progression-free Survival (PFS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.58 (95% CI 0.39–0.87), p=0.0078
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=167
  • Serious adverse events: Randomized Period: Pemigatinib 13.5 mg QD 28%; Randomized Period: Gemcitabine 1000 mg/m^2 Plus Ci 23%; Transition Period: Pemigatinib 13.5 mg QD 21%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 6 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Enrollment difficulty driven by a changed standard of care. FIGHT-302 compared pemigatinib with gemcitabine + cisplatin in first-line FGFR2-fusion cholangiocarcinoma, but the first-line standard became durvalumab + chemotherapy, so the outdated control arm made enrollment impossible. This is a common dilemma for first-line confirmatory trials of targeted drugs.

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “The study was terminated due to lack of enrollment resulting from a change in the standard of care for the first-line treatment of patients with cholangiocarcinoma. There were no safety concerns that contributed to this decision.”
