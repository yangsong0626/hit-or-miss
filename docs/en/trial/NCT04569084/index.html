Efficacy and Safety Study of Oral Edaravone Administered in Subjects With ALS

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=384
  • Serious adverse events: Edaravone 105 mg (Once Daily) 27%; Edaravone 105mg (2 Weeks On/Off) 29%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. Oral edaravone in ALS tested whether on-demand dosing beat standard intermittent dosing; a futility analysis triggered termination. Note this does not negate oral edaravone itself (approved on the standard regimen); more frequent dosing simply added no benefit.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Futility was met.”
