Amgen · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

VESALIUS-CV NCT03872401

Effect of Evolocumab in Patients at High Cardiovascular Risk Without Prior Myocardial Infarction or Stroke

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Experienced Coronary Heart Diseas”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.75 (95% CI 0.65–0.86), p=< 0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Experienced CHD Death, MI, Ischem”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.81 (95% CI 0.73–0.89), p=< 0.001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=12301
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo Q2W 29%; Evolocumab 140 mg Q2W 28%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. VESALIUS-CV is the primary-prevention outcomes trial of the PCSK9 antibody evolocumab in high-risk people without prior heart attack or stroke; primary endpoint HRs 0.75 and 0.81 in 12,000 patients, with confidence intervals far from 1. The first time a PCSK9 inhibitor has shown fewer cardiovascular events in primary prevention, with safety similar to placebo; it will substantially widen the eligible population.
