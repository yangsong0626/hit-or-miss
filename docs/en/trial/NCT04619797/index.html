Hit or Miss / Trials / SKYSCRAPER-06

Roche · Phase 2/3 · Oncology

SKYSCRAPER-06 NCT04619797

A Study of Tiragolumab in Combination With Atezolizumab Plus Pemetrexed and Carboplatin/Cisplatin Versus Pembrolizumab Plus Pemetrexed and Carboplatin/Cisplatin in Participants With Previously Untreated Advanced Non-Squamous Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

Questionable

  • Primary endpoint “Progression-free Survival (PFS) as Determined by the Investi”: p=0.0176
  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival (OS)”: p=0.0325
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=542
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo + Pembrolizumab + Chemotherapy 52%; Tiragolumab + Atezolizumab + Chemotherapy 55%

Likely reasons

  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met (per registered p values). SKYSCRAPER-06 compared tiragolumab + atezolizumab + chemotherapy with pembrolizumab + chemotherapy in first-line non-squamous non-small cell lung cancer. Registered PFS and OS p values are below 0.05 but no effect direction is registered; public reports say the trial failed to show benefit and stopped, so the p values may reflect an unfavourable direction. Check the original announcement for this one.
