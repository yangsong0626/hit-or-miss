Bayer · Phase 3 · Other

Quanti CNS NCT05915702

A Study to Compare How Well Gadoquatrane Works and Its Safety With an Already Available Contrast Agent for MRI in People With Known or Suspected Brain or Spinal Cord-related Problems

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Visualization Parameter Contrast Enhancement Assessed by Sep”: Difference in mean average score 1.11 (95% CI 1.00–1.23), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Visualization Parameter Delineation Assessed by Separate Bli”: Difference in mean average score 0.99 (95% CI 0.87–1.10), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=321
  • Serious adverse events: Gadoquatrane 1%; Approved Macrocyclic GBCAs 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust

Editor’s analysis

Met. Gadoquatrane also beat an approved macrocyclic contrast agent on enhancement and border-definition scores in CNS MRI, consistent with the body trial.
