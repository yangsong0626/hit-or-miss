TRuE-AD4 NCT06238817

A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, and Safety Study of Ruxolitinib Cream in Adults With Moderate Atopic Dermatitis

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Achieving a ≥75% Improvement in t”: Odds Ratio (OR) 11.26 (95% CI 5.69–22.26), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants With Investigator's Global Assess”: Odds Ratio (OR) 10.14 (95% CI 4.90–20.98), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=241
  • Serious adverse events: Vehicle Cream 1%; Ruxolitinib 1.5% BID 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
