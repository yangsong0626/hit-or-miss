Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. Long-term study of Lilly's mGlu2/3 receptor agonist pomaglumetad (LY2140023). The drug failed to beat placebo in several pivotal schizophrenia trials and the programme ended around 2012, a representative failure of glutamate-hypothesis drugs.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The decision to stop the trial was based on efficacy results in the overall schizophrenia participant population.”
