Hit or Miss / Trials / SKYSCRAPER-03

Roche · Phase 3 · Oncology

SKYSCRAPER-03 NCT04513925

A Study of Atezolizumab and Tiragolumab Compared With Durvalumab in Participants With Locally Advanced, Unresectable Stage III Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Progression-free Survival (PFS), as Assessed by an Independe”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.96 (95% CI 0.75–1.23), p=0.7586
  • Primary endpoint “PFS, as Assessed by an IRF in FAS”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 1.00 (95% CI 0.84–1.19), p=0.9948
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=829
  • Serious adverse events: Durvalumab 32%; Atezolizumab + Tiragolumab 33%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo

Editor’s analysis

Missed. SKYSCRAPER-03 compared the TIGIT antibody tiragolumab + atezolizumab with durvalumab consolidation in unresectable stage III non-small cell lung cancer after chemoradiation; PFS HR 0.96 and 1.00, no difference at all. Another negative phase 3 for tiragolumab, further questioning TIGIT as a checkpoint combination target.
