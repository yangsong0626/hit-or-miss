A Study of JNJ-77242113 for the Treatment of Participants With Moderate to Severe Plaque Psoriasis (ICONIC-ADVANCE 2)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved an Investigator's Gl”: Treatment difference 62.60 (95% CI 53.30–69.50), p=<0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Psoriasis Area and S”: Treatment difference 56.50 (95% CI 49.00–62.40), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=731
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo Controlled Period (Week 0-16): Placebo 0%; Placebo Controlled Period (Week 0-16): JNJ-7724211 3%; Placebo Controlled Period (Week 0-16): Deucravacit 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. ICONIC-ADVANCE 2 matched ADVANCE 1: icotrokinra's response rates in moderate-to-severe psoriasis were about 55–60 points above placebo. Two actively controlled phase 3 trials positive together give consistent evidence for filing.
