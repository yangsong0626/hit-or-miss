A Clinical Trial to Study the Efficacy and Safety of an Investigational Drug in… NCT04072354

A Clinical Trial to Study the Efficacy and Safety of an Investigational Drug in Acutely Psychotic People With Schizophrenia

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in PANSS Total Score at Week 6”: LS Mean Difference -0.30 (95% CI -4.60–4.00), p=0.842
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=463
  • Serious adverse events: Adults: Placebo 3%; Adults: SEP-363856 50mg 8%; Adults: SEP-363856 75mg 8%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 8 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Missed. The TAAR1 agonist ulotaront (SEP-363856) had a PANSS difference of only -0.3 in acute schizophrenia, no different from placebo. Both concurrent DIAMOND phase 3 trials failed, mainly because of unusually high placebo response and possibly weaker antipsychotic efficacy of TAAR1 than phase 2 suggested.
