NIMBLE NCT04718389

A Study of GSK3511294 (Depemokimab) Compared With Mepolizumab or Benralizumab in Participants With Severe Asthma With an Eosinophilic Phenotype

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Annualized Rate of Clinically Significant Exacerbations Over”: Rate Ratio 1.16 (95% CI 0.98–1.38), p=0.079
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1717
  • Serious adverse events: Depemokimab 100mg SC 9%; Mepolizumab 100 mg / Benralizumab 30 mg SC 9%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo

Editor’s analysis

Missed on the test registered in the registry. NIMBLE tested switching from mepolizumab or benralizumab to twice-yearly depemokimab; the exacerbation rate ratio was 1.16 (0.98–1.38), with the point estimate against the switch arm. The comparator was an already-working long-acting biologic of the same class, a high bar; the prespecified criterion (including any non-inferiority margin) should be taken from the sponsor's announcement. Depemokimab's selling point remains dosing frequency, better suited to patients starting treatment.
