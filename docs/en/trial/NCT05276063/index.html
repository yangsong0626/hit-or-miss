Sling · Phase 2/3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

LIDS NCT05276063

A Phase 2b, Study of Linsitinib in Subjects With Active, Moderate to Severe Thyroid Eye Disease (TED)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Subjects Who Are Proptosis Responders at Week ”: Risk Difference (RD) 0.34 (95% CI 0.10–0.59), p=0.01
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=90
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 3%; 75 mg BID 0%; 150 mg BID 7%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 7 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
