ROCKET-Ignite NCT05398445

A Study Evaluating Rocatinlimab in Moderate-to-severe Atopic Dermatitis (ROCKET-IGNITE)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Achieved Validated Investigator's”: Common risk difference percentage 14.40 (95% CI 8.40–20.00), p=< 0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Achieved ≥ 75% Reduction From Bas”: Common risk difference percentage 23.40 (95% CI 15.40–30.90), p=< 0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=769
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 6%; Rocatinlimab 150 mg Q4W 3%; Rocatinlimab 300 mg Q4W 5%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. In ROCKET-IGNITE, the OX40 antibody rocatinlimab significantly raised vIGA and EASI-75 responses over placebo in moderate-to-severe atopic dermatitis. But placebo-adjusted gains of about 14% and 23% are clearly below historical data for dupilumab and JAK inhibitors, its main competitive weakness after launch.
