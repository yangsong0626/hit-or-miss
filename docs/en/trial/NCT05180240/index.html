Cardiol · Phase 2 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

ARCHER NCT05180240

Impact of CardiolRx on Myocardial Recovery in Patients With Acute Myocarditis

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Extracellular Volume (ECV)”: Mean Difference (Final Values) -0.87 (95% CI -2.40–0.66), p=0.2609
  • Primary endpoint “Global Longitudinal Strain (GLS)”: Mean Difference (Final Values) -0.07 (95% CI -1.21–1.07), p=0.9021
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=109
  • Serious adverse events: Cannabidiol, Pharmaceutically Produced With < 5 Pp 11%; Placebo 6%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 5 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
