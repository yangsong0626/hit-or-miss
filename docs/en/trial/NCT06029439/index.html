Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. Navacaprant (a κ-opioid receptor antagonist) missed its primary endpoint in all three randomised trials in major depression, and the programme was stopped. J&J's aticaprant, with the same mechanism, stopped at the same time; κ-receptor antagonism as a new antidepressant mechanism took a major blow.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “NMRA-335140 did not achieve the primary endpoint across three randomized controlled trials. Program discontinued due to lack of efficacy in MDD.”
