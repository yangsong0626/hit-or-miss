Study on Foster Efficacy Maintenance and Reliever vs Foster Maintenance + Salbutamol Reliever in Asthmatics (MART2)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Cumulative Number of Patients With Severe Asthma Exacerbatio”: p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1714
  • Serious adverse events: Treatment A 4%; Treatment B 5%

Likely reasons

  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful

Editor’s analysis

Met. Budesonide/formoterol in one inhaler for both maintenance and relief (MART) vs maintenance plus as-needed salbutamol significantly reduced severe exacerbations. One of the classic pieces of evidence for MART: patients get extra inhaled steroid when symptoms worsen.
