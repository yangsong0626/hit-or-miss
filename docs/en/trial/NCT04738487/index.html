Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Sponsor decision / strategy”

Editor’s analysis

Ostensibly "business reasons", but the background is efficacy. KEYVIBE-003 compared MK-7684A with pembrolizumab in PD-L1-positive first-line non-small cell lung cancer, and the programme stopped after a futility analysis. Together with Roche's tiragolumab and BeiGene's ociperlimab, TIGIT plus PD-1 in lung cancer is essentially disproven.

Why it stopped
