LIBerate-FH NCT04797104

Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of LIB003 in HeFH Patients on Oral Lipid Therapy Needing Further LDL-C Reduction

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline Compared to Placebo in LDL-C Level Calc”: Least squares mean difference -60.53 (95% CI -66.72–-54.34), p=<.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline Compared to Placebo in LDL-C Level Calc”: Least squares mean difference -68.27 (95% CI -73.66–-62.88), p=<.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=478
  • Serious adverse events: LIB003 (Lerodalcibep) 3%; Placebo 6%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. In LIBerate-FH, the third-generation PCSK9 inhibitor lerodalcibep (monthly) lowered LDL-C about 60–68% (placebo-adjusted) in heterozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia, comparable to existing PCSK9 antibodies, with advantages in dosing interval and small injection volume.
