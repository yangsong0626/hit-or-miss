TRuST NCT02875548

A Study to Assess Long-term Safety of Tazemetostat in Adult Participants of All Ages With Any Disease Treated With Tazemetostat in a Previous Clinical Study

Descriptive

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants With Treatment-Emergent Adverse Event”: Tazemetostat <=600 mg BD 13.00 Participants; Tazemetostat 800 mg BD 39.00 Participants; Tazemetostat 1200 mg BD 3.00 Participants
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=58
  • Serious adverse events: Tazemetostat <=600 mg BD 31%; Tazemetostat 800 mg BD 51%; Tazemetostat 1200 mg BD 67%

Likely reasons

  • Single-arm or uncontrolled design; the registry has only descriptive data, so success cannot be judged statistically and needs comparison with historical controls or similar drugs

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “Sponsor decision; no additional participant enrollment was anticipated.”
