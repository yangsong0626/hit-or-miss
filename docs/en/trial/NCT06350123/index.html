MIRO-CKD NCT06350123

Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability of Balcinrenone/Dapagliflozin Compared to Dapagliflozin in Adults With Chronic Kidney Disease

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Relative Change in Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (UACR) ”: Percent change difference -32.77 (90% CI -41.96–-22.14), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=324
  • Serious adverse events: Balcinrenone/Dapagliflozin 15 mg/10 mg 1%; Balcinrenone/Dapagliflozin 40 mg/10 mg 7%; Dapagliflozin 10 mg 8%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 7 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
