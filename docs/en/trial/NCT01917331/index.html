TRILOGY NCT01917331

Efficacy of Fixed Combination of Beclometasone + Formoterol + Glycopyrrolate Versus Foster® in COPD

Met

  • Primary endpoint “1_Change From Baseline in Pre-dose Morning Forced Expiratory”: Adjusted mean difference 0.08 (95% CI 0.05–0.11), p=< 0.001
  • Primary endpoint “2_Change From Baseline in 2-hour Post-dose FEV1 -- at Week 2”: Adjusted mean difference 0.12 (95% CI 0.09–0.15), p=< 0.001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1368
  • Serious adverse events: Beclometasone/Formoterol/Glycopyrrolate 15%; Beclometasone/Formoterol 18%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful

Editor’s analysis

Met. TRILOGY showed beclometasone/formoterol/glycopyrronium triple inhalation further improved FEV1 over dual ICS/LABA in COPD, by about 80–120 mL. One of the key registration trials for the single-inhaler triple (Trimbow); the added LAMA component performed as expected.
