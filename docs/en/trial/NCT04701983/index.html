AERIFY-1 NCT04701983

Study to Assess the Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of SAR440340/REGN3500/Itepekimab in Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Annualized Rate of Adjudicated Moderate or Severe Acute Exac”: Rate ratio 0.73 (95% CI 0.60–0.89), p== 0.0019
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1127
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo Q2W 22%; Itepekimab 300 mg Q4W 19%; Itepekimab 300 mg Q2W 18%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
