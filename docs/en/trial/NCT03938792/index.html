Study of the Efficacy and Safety PF-06741086 in Adult and Teenage Participants With Severe Hemophilia A or Moderately Severe to Severe Hemophilia B

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Model-Based Annualized Bleeding Rate (ABR) of Treated Bleedi”: Ratio estimate 0.07 (95% CI 0.04–0.12), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Model-Based ABR of Treated Bleeding Events: Non-Inhibitor Co”: Ratio estimate 0.08 (95% CI 0.06–0.11), p=<0.0001
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=188
  • Serious adverse events: Inhibitor Cohort: RP at OP/ PF-06741086 in ATP [RP 0%; Inhibitor Cohort: OD at OP/ PF-06741086 in ATP [OD 2%; Inhibitor Cohort: OD at OP 9%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Single-arm trial; the p value compares against baseline or a preset threshold, not a control arm, so the evidence is weaker than a randomised trial
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
