SCALE KIDS: Research Study to Look at How Well a New Medicine is at Lowering Weight in Children With Obesity

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Relative Change in Body Mass Index (BMI)”: Treatment difference -8.26 (95% CI -12.55–-3.96), p=0.0003
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=82
  • Serious adverse events: Liraglutide 3.0 mg 12%; Placebo 8%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
