Serenity PsO NCT05655299

VTX958 Versus Placebo for the Treatment of Moderate to Severe Psoriasis

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Period 1: Percentage of Participants Achieving ≥75% Reductio”: Odds Ratio (OR) 5.77 (95% CI 2.01–16.53), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=222
  • Serious adverse events: VTX958 300 mg BID (PC) 2%; VTX958 225 mg BID (PC) 0%; VTX958 300 mg QD (PC) 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Business Decision”
