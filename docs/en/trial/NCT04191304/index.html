NATRON NCT04191304

A Phase III Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Benralizumab in Patients With Hypereosinophilic Syndrome (HES)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Time to First HES Worsening/Flare During the DB Treatment Pe”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.35 (95% CI 0.18–0.69), p=0.0024
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=134
  • Serious adverse events: Benra - DB 7%; Placebo - DB 8%; Benra - OLE 17%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
