A Proof-of-Concept Study to Assess the Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability of Itepekimab (Anti-IL-33 mAb) in Participants With Non-cystic Fibrosis Bronchiectasis

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Annualized Rate of Adjudicated Moderate or Severe Pulmonary ”: Rate ratio 0.83 (90% CI 0.63–1.10), p=0.2742
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=314
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 24%; Itepekimab 300 mg Q4W 19%; Itepekimab 300 mg Q2W 15%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
