Eli Lilly · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

ATTAIN-2 NCT05872620

A Study of Orforglipron in Adult Participants With Obesity or Overweight and Type 2 Diabetes

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in Body Weight”: LS Mean Difference (Net) -3.28 (95% CI -4.10–-2.47), p=<.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1613
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 9%; 6 mg Orforglipron QD 7%; 12 mg Orforglipron QD 10%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. In ATTAIN-2, in obesity with type 2 diabetes, orforglipron produced about 3–4% more weight loss than placebo (per the registered primary comparison). Weight loss is harder in diabetes, and this size matches the pattern for same-class drugs in that population.
