Mirdametinib + BGB-3245 in Advanced Solid Tumors

Stopped

  • Design: non-randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=23
  • Serious adverse events: Phase 1, Cohort 1 - Dose Escalation 67%; Phase 1, Cohort 2 - Dose Escalation 43%; Phase 1, Cohort 3 - Dose Escalation 83%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Safety”
  • Only 23 patients enrolled at termination, too few to judge the drug itself

Why it stopped

Safety · “Lack of tolerability of BGB-3245 (Brimarafenib) when given in combination with Mirdametinib”
