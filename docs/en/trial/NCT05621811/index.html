MOVE-IT NCT05621811

Different Doses of Naronapride vs. Placebo in Gastroparesis

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Change in the American Neurogastroenterology and Motility So”: Naronapride 10 mg -1.24 Score on a s; Naronapride 20 mg -1.51 Score on a s; Naronapride 40 mg -1.45 Score on a s
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=328
  • Serious adverse events: Naronapride 10 mg 5%; Naronapride 20 mg 5%; Naronapride 40 mg 5%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen
