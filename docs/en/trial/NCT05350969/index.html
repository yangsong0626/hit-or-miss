HF-REVERT NCT05350969

Study to Assess Efficacy and Safety of CDR132L in Patients With Reduced Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction After Myocardial Infarction

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in LVESVI (Left Ventricular End”: LS Mean Difference -2.14 (95% CI -8.60–4.32), p=0.257
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=294
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 10%; CDR132L 5 mg/kg 10%; CDR132L 10 mg/kg 8%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
