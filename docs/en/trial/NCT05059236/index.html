ARASEC NCT05059236

A Study to Learn How Well Darolutamide Administered Together With Androgen Deprivation Therapy (ADT) Works in Men With Metastatic Hormone-sensitive Prostate Cancer. Results Will be Compared With ADT Alone From a Previously Conducted Study.

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Progression Free Survival (PFS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.29 (95% CI 0.20–0.40), p=<0.001
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=223
  • Serious adverse events: M-FAS 24%; ME-FAS 23%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Single-arm trial; the p value compares against baseline or a preset threshold, not a control arm, so the evidence is weaker than a randomised trial
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
