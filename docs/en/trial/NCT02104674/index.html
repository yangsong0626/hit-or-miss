A Study Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Lebrikizumab in Adult Patients With Mild to Moderate Asthma

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Pre-bronchodilator Forced Expiratory”: Mean Difference (Final Values) 0.08 (95% CI 0.00–0.17), p=0.06
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=313
  • Serious adverse events: Lebrikizumab-Safety 2%; Placebo-Safety 1%; Montelukast-Safety 0%

Likely reasons

  • p=0.06 is close to significance; possibly underpowered rather than ineffective

Editor’s analysis

Missed. The IL-13 antibody lebrikizumab improved FEV1 by 0.08 L in mild-to-moderate asthma, p=0.06, with the lower confidence limit right at 0. Mild-to-moderate patients are already well controlled on inhaled steroids, leaving little room for a biologic. Results on exacerbations in severe asthma were also inconsistent, and the drug eventually moved to atopic dermatitis, where it was approved.
