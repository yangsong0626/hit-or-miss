Johnson & Johnson · Phase 3 · Immunology / inflammation

ICONIC-LEAD NCT06095115

A Study of JNJ-77242113 in Adolescent and Adult Participants With Moderate to Severe Plaque Psoriasis

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved an Investigator's Gl”: Treatment difference 56.40 (95% CI 50.40–61.70), p=<0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Psoriasis Area and S”: Treatment difference 45.10 (95% CI 39.50–50.40), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=684
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo (PCP Week 0-16) 3%; JNJ-77242113 200 mg (PCP Week 0-16) 1%; Placebo to JNJ-77242113 200 mg (After PCP Week 16- 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust

Editor’s analysis

Met. ICONIC-LEAD is icotrokinra's foundational phase 3 vs placebo, including adolescents; IGA and PASI90 responses were 45–56 points higher, with serious adverse events lower than placebo.
