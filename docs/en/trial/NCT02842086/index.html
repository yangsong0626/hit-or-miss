Gilead · Phase 3 · Infection / vaccines

DISCOVER NCT02842086

Study to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of Emtricitabine and Tenofovir Alafenamide (F/TAF) Fixed-Dose Combination Once Daily for Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis in Men and Transgender Women Who Have Sex With Men and Are At Risk of HIV-1 Infection

Ended normally

  • Design: randomised, double-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=5399
  • Serious adverse events: Descovy (DVY) 7%; Truvada (TVD) 7%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Goals met”

Editor’s analysis

Not a failure. DISCOVER was the pivotal trial of F/TAF (Descovy) for HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis, long since shown non-inferior to F/TDF and approved. It ended because long-term safety data were sufficient and in-study follow-up was no longer needed, a normal close after meeting its goals.

Why it stopped

Goals met · “Following review of accumulated safety data from DISCOVER and real-world use of F/TAF, the sponsor determined that the long-term safety of F/TAF had been demonstrated and no further safety monitoring within the study was required.”
