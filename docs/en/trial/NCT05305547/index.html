SCORPIO-HR NCT05305547

A Study to Compare S-217622 With Placebo in Non-Hospitalized Participants With COVID-19

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Time to Sustained Symptom Resolution”: Mean Difference -0.60 (95% CI -1.38–0.19), p=0.1397
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=2093
  • Serious adverse events: Ensitrelvir 1%; Placebo 1%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high

Editor’s analysis

Missed. Ensitrelvir (a 3CL protease inhibitor) in non-hospitalised COVID-19 shortened time to sustained symptom resolution by only about 0.6 days, with the confidence interval crossing 0. The main reason is more likely the population than antiviral activity: with widespread vaccination and prior infection, most patients have mild symptoms and recover quickly, leaving little room to improve. The value of such antivirals is shifting to high-risk patients and endpoints such as long COVID.
