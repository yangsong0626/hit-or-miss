Efficacy and Safety of Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) Plus Enzalutamide Plus Androgen… NCT04191096

Efficacy and Safety of Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) Plus Enzalutamide Plus Androgen Deprivation Therapy (ADT) Versus Placebo Plus Enzalutamide Plus ADT in Participants With Metastatic Hormone-Sensitive Prostate Cancer (mHSPC) (MK-3475-991/KEYNOTE-991)

Missed

  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1251
  • Serious adverse events: Pembrolizumab + Enzalutamide + ADT 40%; Placebo + Enzalutamide + ADT 23%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. KEYNOTE-991 added pembrolizumab to enzalutamide + androgen deprivation in metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer and stopped for futility. Prostate cancer is a "cold tumour"; PD-1 alone or with hormone therapy has not helped in several phase 3 trials.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The study stopped due to futility”
