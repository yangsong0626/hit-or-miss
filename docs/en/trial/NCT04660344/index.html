Roche · Phase 3 · Oncology

IMvigor011 NCT04660344

A Study of Atezolizumab Versus Placebo as Adjuvant Therapy in Participants With High-risk Muscle-invasive Bladder Cancer (MIBC) Who Are ctDNA Positive Following Cystectomy

Met

  • Primary endpoint “INV-assessed Disease-free Survival (DFS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.64 (95% CI 0.47–0.87), p=0.0047
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=761
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 20%; Atezolizumab 27%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 6 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. IMvigor011 in ctDNA-positive high-risk muscle-invasive bladder cancer after cystectomy: adjuvant atezolizumab DFS HR 0.64. The earlier IMvigor010, which did not select by ctDNA, was negative; the key to this positive trial was using ctDNA to find patients with true residual disease, an important validation of ctDNA-guided adjuvant therapy.
