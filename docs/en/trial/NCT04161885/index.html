A Study Evaluating Safety and Efficacy of Venetoclax in Combination With Azacitidine Versus Standard of Care After Allogeneic Stem Cell Transplantation (SCT) in Participants With Acute Myeloid Leukemia (AML)

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants With Dose-Limiting Toxicities (DLTs) ”: Part 1: Venetoclax 200 mg QD 28 Days + Azacitidine 3.00 Participants; Part 1: Venetoclax 200 mg QD 14 Days + Azacitidine 0.00 Participants
  • Design: randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=465
  • Serious adverse events: Part 1: Venetoclax 200 mg QD 28 Days + Azacitidine 30%; Part 1: Venetoclax 200 mg QD 14 Days + Azacitidine 40%; Part 2: Best Supportive Care 31%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen
