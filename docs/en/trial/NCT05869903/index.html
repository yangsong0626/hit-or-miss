Eli Lilly · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

ATTAIN-1 NCT05869903

A Study of Orforglipron (LY3502970) in Adult Participants With Obesity or Overweight With Weight-Related Comorbidities

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in Body Weight (Primary Treatme”: LS Mean Difference (Net) -6.95 (95% CI -7.71–-6.18), p=<.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=3127
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 5%; 6 mg Orforglipron 6%; 12 mg Orforglipron 5%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. ATTAIN-1 is the pivotal phase 3 of the oral small-molecule GLP-1 agonist orforglipron in obesity, with about 7% more weight loss than placebo (more at the top dose). A clear effect but smaller than injectable semaglutide and tirzepatide; its selling points are oral dosing without food restrictions and easier large-scale manufacturing.
