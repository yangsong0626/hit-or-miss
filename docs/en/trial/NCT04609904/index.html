AstraZeneca · Phase 3 · Immunology / inflammation

LOGOS NCT04609904

Study to Assess PT010 in Adult and Adolescent Participants With Inadequately Controlled Asthma (LOGOS)

Partly met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in FEV1 AUC0-3 (L) at Week 24”: LS Mean Difference 0.15 (95% CI 0.11–0.20), p=<0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Pooled (LOGOS/KALOS): Rate of Severe Asthma Exacerbations”: Rate ratio 0.88 (95% CI 0.75–1.03), p=0.120
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=2187
  • Serious adverse events: BGF MDI 320/14.4/9.6 μg BID 8%; BGF MDI 320/28.8/9.6 μg BID 7%; BFF MDI 320/9.6 μg BID 8%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo

Editor’s analysis

Partly met. LOGOS reached the same conclusion as KALOS: triple inhalation significantly improved FEV1, but the pooled exacerbation rate did not fall significantly. Consistency across the two trials shows the issue is not single-trial power.
