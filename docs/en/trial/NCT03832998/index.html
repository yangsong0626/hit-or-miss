OASIS(CM) NCT03832998

Efficacy and Safety of Erenumab in Pediatric Subjects With Chronic Migraine

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in MMD to Week 9 Through Week 12 (Month”: Difference in LSM -0.08 (95% CI -1.80–1.65), p=0.93
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=284
  • Serious adverse events: Adolescents - Placebo (DBTP) 5%; Adolescents - AMG 334 (DBTP) 3%; Children - Placebo (DBTP) 7%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 7 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
