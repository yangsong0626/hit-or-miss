FORTUNE NCT03453112

Foster 100/6 mg NEXThaler Versus Foster 100/6mg Pressurized Metered-dose Inhaler (pMDI) in Patients With Controlled Asthma.

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline to End of Treatment Period in Average P”: Adjusted Mean Difference 5.25 (95% CI -0.56–11.06), p=0.077
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=494
  • Serious adverse events: Run-in and Randomized Foster 100/6µg NEXThaler (SA 1%; Run-in and Randomized Foster 100/6µg pMDI (SAF Pop 2%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo

Editor’s analysis

Missed. A comparison of the same budesonide/formoterol combination switched from a pressurised metered-dose inhaler to a dry-powder inhaler (NEXThaler); the peak-flow difference favoured it but p=0.077. Two devices delivering the same drug are not expected to differ much; such trials usually aim to show interchangeability, not superiority.
