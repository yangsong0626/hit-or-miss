Efficacy and Safety Extension Study of Oral Edaravone Administered in Subjects With ALS

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=202
  • Serious adverse events: Edaravone 105 mg (Once Daily) 32%; Edaravone 105 mg (on/Off) 28%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Editor’s analysis

Stopped as a consequence of efficacy failure. Extension study of the on-demand oral edaravone regimen, stopped together with the parent study MT-1186-A02 after it triggered a futility analysis.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Futility Analysis of parent study MT-1186-A02 was met.”
