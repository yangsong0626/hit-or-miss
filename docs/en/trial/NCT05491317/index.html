A Safety and Antitumor Activity Trial of Immunoradiotherapy Combinations as a Treatment Option for Subjects With Metastatic Solid Tumors

No statistical test

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “After reviewing all available data from Part 1 of the trial, the Sponsor has decided not to proceed to Part 2 (randomized Phase 2) of the trial due to lack of efficacy”
