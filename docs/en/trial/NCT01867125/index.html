A Study of Lebrikizumab in Participants With Uncontrolled Asthma Who Are on Inh… NCT01867125

A Study of Lebrikizumab in Participants With Uncontrolled Asthma Who Are on Inhaled Corticosteroids and a Second Controller Medication

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Adjusted Exacerbation Rate of Asthma During the 52-Week PCP”: Rate Ratio 0.49 (95% CI 0.35–0.69), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1081
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 9%; Placebo/Lebrikizumab (37.5 mg) 9%; Placebo/Lebrikizumab (125 mg) 10%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. This LAVOLTA trial halved the exacerbation rate in severe asthma (rate ratio 0.49). But the parallel trial in the same programme missed, and lebrikizumab's asthma programme stopped over the inconsistency, later shifting to atopic dermatitis. A single positive phase 3 is not enough for filing; consistency is key.
