RELATIVITY-123 NCT05328908

A Study of Nivolumab-relatlimab Fixed-dose Combination Versus Regorafenib or TAS-102 in Participants With Later-lines of Metastatic Colorectal Cancer

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival (OS)”: Arm A 7.72 Months; Arm B 8.57 Months
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=769
  • Serious adverse events: Arm A 59%; Arm B 54%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

The registry gives no reason. RELATIVITY-123 tested nivolumab + relatlimab (LAG-3) against regorafenib or TAS-102 in microsatellite-stable metastatic colorectal cancer. Such colorectal cancers are generally insensitive to immunotherapy, so early termination most likely relates to efficacy, an inference pending company statements.
