Likely reasons

Editor’s analysis

Reason unclear. HERMES is the large outcomes trial of the IL-6 antibody ziltivekimab in heart failure with preserved ejection fraction; the registry entry "No longer looking for participants" is a status note, not a reason for stopping. This is the core trial of Novo Nordisk's cardiovascular inflammation pipeline; wait for a company statement to judge whether it is efficacy, event rate or strategy.
