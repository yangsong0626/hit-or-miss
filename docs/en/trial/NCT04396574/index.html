PIONEER-PEDS2 NCT04396574

A 12-Month Study of Lasmiditan (LY573144) Treatment in Children Aged 6 to 17 With Migraine

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants With at Least One Treatment Emergent ”: 25/50/100 mg Lasmiditan 109 Participants; 50/100/200 mg Lasmiditan 133 Participants
  • Design: randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=558
  • Serious adverse events: 25/50/100 mg Lasmiditan 2%; 50/100/200 mg Lasmiditan 3%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

Strategic termination. PIONEER-PEDS2, a long-term study of lasmiditan in paediatric migraine, was closed together with PEDS1 for business reasons, unrelated to drug safety.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Study terminated due to strategic business decision”
