Re-PHIRE NCT05737940

A Study of AZD3427 in Participants With Heart Failure and Pulmonary Hypertension Group 2

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Pulmonary Vascular Resistance (PVR)”: Geometric mean ratio compared to placebo 1.16 (95% CI 0.97–1.35), p=0.113
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=260
  • Serious adverse events: AZD3427 Dose 1.1 mg 17%; AZD3427 Dose 5.4 mg 21%; AZD3427 Dose 30.0 mg 16%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 10 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
