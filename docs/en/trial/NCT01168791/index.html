PICASSO III NCT01168791

Study of Palifosfamide-tris in Combination With Doxorubicin in Patients With Front-line Metastatic Soft Tissue Sarcoma

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=447

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. PICASSO 3 added palifosfamide to doxorubicin in soft-tissue sarcoma; PFS missed the primary endpoint and the sponsor stopped the study before the final OS analysis. Almost every first-line soft-tissue sarcoma trial adding to doxorubicin has failed.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Primary endpoint (PFS) not met at the final analysis; sponsor decided to stop the study before the planned final overall survival analysis.”
