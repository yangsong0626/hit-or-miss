A Study of JNJ-63733657 in Participants With Early Alzheimer's Disease

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Double-blind (DB) Treatment Period: Change From Baseline in ”: Difference of LS Means 1.00 (95% CI -2.01–4.08), p==0.986
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=523
  • Serious adverse events: Double-Blind (DB) Treatment Period: JNJ-63733657 1 20%; Double-Blind (DB) Treatment Period: JNJ-63733657 3 24%; Double-Blind (DB) Treatment Period: Placebo 25%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 17 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Posdinemab did not achieve statistical significance in slowing clinical decline”
