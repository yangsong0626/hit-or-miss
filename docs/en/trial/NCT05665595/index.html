A Study of Adjuvant Pembrolizumab/Vibostolimab (MK-7684A) Versus Pembrolizumab for Resected High-Risk Melanoma in Participants With High-Risk Stage II-IV Melanoma (MK-7684A-010/KEYVIBE-010)

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1594
  • Serious adverse events: Pembrolizumab/Vibostolimab 32%; Pembrolizumab 17%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Sponsor decision / strategy”

Editor’s analysis

Ostensibly "business reasons", but the background is efficacy. This was the trial of Merck's TIGIT antibody vibostolimab coformulated with pembrolizumab (MK-7684A) in adjuvant melanoma. The programme's lung cancer trials (KEYVIBE-003, -007) had already stopped for futility, and Merck then halted the whole vibostolimab programme. Part of the collective failure of TIGIT.
