Adoring NCT07265479

A Study to Investigate Safety and Efficacy of Tapinarof Cream, 1% in Participants Ages 3 Months to < 24 Months With Atopic Dermatitis

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=187

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Safety”

Editor’s analysis

Precautionary pause. Organon's tapinarof cream (an aryl hydrocarbon receptor agonist approved for psoriasis and atopic dermatitis) paused dosing in this trial because of a pharmacokinetic finding in another study; the sponsor said no safety signal has been found. Watch the follow-up assessment.

Why it stopped

Safety · “As a precaution, study drug dosing is paused due to a PK finding in OG505-3104. No safety signal or change to the established safety or benefit-risk profile has been identified. The Sponsor is further investigating and assessing the data.”
