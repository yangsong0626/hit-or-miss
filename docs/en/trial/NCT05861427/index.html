JPN PIVOTAL NCT05861427

Study of Oral Atogepant Tablets to Assess Change in Disease Activity in Adult Japanese Participants With Episodic Migraine

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Mean Monthly Migraine Days”: LS Mean -1.57 (95% CI -2.24–-0.89), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=524
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 1%; Placebo/Atogepant 10 mg 2%; Placebo/Atogepant 30 mg 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust

Editor’s analysis

Met. Atogepant significantly reduced monthly migraine days in Japanese patients with episodic migraine, consistent with global data and supporting a Japanese filing.
