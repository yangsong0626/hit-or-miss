A Study to Learn About the Safety and Effects of Rimegepant to Prevent Migraine in Chinese Subjects.

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Mean Change From the Observation Phase (OP) in Number of Mig”: Difference in Least square mean (LSM) -1.02 (95% CI -1.40–-0.63), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=787
  • Serious adverse events: DBT Rimegepant 2%; DBT Placebo 1%; DBT Rimegepant/ OLE Rimegepant 1%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. Rimegepant for migraine prevention in Chinese patients reduced monthly migraine days about 1 day more than placebo. The effect matches global trials, supporting a prevention filing in China.
