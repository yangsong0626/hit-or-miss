DANTE NCT03874858

A Study of Full Treatment-free Remission in Patients With Chronic Myeloid Leukemia

Stopped

  • Design: non-randomised, open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=135

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Sponsor decision: The primary endpoint of the core/TF1 was achieved, and the sample size of Extension/TFR2 was unlikely to yield meaningful results. No new safety signals were observed. For these reasons, continuing the study was not justified.”
