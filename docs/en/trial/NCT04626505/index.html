RESCUE-2 NCT04626505

Trial to Evaluate Reduction in Inflammation in Patients With Advanced Chronic Renal Disease Utilizing Antibody Mediated IL-6 Inhibition in Japan.

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change in High-sensitivity C-reactive Protein (Hs-CR”: Median of differences -66.80 (95% CI -87.79–-44.84), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=36
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 17%; Ziltivekimab 15 mg 18%; Ziltivekimab 30 mg 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 18 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
