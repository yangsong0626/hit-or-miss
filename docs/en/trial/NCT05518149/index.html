VENTURA-LT NCT05518149

A Study of Aticaprant in Adult and Elderly Participants With Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Open Label (OL) Treatment Phase: Number of Participants With”: Aticaprant 10 mg 504 Participants
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=871
  • Serious adverse events: Open-label Treatment Phase: Aticaprant 10 mg 3%; Follow-up Phase: Aticaprant 10 mg 0%

Likely reasons

  • Single-arm or uncontrolled design; the registry has only descriptive data, so success cannot be judged statistically and needs comparison with historical controls or similar drugs

Editor’s analysis

The registry says "sponsor decision", but it was insufficient efficacy. VENTURA-LT was the long-term study of J&J's κ-opioid receptor antagonist aticaprant; J&J stopped the whole phase 3 programme in March 2025 for insufficient efficacy. Together with navacaprant's failure, this mechanism's efficacy in depression is not enough to support phase 3.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “The study was Early Terminated as per Sponsor's decision (communicated to sites on 06Mar2025)”
