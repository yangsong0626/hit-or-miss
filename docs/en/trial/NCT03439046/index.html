Novartis · Phase 3 · Oncology

BioItaLEE NCT03439046

Study of the Molecular Features of Postmenopausal Women With HR+ HER2-negative aBC on First-line Treatment With Ribociclib and Letrozole and, in Patients With a PIK3CA Mutation, on Second-line Treatment With Alpelisib Plus Fulvestrant

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Progression-Free Survival (PFS) by Cycle 1 Day 15 Complete M”: Ribociclib+Letrozole (Core Phase) 55.82 months
  • Design: open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=287
  • Serious adverse events: Ribociclib+Letrozole (Core Phase) 28%; Alpelisib+Fulvestrant (Extension Phase) 29%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

Not a failure. Novartis ended BioItaLEE early because the data collected were enough to complete the primary endpoint analysis, a normal completion.

Why it stopped

Goals met · “Novartis terminated the trial early because the collected data were sufficient to perform the analysis stated for the primary endpoint.”
