Met

  • Primary endpoint “Progression Free Survival (PFS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.38 (95% CI 0.25–0.57), p=< 0.001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=153
  • Serious adverse events: Dabrafenib Plus Trametinib (On-treatment) 43%; Dabrafenib Placebo Plus Trametinib Placebo (On-tre 25%; Crossover Dabrafenib Plus Trametinib (On-treatment 47%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 22 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
