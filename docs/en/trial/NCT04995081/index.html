Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in MDS-UPDRS Part III (Statistical Anal”: Mean Difference (Net) -2.65 (95% CI -7.38–2.07), p=0.2629
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled

  • Serious adverse events: Allogeneic HB-adMSCs. 3%; Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Sample size only 60, limited power to detect a moderate effect
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
