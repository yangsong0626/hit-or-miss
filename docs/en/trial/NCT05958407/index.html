ADHAND NCT05958407

A 32-week Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tralokinumab in Subjects With Moderate-to-severe Atopic Hand Eczema Who Are Candidates for Systemic Therapy

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants With IGA-AHE Score of 0 (Clear) o”: treatment difference in percentage 29.60 (95% CI 19.20–40.00), p=<.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=235
  • Serious adverse events: Tralokinumab DB 2%; Placebo DB 1%; Tralokinumab OL 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
