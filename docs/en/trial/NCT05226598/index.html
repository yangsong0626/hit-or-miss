Study of Pembrolizumab/Vibostolimab Coformulation (MK-7684A) in Combination With Chemotherapy Versus Pembrolizumab Plus Chemotherapy in Participants With Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (MK-7684A-007/KEYVIBE-007)

Missed

  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=739
  • Serious adverse events: MK-7684A + Chemotherapy 57%; Pembrolizumab + Chemotherapy 49%

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Efficacy / futility”

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. KEYVIBE-007, the vibostolimab/pembrolizumab coformulation + chemotherapy in first-line non-small cell lung cancer, stopped for futility; one of the direct reasons Merck halted its whole TIGIT programme.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “MK-7684A-007 was terminated early due to futility. A non-submission CSR, not intended to support any registration, was issued; therefore, an abbreviated CSR was generated.”
