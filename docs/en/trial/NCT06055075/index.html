A Study Evaluating Safety, Tolerability, and Clinical Activity of Forimtamig-Ba… NCT06055075

A Study Evaluating Safety, Tolerability, and Clinical Activity of Forimtamig-Based Treatment Combinations in Participants With Relapsed or Refractory Multiple Myeloma

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants With Adverse Effects (AEs)”: Cohort 1: Arm B1: Forimtamig 0.3 mg + Carfilzomib 7.00 Participants; Cohort 1: Arm B2: Forimtamig 0.75 mg + Carfilzomib 6.00 Participants; Cohort 1: Arm B3: Forimtamig 1.5 mg + Carfilzomib 6.00 Participants
  • Design: randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=21
  • Serious adverse events: Cohort 1: Arm B1: Forimtamig 0.3 mg + Carfilzomib 71%; Cohort 1: Arm B2: Forimtamig 0.75 mg + Carfilzomib 50%; Cohort 1: Arm B3: Forimtamig 1.5 mg + Carfilzomib 67%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Regulatory · “The study was terminated prematurely because the Sponsor decided not to pursue the development of forimtamig.”
