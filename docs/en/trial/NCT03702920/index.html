Instituto Grifols · Phase 3 · GI / liver

APACHE NCT03702920

Short-term Survival of Subjects With Acute-on-chronic Liver Failure After Plasma Exchange With Human Serum Albumin 5%

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Time to Death Without a Prior Liver Transplant Through Day 9”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.65 (95% CI 0.45–0.94), p=0.021
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=274
  • Serious adverse events: SMT + PE-A 5% 29%; SMT Alone 20%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Due to corporate business reasons only (non-safety related decision)”
