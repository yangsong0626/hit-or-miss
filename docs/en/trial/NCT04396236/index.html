Eli Lilly · Phase 3 · Neuro / psych / pain

PIONEER-PEDS1 NCT04396236

A Study of Lasmiditan (LY573144) Treatment in Children Aged 6 to 17 With Migraine

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Had Freedom From Pain at 2 Ho”: Odds Ratio (OR) 1.81 (95% CI 1.09–3.01), p=0.021
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=847
  • Serious adverse events: Stage 1 Placebo Responders + Stage 1 and Stage 2 P 0%; Stage 2: 25/ 50 mg Lasmiditan 0%; Stage 1 Lasmiditan Responders + Stage 2 50/100 mg 1%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Strategic termination, but the primary endpoint was met. The lasmiditan paediatric migraine trial had a positive result before stopping early (odds ratio 1.81 for pain freedom at 2 hours). Termination more likely reflects lower commercial priority for the product (competition from oral gepants) than lack of efficacy in children.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Study terminated due to strategic business decision”
