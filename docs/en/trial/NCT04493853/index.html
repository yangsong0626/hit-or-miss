AstraZeneca · Phase 3 · Oncology

CAPItello-281 NCT04493853

Capivasertib+Abiraterone as Treatment for Patients With Metastatic Hormone-sensitive Prostate Cancer and PTEN Deficiency

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Radiographic Progression-free Survival (rPFS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.81 (95% CI 0.66–0.98), p=0.034
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=1012
  • Serious adverse events: Capivasertib + Abiraterone 43%; Placebo + Abiraterone 26%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 17 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. CAPItello-281 in PTEN-deficient metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer: the AKT inhibitor capivasertib + abiraterone had radiographic PFS HR 0.81, but the upper confidence limit of 0.98 is close to 1, a limited effect, with serious adverse events 17 points higher. Biomarker selection is the right direction, but the balance of benefit and toxicity will shape clinical uptake.
