A Study of Seltorexant Compared to Quetiapine XR as Adjunctive Therapy to Antidepressants in Adult and Elderly Participants With Major Depressive Disorder With Insomnia Symptoms Who Have Responded Inadequately to Antidepressant Therapy

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Response (Greater Th”: Percentage difference 4.00 (95% CI -3.30–11.30), p==0.2422
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=757
  • Serious adverse events: Double Blind Phase: Seltorexant 1%; Double Blind Phase: Quetiapine XR 2%; Follow-up Phase: Seltorexant 1%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo
