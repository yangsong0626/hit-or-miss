ADVANCE-2 NCT05557409

A Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of AXS-05 in Subjects With Alzheimer's Disease Agitation

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory (CMAI)”: p=0.407
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=408
  • Serious adverse events: AXS-05 1%; Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high

Editor’s analysis

Missed. ADVANCE-2 of AXS-05 (dextromethorphan + bupropion) in Alzheimer's agitation showed no significant difference from placebo in CMAI improvement (p=0.41). Agitation trials have large placebo responses and noisy caregiver ratings, and only brexpiprazole had been approved in this indication. The drug's evidence in this indication is now inconsistent.
