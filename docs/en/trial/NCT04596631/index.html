PIONEER TEENS NCT04596631

A Research Study to Compare a New Medicine Oral Semaglutide to a Dummy Medicine in Children and Teenagers With Type 2 Diabetes

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline (Week 0) to Week 26 in Glycosylated Hae”: Treatment difference -0.83 (95% CI -1.25–-0.40), p=0.0002
  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline (Week 0) in HbA1c to Week 26- Millimole”: Treatment difference -9.06 (95% CI -13.69–-4.42), p=0.0002
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=132
  • Serious adverse events: Oral Semaglutide 8%; Placebo 3%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
