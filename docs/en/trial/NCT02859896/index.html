  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved 2 Consecutive >=30% ”: Doxercalciferol (Hectorol®) 14.30 percentage o; Calcitriol (Rocaltrol®) 71.40 percentage o

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped
