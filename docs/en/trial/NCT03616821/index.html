Expedition NCT03616821

Placebo-Controlled Study of Brazikumab in Participants With Moderately to Severely Active Ulcerative Colitis

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Clinical Remission”: Brazikumab 720 mg 13.00 Participants; Brazikumab 1440 mg 16.00 Participants; Brazikumab 2100 mg 2.00 Participants
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=242
  • Serious adverse events: Brazikumab 720 mg (Induction Period) 4%; Brazikumab 1440 mg (Induction Period)) 3%; Brazikumab 2100 mg (Induction Period) 0%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Strategic decision to discontinue the development of brazikumab in inflammatory bowel disease.”
