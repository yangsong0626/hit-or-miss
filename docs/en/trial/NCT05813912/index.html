A Research Study to See How a New Weekly Insulin, Insulin Icodec When Given Along With Semaglutide Helps in Reducing the Blood Sugar Level in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change in Glycated Haemoglobin (HbA1c)”: Mean Difference (Final Values) -1.23 (95% CI -1.39–-1.07), p=<0.0001
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=148
  • Serious adverse events: Icodec: Run-in Period 5%; Insulin Icodec + Semaglutide: Treatment Period 3%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Single-arm trial; the p value compares against baseline or a preset threshold, not a control arm, so the evidence is weaker than a randomised trial
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
