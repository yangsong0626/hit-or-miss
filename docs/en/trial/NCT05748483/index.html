ATO-TOPIRAMATE NCT05748483

Comparative Study of Oral Atogepant Versus Oral Topiramate to Assess Adverse Events in Adult Participants With Migraine

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Discontinued Treatment Due to”: Relative Risk 0.41 (95% CI 0.28–0.59), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=545
  • Serious adverse events: DB: Topiramate 1%; DB: Atogepant 3%; OL: Atogepant 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful

Editor’s analysis

Met. A head-to-head migraine-prevention trial of atogepant vs topiramate; the primary endpoint was discontinuation for adverse events, with about 59% lower risk on atogepant. It proves a tolerability advantage; topiramate's cognitive side effects are a common reason for stopping in practice.
