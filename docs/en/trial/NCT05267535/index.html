Piromelatine 20 mg in Participants With Mild Dementia Due to Alzheimer's Disease NCT05267535

Piromelatine 20 mg in Participants With Mild Dementia Due to Alzheimer's Disease

No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale Cognitive Subscale (ADA”: Piromelatine -1.90 ADAS-Cog sco; Placebo -2.00 ADAS-Cog sco
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=216
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo (Double Blind) 0%; Piromelatine (Double Blind) 4%; Placebo (Open Label) 5%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. Piromelatine (a melatonin and 5-HT receptor agonist) in mild Alzheimer's dementia did not beat placebo on cognitive scales or other neuropsychiatric measures.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Treatment with 20 mg piromelatine did not demonstrate efficacy over treatment with placebo based on analysis with the ADAS-Cog 14 subscale and with other cognitive and neuropsychiatric parameters.”
