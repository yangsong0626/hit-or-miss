Editor’s analysis

Stopped as a consequence of efficacy failure. Carbetocin nasal spray for hyperphagia in Prader-Willi syndrome; the parent study missed its primary endpoint and the extension stopped with it. PWS hyperphagia scoring relies on caregivers, and high placebo response is one reason this field keeps failing.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Primary efficacy endpoint was not met in the parent study (ACP-101-302). Based on review of available data, continued administration of investigational product in ACP-101-303 was not considered likely to provide significant clinical benefit.”
