APPLAUSE-IgAN NCT04578834

Study of Efficacy and Safety of LNP023 in Primary IgA Nephropathy Patients

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Ratio to Baseline in UPCR at 9 Months - Interim Analysis”: Geometric mean ratio 0.62 (95% CI 0.51–0.74), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Annualized Total eGFR Slope Estimated Over 24 Months - Final”: LSM of difference 3.02 (95% CI 2.02–4.01), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=517
  • Serious adverse events: On-trt LNP023 200mg b.i.d. Main 12%; On-trt Placebo Main 12%; On-trt All Participants Main 12%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 9 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
