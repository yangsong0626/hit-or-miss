A Study of Lerociclib in Participants With Advanced Breast Cancer

Descriptive

  • Primary endpoint “Incidence of AEs and SAEs”: Lerociclib + Letrozole or Fulvestrant 1L 11.00 participants; Lerociclib + Letrozole or Fulvestrant 2L 12.00 participants
  • Design: open-label, single-arm, uncontrolled
  • actual enrollment n=100
  • Serious adverse events: Lerociclib + Letrozole or Fulvestrant 1L 7%; Lerociclib + Letrozole or Fulvestrant 2L 4%

Likely reasons

  • Single-arm or uncontrolled design; the registry has only descriptive data, so success cannot be judged statistically and needs comparison with historical controls or similar drugs

Why it stopped

Safety · “The study is being closed based on corporate changes at EQRx and is not related to any efficacy or safety issues with lerociclib.”
