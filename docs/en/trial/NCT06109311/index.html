ACHIEVE-5 NCT06109311

A Study of Orforglipron (LY3502970) in Participants With Type 2 Diabetes and Inadequate Glycemic Control With Insulin Glargine, With or Without Metformin and/or SGLT-2 Inhibitor

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)”: Least Square (LS) Mean Difference (Net) -0.77 (95% CI -1.01–-0.52), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=546
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 4%; 3 mg Orforglipron QD 5%; 12 mg Orforglipron QD 7%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. ACHIEVE-5 tested orforglipron in insulin-treated type 2 diabetes; HbA1c fell about 0.8 points more than placebo, consistent with earlier ACHIEVE trials.
