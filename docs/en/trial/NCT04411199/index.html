D-PLEX312 NCT04411199

D-PLEX 312 - Safety and Efficacy of D-PLEX in the Prevention of Post Abdominal Surgery Incisional Infection (SHIELD II)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Infection Rate as Measured by the Percentage of Subjects Wit”: Risk Difference (RD) -7.20 (95% CI -12.10–-2.30), p=0.0039
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=975
  • Serious adverse events: D-PLEX + Standard of Care (SoC) 16%; Standard of Care (SoC) 17%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust

Editor’s analysis

Met. D-PLEX is a locally sustained-release doxycycline implant for surgical incisions, cutting incision infection in colorectal surgery by about 7 points. An open-label design vs standard care, but infection is an objective endpoint, limiting bias.
