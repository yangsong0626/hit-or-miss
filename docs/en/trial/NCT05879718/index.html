A Study to Learn About the Study Medicine (PF-06823859) in Adults With Active CLE or SLE With Skin Symptoms.

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Type 1 Interferon (IFN) Gene Signatu”: Least Square Mean Difference 0.50 (90% CI -3.70–4.60), p==0.8243
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=8
  • Serious adverse events: PF-06823859 (at Q4W) 0%; PF-06823859 (at Q4W) Then PF-06823859 (at Q8W) 0%; Placebo (at Q4W) 0%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • Sample size only 8, limited power to detect a moderate effect
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Pfizer has made an internal business decision to not continue this study. This decision was not due to safety concerns or clinical effect reasons or requests from any regulatory authorities.”
