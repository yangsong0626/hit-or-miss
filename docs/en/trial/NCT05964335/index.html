Cough Reduction in IPF With Nalbuphine ER

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Relative Change From Baseline in 24-hour Cough Frequency at ”: Least square (LS) mean difference -1.08 (95% CI -1.55–-0.61), p=0.0000
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=165
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 10%; NAL ER 27 mg 2%; NAL ER 54 mg 0%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 10 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
