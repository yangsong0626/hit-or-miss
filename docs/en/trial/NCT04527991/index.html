Gilead · Phase 3 · Oncology

TROPiCS-04 NCT04527991

Study of Sacituzumab Govitecan Versus Physician's Choice of Treatment in Participants With Urothelial Cancer That Cannot Be Removed or Has Spread

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival (OS)”: Stratified Hazard Ratio 0.86 (95% CI 0.73–1.02), p=0.0870
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=711
  • Serious adverse events: Sacituzumab Govitecan-hziy (SG) 53%; Treatment of Physician's Choice 33%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 20 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Missed. TROPiCS-04 compared sacituzumab govitecan with physician's-choice chemotherapy in urothelial cancer after platinum and immunotherapy; OS HR 0.86 (0.73–1.02), p=0.087. Favourable but not significant, while serious adverse events were 20 points higher with the ADC (53% vs 33%) and early neutropenia-related deaths dragged down overall survival. The drug's US urothelial indication was later withdrawn.
