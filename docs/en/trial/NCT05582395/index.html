Hit or Miss / Trials / ODYSSEY-HCM

Bristol Myers Squibb · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

ODYSSEY-HCM NCT05582395

A Study of Mavacamten in Non-Obstructive Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in KCCQ-23 CSS at Week 48”: Least Square Mean Difference 2.74 (95% CI -0.08–5.56), p=0.056
  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in pVO2 at Week 48”: Least Square Mean 0.47 (95% CI -0.03–0.98), p=0.066
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=580
  • Serious adverse events: MAVACAMTEN 53%; PLACEBO 42%; MAVACAMTEN/MAVACAMTEN 25%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 33 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Efficacy failure. ODYSSEY-HCM used mavacamten in non-obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy; both primary endpoints had p values around 0.06 and the trial stopped for lack of short-term efficacy. Non-obstructive disease has no outflow gradient to relieve, so the benefit of cardiac myosin inhibitors is clearly smaller in this population.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Trial terminated because of lack of efficacy in the short term acute phase”
