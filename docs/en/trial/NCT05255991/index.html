TETON-2 NCT05255991

Multinational Study of Efficacy and Safety of Inhaled Treprostinil in Subjects With Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change in Absolute FVC From Baseline to Week 52”: Treatment difference 95.60 (95% CI 52.20–139), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=597
  • Serious adverse events: Inhaled Treprostinil 25%; Placebo 23%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
