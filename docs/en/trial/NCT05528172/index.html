A Study to Investigate the Safety and Efficacy of Ripasudil (K-321) Eye Drops After Cataract Surgery

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Central Corneal Endothelial Cell Den”: p=0.8607
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=330
  • Serious adverse events: K-321 12-Week Treatment (Group A and B) 3%; Placebo 12-Week Treatment (Group C and D) 2%; K-321 4-Week Follow-Up (Group A and B) 0%

Likely reasons

  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high

Editor’s analysis

Missed. Ripasudil eye drops (a ROCK inhibitor) for corneal oedema after cataract surgery showed no difference from placebo on the primary endpoint of corneal endothelial cell density change. Most post-operative endothelial injury compensates on its own, leaving a very small detectable difference for a drug.
