A Study to Assess Change in Disease Activity, Adverse Events, and How the Drug… NCT06032546

A Study to Assess Change in Disease Activity, Adverse Events, and How the Drug Moves Through the Body in Adult Participants Living With Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) Receiving Intravenous (IV) Infusion or Subcutaneous (SC) Injection of Budigalimab and/or ABBV-382

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants With Viral Control Without Antire”: Response Rate Difference 18.80 (90% CI 5.35–32.27), p=0.0722
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=163
  • Serious adverse events: Part 1: Placebo 0%; Part 1: Budigalimab IV Mono 25%; Part 1: ABBV-382 IV Mono 5%

Likely reasons

  • p=0.07 is close to significance; possibly underpowered rather than ineffective
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 25 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Company Decision”
