Parallel-Arm Study to Compare AD109 to Placebo With Patients With OSA (SynAIRgy Study)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Apnea-Hypopnea Index (AHI)”: Least Squares Mean Difference -6.54 (95% CI -8.80–-4.28), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=646
  • Serious adverse events: AD109 2%; Placebo 3%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. AD109 (atomoxetine + aroxybutynin) is the first oral drug targeting a cause of obstructive sleep apnoea (upper-airway muscle tone), lowering AHI by about 6.5 events/hour more than placebo. A clear but moderate effect, probably best suited to patients who cannot tolerate CPAP.
