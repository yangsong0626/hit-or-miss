A Study to Evaluate Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Darigabat in Participants With Panic Disorder

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Free of Panic Attacks as Assessed”: Difference in proportion of participants -0.06 (95% CI -0.33–0.23), p=0.7488
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=80
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo 0%; Darigabat 25 mg BID 0%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • Sample size only 80, limited power to detect a moderate effect
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “Sponsor terminated due to significant enrollment and study design challenges”
