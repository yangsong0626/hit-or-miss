POSITANO NCT05281328

A Trial to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of Octreotide Subcutaneous Depot in Patients With PLD

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Height-adjusted Total Liver Volume (htTLV)”: estimated relative treatment diff (%) -4.30 (95% CI -8.40–-0.10), p=0.0443
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=71
  • Serious adverse events: CAM2029 Once Weekly 8%; CAM2029 Once Every 2 Weeks 13%; Placebo 22%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 13 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
