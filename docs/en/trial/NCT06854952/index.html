FALCON NCT06854952

A Clinical Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of TERN-601 in Adults With Overweight or Obesity

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change in Body Weight”: LSM Difference -4.58 (90% CI -6.10–-3.00), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=167
  • Serious adverse events: TERN-601 250 mg 0%; TERN-601 500 mg (Slow Titration) 0%; TERN-601 500 mg 3%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 6 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
