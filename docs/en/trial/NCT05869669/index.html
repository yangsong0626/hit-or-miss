Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change in Clinical Dementia Rating Scale - Sum of Boxes (CDR”: Mean Difference (Final Values) 0.01 (95% CI -0.37–0.40), p=0.9448
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 8 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
