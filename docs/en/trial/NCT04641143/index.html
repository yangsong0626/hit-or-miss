Efficacy and Safety Study of Adjunctive Troriluzole in Obsessive Compulsive Disorder

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in the Y-BOCS Total Score in the Baseli”: Least squares mean difference -0.48 (95% CI -1.65–0.70), p=0.406
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=456
  • Serious adverse events: Troriluzole 2%; Placebo 1%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The arms are very close; the placebo response may be high
