No statistical test

  • Primary endpoint “Absolute Change From Baseline in Forced Vital Capacity (FVC)”: GSK3915393 -110 Milliliters; Placebo -79.00 Milliliters
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=158
  • Serious adverse events: GSK3915393 12%; Placebo 2%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “The study met futility criteria at pre-planned interim analysis, showing no clinical efficacy of the investigational drug. Based on the lack of efficacy at the interim data review, the sponsor decided to terminate the study.”
