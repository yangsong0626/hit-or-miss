A Study of Enlicitide Decanoate (MK-0616 Oral PCSK9 Inhibitor) in Adults With Hypercholesterolemia (MK-0616-013) CORALreef Lipids

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Mean Percent Change From Baseline in Low-density Lipoprotein”: Difference in Means -55.80 (95% CI -60.90–-50.70), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=2912
  • Serious adverse events: Enlicitide 20 mg 10%; Placebo 12%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. Merck's oral PCSK9 macrocyclic peptide enlicitide lowered LDL-C about 56% (placebo-adjusted) in hypercholesterolaemia, close to injectable PCSK9 antibodies, with serious adverse events similar to placebo. The first time an oral PCSK9 inhibitor reached this reduction in phase 3; the key next step is the cardiovascular outcomes trial.
