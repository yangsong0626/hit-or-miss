FORTUNA NCT05809934

A Study to Evaluate AZD2693 in Participants Who Are Carriers of the PNPLA3 148M Risk Allele With Non-cirrhotic Non-alcoholic Steatohepatitis With Fibrosis

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Proportion of Participants Achieving NASH Resolution Without”: Odds Ratio (OR) 0.20 (95% CI 0.02–1.78), p=0.116
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=220
  • Serious adverse events: AZD2693 25 mg 10%; AZD2693 50 mg 4%; Placebo 9%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 5 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
