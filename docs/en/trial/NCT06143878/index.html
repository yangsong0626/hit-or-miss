A Study of JNJ-77242113 for the Treatment of Participants With Moderate to Severe Plaque Psoriasis

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved an Investigator's Gl”: Treatment difference 57.60 (95% CI 49.90–64.20), p=<0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved Psoriasis Area and S”: Treatment difference 51.20 (95% CI 44.50–57.30), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=774
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo Controlled Period (Week 0 to 16): Placebo 3%; Placebo Controlled Period (Week 0 to 16): JNJ-7724 2%; Placebo Controlled Period (Week 0 to 16): Deucrava 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy

Editor’s analysis

Met. In ICONIC-ADVANCE 1, J&J's oral IL-23 receptor antagonist peptide icotrokinra (JNJ-2113) raised IGA and PASI90 responses 50–60 points over placebo in moderate-to-severe psoriasis, with a deucravacitinib active control. An oral peptide approaching injectable biologic efficacy is an important milestone in the race for oral psoriasis drugs.
