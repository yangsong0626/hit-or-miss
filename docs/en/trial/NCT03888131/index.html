FORSYYN NCT03888131

Foster® pMDI (CHF 1535) Versus Symbicort® Turbohaler in COPD Patient (FORSYYN)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline in Pre-dose Morning First Expiratory Vo”: Adjusted Mean Difference -0.00 (95% CI -0.03–0.02), p=<0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=750
  • Serious adverse events: Run-in and Randomized CHF 1535 100/6 µg pMDI (SAF 12%; Run-in and Randomized Symbicort® Turbohaler® (SAF 13%

Likely reasons

  • This is a non-inferiority test; meeting it means no worse than the comparator, not better
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful

Editor’s analysis

Met (non-inferiority). The Foster pressurised metered-dose inhaler vs the Symbicort dry-powder inhaler had an FEV1 difference of 0, with the p value from a non-inferiority test. It proves "no worse than the comparator" for device substitution, not better.
