Study of Palifosfamide-tris in Combination With Carboplatin and Etoposide in Chemotherapy Naïve Patients With Extensive-Stage Small Cell Lung Cancer (The MATISSE Study)

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=188

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Enrollment / operations”

Editor’s analysis

Business decision. A trial of palifosfamide in extensive-stage small cell lung cancer stopped enrollment after the development plan changed following the failure of the PICASSO 3 phase 3 in soft-tissue sarcoma, unrelated to safety.

Why it stopped

Enrollment / operations · “Sponsor closed enrollment in April 2013 following a change in the palifosfamide development plan (business decision), not for safety reasons. Enrolled participants continued treatment and were followed for survival”
