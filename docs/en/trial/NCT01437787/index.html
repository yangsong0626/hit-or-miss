Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

The registry gives no reason; the background is safety. JAKARTA was the pivotal trial of the JAK2 inhibitor fedratinib (SAR302503) in myelofibrosis; efficacy met the target, but in 2013 the FDA halted development after cases of Wernicke's encephalopathy. After further data analysis, fedratinib was approved in 2019 with required vitamin B1 monitoring.
