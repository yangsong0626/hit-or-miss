Chiesi Farmaceutici · Phase 3 · Other

FUTURE NCT01245569

A Study in Patients With Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease

Partly met

  • Primary endpoint “Area Under the Curve (AUC) 0-30min Standardized by Time of C”: Adjusted Mean Difference 0.07 (95% CI 0.05–0.10), p=<0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Transition Dyspnoea Index (TDI) Score at Day 84”: Adjusted Mean Difference 0.11 (95% CI -0.45–0.67), p=0.705
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=419
  • Serious adverse events: Run-in and Randomized Foster® (Safety Set) 2%; Run-in and Seretide® Accuhaler® (Safety Set) 6%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo

Editor’s analysis

Partly met. Foster vs Seretide in COPD had significantly faster onset (FEV1 AUC0-30min) but no difference in the dyspnoea index. It proves faster onset, not better long-term symptom control.
