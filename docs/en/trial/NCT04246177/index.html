Safety and Efficacy of Lenvatinib (E7080/MK-7902) With Pembrolizumab (MK-3475)… NCT04246177

Safety and Efficacy of Lenvatinib (E7080/MK-7902) With Pembrolizumab (MK-3475) in Combination With Transarterial Chemoembolization (TACE) in Participants With Incurable/Non-metastatic Hepatocellular Carcinoma (MK-7902-012/E7080-G000-318/LEAP-012)

Partly met

  • Primary endpoint “Progression Free Survival (PFS) Per Response Evaluation Crit”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.66 (95% CI 0.51–0.84), p=0.0002
  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival (OS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.98 (95% CI 0.76–1.26), p=.4442
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=480
  • Serious adverse events: Lenvatinib + Pembrolizumab + TACE 51%; Placebo + TACE 26%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The comparator is an active drug; beating existing therapy is a higher bar than beating placebo
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 26 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Ostensibly a business decision; the real background is no overall survival benefit. LEAP-012 added lenvatinib + pembrolizumab to TACE in intermediate hepatocellular carcinoma; PFS improved (HR 0.66) but OS HR was 0.98 and serious adverse events doubled with the combination, after which the sponsor closed the trial.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Business Reasons”
