RELIANCE-II NCT04855747

A Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of REL-1017 as Adjunctive Treatment for Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Change From Baseline to Day 28 in MADRS Total Score”: REL-1017 -14.81 Scores on a; Placebo -13.90 Scores on a
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=236
  • Serious adverse events: REL-1017 0%; Placebo 0%

Likely reasons

  • There is a control arm but no registered test of the primary endpoint; only the gap between arms can be seen

Editor’s analysis

Stopped after interim analysis. A trial of esmethadone (REL-1017, an NMDA antagonist) in major depression was stopped by the sponsor after a sample-size re-estimation interim analysis at about 75% enrollment. Another phase 3 in the same programme had already failed; taken together this indicates insufficient efficacy.

Why it stopped

Efficacy / futility · “Study REL-1017-302 was terminated early by the Sponsor following the prespecified interim analysis for sample size re-estimation at approximately 75% enrollment of the planned sample size (N=280). Safety data were collected.”
