OASIS 2 NCT05132088

Research Study to Investigate How Well Semaglutide Tablets Taken Once Daily Work in East Asian People Who Are Overweight or Living With Obesity

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in Body Weight”: Treatment Difference -13.07 (95% CI -15.61–-10.52), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Achieved Body Weight Reduction Gr”: Treatment odds ratio 23.00 (95% CI 10.28–51.42), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=201
  • Serious adverse events: Oral Semaglutide 50 mg 6%; Oral Semaglutide Placebo 9%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
