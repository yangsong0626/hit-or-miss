Editor’s analysis

Business decision. PATHFNDR-2 tested the oral SST2 agonist paltusotine in treatment-naive acromegaly and was stopped early for commercial reasons. The pivotal PATHFNDR-1 trial was already positive and supports the filing, so closing this trial does not mean the drug failed.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “The study was stopped early for business reasons (not due to changes in the benefits or risks of paltusotine).”
