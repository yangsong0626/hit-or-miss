ROCKET-ASTRO NCT05704738

A Study to Evaluate Rocatinlimab (AMG 451) in Adolescent Participants With Moderate-to-severe Atopic Dermatitis (AD)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Achieved vIGA-AD 1 Response With ”: Common risk difference percentage 24.70 (95% CI 15.60–33.20), p=< 0.001
  • Primary endpoint “Number of Participants Who Achieved EASI 75 at Week 24”: Common risk difference percentage 32.80 (95% CI 22.10–42.70), p=< 0.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=532
  • Serious adverse events: Initiation Period: Placebo ± TCS/TCI 4%; Initiation Period: Roca 150mg Q4W ± TCS/TCI 1%; Initiation Period: Roca 300mg Q4W ± TCS/TCI 2%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust

Editor’s analysis

Met. ROCKET-ASTRO tested rocatinlimab in adolescent atopic dermatitis; the response-rate gain (25–33 points) was larger than in adult trials, perhaps because adolescents have shorter disease duration and a different inflammatory burden.
