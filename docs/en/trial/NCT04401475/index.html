A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of EB05 + SOC vs. Placebo + SOC in Adult Hospitalized Patients With COVID-19

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=278

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Sponsor decision / strategy”

Editor’s analysis

Business decision. EB05 (a TLR4 antibody) for COVID-related acute respiratory distress syndrome; the sponsor said it stopped for commercial reasons only. With fewer severe COVID cases, enrollment and commercial prospects for such indications fell sharply.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “Edesa has made the decision to terminate this study solely for business reasons. The study termination was not related to any safety concerns.”
