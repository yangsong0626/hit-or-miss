A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of DA-1229 (Evogliptin) in Patient's Calcific Aortic Valve Disease With Mild to Moderate Aortic Stenosis (EVOID-AS)

Stopped

  • Design: randomised, quadruple-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=486

Likely reasons

  • Sponsor's stated reason classified as “Sponsor decision / strategy”

Editor’s analysis

Business decision. Evogliptin (a DPP-4 inhibitor) for calcific aortic valve disease was a drug-repurposing attempt; the sponsor said explicitly it was unrelated to safety. Slowly progressing valve disease needs long follow-up to show structural benefit, a heavy financial burden for a small company.

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “The study is terminated due to sponsor's business decision. There were no safety issues influencing this course of action.”
