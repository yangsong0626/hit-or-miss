AstraZeneca · Phase 2 · GI / liver

ZEAL NCT05516498

Zibotentan and Dapagliflozin Combination, EvAluated in Liver Cirrhosis (ZEAL Study)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Absolute Change in HVPG From Baseline to Week 6, Part A”: Mean Difference (Final Values) 1.02 (90% CI -0.31–2.35), p=0.896
  • Primary endpoint “Absolute Change in HVPG From Baseline to Week 6, Part B”: Mean Difference (Final Values) -0.10 (90% CI -1.26–1.05), p=0.442
  • Design: randomised, triple-blind, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=205
  • Serious adverse events: Part A Zibo/Dapa 2.5 mg/10 mg 0%; Part A Placebo 0%; Part B Dapa 10 mg 15%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • The trial stopped early with fewer patients than planned, reducing statistical power
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 18 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Why it stopped

Sponsor decision / strategy · “The study was ended earlier than planned due to a sponsor decision.”
