Eli Lilly · Phase 3 · Cardio / metabolic / renal

ATTAIN-J NCT05931380

A Study of Once-Daily Oral Orforglipron (LY3502970) in Japanese Adult Participants With Obesity Disease

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Percent Change From Baseline in Body Weight at Week 72”: LS Mean Difference (Final values) -5.28 (95% CI -7.52–-3.03), p=<.001
  • Primary endpoint “Percentage of Participants Who Achieved With Greater Than or”: Risk Difference (RD) 30.36 (95% CI 15.09–45.62), p=<.001
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=238
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo QD 8%; 6 mg Orforglipron QD 2%; 12 mg Orforglipron QD 5%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Placebo-controlled: it shows efficacy, but head-to-head data are needed to know whether it beats existing therapy
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 8 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
