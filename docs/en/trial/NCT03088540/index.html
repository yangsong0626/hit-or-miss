Study of REGN 2810 Compared to Platinum-Based Chemotherapies in Participants With Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Overall Survival (OS)”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.66 (95% CI 0.55–0.79), p=<0.0001
  • Primary endpoint “Progression-free Survival (PFS) Per Blinded IRC”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.56 (95% CI 0.47–0.67), p=<0.0001
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=712
  • Serious adverse events: Cemiplimab 37%; Extended Treatment: Cemiplimab + Chemotherapy 27%; Chemotherapy 30%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is well away from the line of no effect; the effect is robust
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 10 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Met. EMPOWER-Lung 1 in first-line advanced non-small cell lung cancer with PD-L1 ≥50%: cemiplimab monotherapy vs platinum chemotherapy, OS HR 0.66 and PFS HR 0.56. The basis for cemiplimab's first-line lung approval, with an effect similar to pembrolizumab in KEYNOTE-024.
