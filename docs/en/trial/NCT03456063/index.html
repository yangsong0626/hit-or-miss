A Study of Neoadjuvant Atezolizumab Plus Chemotherapy Versus Placebo Plus Chemo… NCT03456063

A Study of Neoadjuvant Atezolizumab Plus Chemotherapy Versus Placebo Plus Chemotherapy in Patients With Resectable Stage II, IIIA, or Select IIIB Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (IMpower030)

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Stage IIB-IIIB Wild-type (WT): Independent Review Facility (”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.77 (95% CI 0.58–1.02), p=0.0704
  • Design: randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=453
  • Serious adverse events: Placebo + Platinum-based Chemotherapy (Control) 32%; Atezolizumab + Platinum-based Chemotherapy 46%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate leans one way but the confidence interval crosses the line of no effect; the effect may be small or the sample too small to detect it
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 14 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention

Editor’s analysis

Missed. IMpower030 tested neoadjuvant plus adjuvant atezolizumab + chemotherapy in resectable non-small cell lung cancer; EFS HR 0.77, p=0.07, favourable but not significant, with serious adverse events 14 points higher in the immunotherapy arm. Same-class perioperative trials of nivolumab, pembrolizumab and durvalumab were all positive, so this lag more likely reflects statistical power and population than ineffective PD-L1 blockade in this setting.
