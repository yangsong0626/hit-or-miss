TRAVERSE NCT01387555

A Phase 2b Study of Modified Vaccinia Virus to Treat Patients Advanced Liver Cancer Who Failed Sorafenib

Missed

  • Primary endpoint “Survival”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 1.19 (95% CI 0.78–1.80), p=0.426
  • Design: randomised, open-label, multi-arm
  • actual enrollment n=129
  • Serious adverse events: JX-594 + Best Supportive Care 60%; Best Supportive Care 40%

Likely reasons

  • The point estimate sits almost on the line of no effect; the arms barely differ, suggesting the drug itself does not work
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 20 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
