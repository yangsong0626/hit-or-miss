A Study of Coformulated Favezelimab/Pembrolizumab (MK-4280A) Versus Physician's Choice Chemotherapy in PD-(L)1-refractory, Relapsed or Refractory Classical Hodgkin Lymphoma (MK-4280A-008)

Met

  • Primary endpoint “Progression Free Survival (PFS) Per Lugano Response Criteria”: Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.76 (95% CI 0.55–1.05), p=0.0462
  • Design: randomised, open-label, active-controlled
  • actual enrollment n=203
  • Serious adverse events: Favezelimab/Pembrolizumab 24%; Bendamustine or Gemcitabine 28%; Bendamustine or Gemcitabine Crossed Over to Faveze 17%

Likely reasons

  • The confidence interval is close to the line of no effect; the effect is modest, so check whether secondary endpoints and subgroups agree
  • Won against an active comparator, which is more clinically meaningful
  • Open-label design; subjective endpoints may be biased
  • Serious adverse event rates differ by 11 percentage points across arms; safety needs attention
