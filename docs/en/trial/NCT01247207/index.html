Editor’s analysis

The registry says only "sponsor decision". The pivotal trial of ataluren in nonsense-mutation Duchenne muscular dystrophy (Study 041) missed its primary endpoint, and the EU then declined to renew the drug's conditional marketing authorisation. Closing this long-term study relates to the product's overall regulatory outlook.
