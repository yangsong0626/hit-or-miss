CD19-Targeted NEX-T CAR T (Bristol Myers Squibb) is a cell therapy drug targeting CD19. As of 2026-11-16, the site tracks 3 registry trials of it; the furthest has reached phase 3. Main conditions: Systemic Sclerosis, Defined by parent protocol. The study will enroll all adult and paediatric subjects who received at least one genetically modified T cells infusion in a previous Celgene sponsored study., Chronic Immune Thrombocytopenia (cITP) and Autoimmune Hemolytic Anemia (AIHA).
