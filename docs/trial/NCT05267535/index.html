Piromelatine 20 mg in Participants With Mild Dementia Due to Alzheimer's Disease NCT05267535

Piromelatine 20 mg in Participants With Mild Dementia Due to Alzheimer's Disease

无统计检验

  • 主要终点「Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale Cognitive Subscale (ADA」：Piromelatine -1.90 ADAS-Cog sco；Placebo -2.00 ADAS-Cog sco
  • 设计：随机，四盲，安慰剂对照
  • 实际入组 216 例
  • 严重不良事件：Placebo (Double Blind) 0%；Piromelatine (Double Blind) 4%；Placebo (Open Label) 5%

可能原因

  • 有对照组但没有登记主要终点的统计检验，只能看两组数值的差距

编辑分析

疗效失败。piromelatine（褪黑素与 5-HT 受体激动剂）治疗轻度阿尔茨海默病痴呆，在认知量表和其他神经精神指标上都没有超越安慰剂。

终止原因

疗效 / 无效性 · “Treatment with 20 mg piromelatine did not demonstrate efficacy over treatment with placebo based on analysis with the ADAS-Cog 14 subscale and with other cognitive and neuropsychiatric parameters.”
