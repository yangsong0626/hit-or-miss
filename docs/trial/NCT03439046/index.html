BioItaLEE NCT03439046

Study of the Molecular Features of Postmenopausal Women With HR+ HER2-negative aBC on First-line Treatment With Ribociclib and Letrozole and, in Patients With a PIK3CA Mutation, on Second-line Treatment With Alpelisib Plus Fulvestrant

无统计检验

  • 主要终点「Progression-Free Survival (PFS) by Cycle 1 Day 15 Complete M」：Ribociclib+Letrozole (Core Phase) 55.82 months
  • 设计：开放标签，多组对比
  • 实际入组 287 例
  • 严重不良事件：Ribociclib+Letrozole (Core Phase) 28%；Alpelisib+Fulvestrant (Extension Phase) 29%

可能原因

  • 有对照组但没有登记主要终点的统计检验，只能看两组数值的差距

编辑分析

这不是失败。诺华提前终止 BioItaLEE 是因为已收集的数据足以完成主要终点分析，属于正常结束。

终止原因

目标已达成 · “Novartis terminated the trial early because the collected data were sufficient to perform the analysis stated for the primary endpoint.”
