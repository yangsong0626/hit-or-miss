ADHAND NCT05958407

A 32-week Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tralokinumab in Subjects With Moderate-to-severe Atopic Hand Eczema Who Are Candidates for Systemic Therapy

达到

  • 主要终点「Percentage of Participants With IGA-AHE Score of 0 (Clear) o」：treatment difference in percentage 29.60（95% CI 19.20–40.00），p=<.001
  • 设计：随机，双盲，安慰剂对照
  • 实际入组 235 例
  • 严重不良事件：Tralokinumab DB 2%；Placebo DB 1%；Tralokinumab OL 2%

可能原因

  • 置信区间离无效线较远，效应稳健
  • 对照为安慰剂，证明了「有效」，但和现有疗法谁更好还需头对头数据
