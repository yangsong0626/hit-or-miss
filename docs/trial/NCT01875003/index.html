A Study of Lebrikizumab in Adolescent Participants With Uncontrolled Asthma Who… NCT01875003

A Study of Lebrikizumab in Adolescent Participants With Uncontrolled Asthma Who Are on Inhaled Corticosteroids and a Second Controller Medication

无统计检验

  • 主要终点「Rate of Asthma Exacerbations During 52-Week Placebo Controll」：Placebo 0.43 events per p；Lebrikizumab 37.5 mg 0.26 events per p；Lebrikizumab 125 mg 0.21 events per p
  • 设计：随机，双盲，安慰剂对照
  • 实际入组 346 例
  • 严重不良事件：Placebo 5%；Lebrikizumab 37.5 mg 5%；Lebrikizumab 125 mg 7%

可能原因

  • 有对照组但没有登记主要终点的统计检验，只能看两组数值的差距

编辑分析

项目终止的连带结果。lebrikizumab 的成人哮喘三期结果不一致（一项阳性一项阴性），罗氏停止了该药的哮喘开发，这项青少年试验随之终止。该药后来在特应性皮炎中获批。

终止原因

申办方决定 / 战略 · “Sponsor decision to discontinue lebrikizumab development for the treatment of asthma.”
