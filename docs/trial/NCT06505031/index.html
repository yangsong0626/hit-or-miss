达到

  • 主要终点「Change From Baseline to Week 12 in Mean Sleep Latency From t」：LS Mean Difference 20.09（95% CI 16.57–23.61），p=<0.001
  • 主要终点「Change From Baseline to Week 12 in ESS Total Score」：Least Square (LS) Mean Difference -9.53（95% CI -11.10–-7.97），p=<0.001
