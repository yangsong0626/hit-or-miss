A Clinical Trial to Assess the Safety and the Anti-caries Efficacy of COL 101 (Arginine) Non-fluoride Dentifrices in Comparison With 0.24% Sodium Fluoride (1100 Ppm F) Dentifrice Control in 10 to 14-year-old Children

达到

  • 主要终点「Incremental Subject-wise Decayed Missing or Filled Surfaces 」：LS Means Difference 0.49（95% CI 0.06–0.92），p=0.0267
  • 设计：随机，四盲，活性药物对照
  • 实际入组 2025 例
  • 严重不良事件：0.24% Sodium Fluoride Dentifrice 0%；1.5% Arginine Dentifrice 0%；4.0% Arginine Dentifrice 0%

可能原因

  • 置信区间贴近无效线，效应幅度有限，需看次要终点和亚组是否一致
  • 在活性药物对照下胜出，临床意义更强
