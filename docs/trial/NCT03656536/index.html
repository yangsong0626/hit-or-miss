FIGHT-302 NCT03656536

A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Pemigatinib Versus Chemotherapy in Unresectable or Metastatic Cholangiocarcinoma

达到

  • 主要终点「Randomized Treatment Period: Progression-free Survival (PFS)」：Hazard Ratio (HR) 0.58（95% CI 0.39–0.87），p=0.0078
  • 设计：随机，开放标签，活性药物对照
  • 实际入组 167 例
  • 严重不良事件：Randomized Period: Pemigatinib 13.5 mg QD 28%；Randomized Period: Gemcitabine 1000 mg/m^2 Plus Ci 23%；Transition Period: Pemigatinib 13.5 mg QD 21%

可能原因

  • 置信区间离无效线较远，效应稳健
  • 在活性药物对照下胜出，临床意义更强
  • 开放标签设计，主观终点可能有偏倚
  • 各组严重不良事件率相差 6 个百分点，安全性需要关注

编辑分析

入组困难，背景是标准治疗改变。FIGHT-302 在 FGFR2 融合胆管癌一线中对比佩米替尼与吉西他滨+顺铂，但一线标准治疗已变为度伐利尤单抗+化疗，对照组过时导致无法入组。这是靶向药一线确证试验常见的困境。

终止原因

入组 / 运营 · “The study was terminated due to lack of enrollment resulting from a change in the standard of care for the first-line treatment of patients with cholangiocarcinoma. There were no safety concerns that contributed to this decision.”
