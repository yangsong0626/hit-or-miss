编辑分析

期中分析后终止。esmethadone（REL-1017，NMDA 拮抗剂）在重度抑郁中的试验，在约 75% 入组时的样本量重估期中分析后被申办方终止。同一项目另一项三期已失败，结合来看属于疗效不足。

终止原因

疗效 / 无效性 · “Study REL-1017-302 was terminated early by the Sponsor following the prespecified interim analysis for sample size re-estimation at approximately 75% enrollment of the planned sample size (N=280). Safety data were collected.”
