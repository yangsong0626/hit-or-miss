Quanti OBR NCT05915728

A Study to Compare How Well Gadoquatrane Works and Its Safety With an Already Available Contrast Agent for MRI in People With Any Known or Suspected Problems of the Body (Except Brain or Spinal Cord-related Problems)

达到

  • 主要终点「Visualization Parameter Contrast Enhancement Assessed by Sep」：Difference in mean average score 2.28（95% CI 2.17–2.40），p=<0.0001
  • 主要终点「Visualization Parameter Delineation Assessed by Separate Bli」：Difference in mean average score 2.16（95% CI 2.04–2.28），p=<0.0001
  • 设计：随机，四盲，多组对比
  • 实际入组 410 例
  • 严重不良事件：Gadoquatrane 0%；SoC GBCAs 0%

可能原因

  • 置信区间离无效线较远，效应稳健

编辑分析

达到。拜耳新一代钆造影剂 gadoquatrane 在体部 MRI 中，图像增强评分优于已获批大环类造影剂。卖点是更低的钆剂量，交叉设计和盲法阅片让结果比较可靠。
