Study of OCA in Combination With BZF Evaluating Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability in Participants With PBC

达到

  • 主要终点「Change in Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP) From Baseline in the Do」：Least square mean difference -46.90（95% CI -92.20–-1.60），p=0.043
  • 设计：随机，四盲，活性药物对照
  • 实际入组 75 例
  • 严重不良事件：DB Period, Placebo + BZF 200 mg IR 11%；DB Period, OCA 5 mg + BZF 200 mg IR 5%；DB Period, Placebo + BZF 400 mg SR 16%

可能原因

  • 置信区间贴近无效线，效应幅度有限，需看次要终点和亚组是否一致
  • 在活性药物对照下胜出，临床意义更强
  • 各组严重不良事件率相差 14 个百分点，安全性需要关注

终止原因

申办方决定 / 战略 · “Intercept made a business decision to terminate the study based on FDA's request for voluntary withdrawal of Ocaliva and the issuance of clinical hold on studies under US IND involving OCA.”
