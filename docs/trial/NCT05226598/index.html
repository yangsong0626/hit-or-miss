Study of Pembrolizumab/Vibostolimab Coformulation (MK-7684A) in Combination With Chemotherapy Versus Pembrolizumab Plus Chemotherapy in Participants With Metastatic Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (MK-7684A-007/KEYVIBE-007)

未达到

  • 设计：随机，三盲，活性药物对照
  • 实际入组 739 例
  • 严重不良事件：MK-7684A + Chemotherapy 57%；Pembrolizumab + Chemotherapy 49%

可能原因

  • 申办方给出的原因归为「疗效 / 无效性」

编辑分析

疗效失败。KEYVIBE-007 在非小细胞肺癌一线中，vibostolimab/帕博利珠单抗共配方+化疗因无效终止，是默沙东 TIGIT 项目整体停止的直接依据之一。

终止原因

疗效 / 无效性 · “MK-7684A-007 was terminated early due to futility. A non-submission CSR, not intended to support any registration, was issued; therefore, an abbreviated CSR was generated.”
