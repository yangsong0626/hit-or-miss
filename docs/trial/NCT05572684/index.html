A Safety, Tolerability and Efficacy Study of NC410 Plus Pembrolizumab in Participants With Advanced Unresectable or Metastatic Solid Tumors

描述性

  • 主要终点「Number of Participants With Treatment-emergent Adverse Event」：NC410 30mg and Pembrolizumab 3.00 Participants；60mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 11.00 Participants；100mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 62.00 Participants
  • 设计：开放标签，单臂无对照
  • 实际入组 97 例
  • 严重不良事件：NC410 30mg and Pembrolizumab 33%；60mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 64%；100mg of NC410 and Pembrolizumab 38%

可能原因

  • 单臂或无对照设计，注册库只登记了描述性数据，无法用统计检验判断成败，需要和历史对照或同类药物比较

终止原因

申办方决定 / 战略 · “Following completion of Phase 1b, the Sponsor decided not to initiate Part 2 of the trial.”
