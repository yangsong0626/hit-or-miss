Quanti CNS NCT05915702

A Study to Compare How Well Gadoquatrane Works and Its Safety With an Already Available Contrast Agent for MRI in People With Known or Suspected Brain or Spinal Cord-related Problems

达到

  • 主要终点「Visualization Parameter Contrast Enhancement Assessed by Sep」：Difference in mean average score 1.11（95% CI 1.00–1.23），p=<0.0001
  • 主要终点「Visualization Parameter Delineation Assessed by Separate Bli」：Difference in mean average score 0.99（95% CI 0.87–1.10），p=<0.0001
  • 设计：随机，四盲，多组对比
  • 实际入组 321 例
  • 严重不良事件：Gadoquatrane 1%；Approved Macrocyclic GBCAs 0%

可能原因

  • 置信区间离无效线较远，效应稳健

编辑分析

达到。gadoquatrane 在中枢神经系统 MRI 中的增强和边界清晰度评分也优于已获批大环类造影剂，与体部试验一致。
