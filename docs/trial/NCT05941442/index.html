A Study to Evaluate Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Darigabat in Participants With Panic Disorder

未达到

  • 主要终点「Percentage of Participants Free of Panic Attacks as Assessed」：Difference in proportion of participants -0.06（95% CI -0.33–0.23），p=0.7488
  • 设计：随机，四盲，安慰剂对照
  • 实际入组 80 例
  • 严重不良事件：Placebo 0%；Darigabat 25 mg BID 0%

可能原因

  • 点估计几乎落在无效线上，两组基本没有差别，更像药物本身无效
  • 样本量只有 80 例，检出中等效应的把握度有限
  • 试验提前终止，实际入组少于计划，统计把握度下降

终止原因

入组 / 运营 · “Sponsor terminated due to significant enrollment and study design challenges”
