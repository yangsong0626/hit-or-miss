HAE CHAPTER-1 NCT05047185

Dose-ranging Study of Oral PHA-022121 for Prophylaxis Against Angioedema Attacks in Patients With Hereditary Angioedema Type I or Type II

达到

  • 主要终点「Number of Investigator-confirmed Hereditary Angioedema (HAE)」：Percent Reduction 84.46（95% CI 53.83–94.77），p=0.0008
  • 设计：随机，四盲，安慰剂对照
  • 实际入组 34 例
  • 严重不良事件：Part 1: Placebo 0%；Part 1: Deucrictibant 10 mg BID 0%；Part 1: Deucrictibant 20 mg BID 0%

可能原因

  • 置信区间离无效线较远，效应稳健
  • 对照为安慰剂，证明了「有效」，但和现有疗法谁更好还需头对头数据
  • 各组严重不良事件率相差 7 个百分点，安全性需要关注

终止原因

监管 · “Sponsor decision. All remaining participants (21 total) were rolled over from Part 2 to study PHA022121-C307 (NCT06679881).”
