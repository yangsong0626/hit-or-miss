EPIK-B3 NCT04251533

Study Assessing the Efficacy and Safety of Alpelisib + Nab-paclitaxel in Subjects With Advanced TNBC Who Carry Either a PIK3CA Mutation or Have PTEN Loss

终止

  • 设计：随机，双盲，安慰剂对照
  • 实际入组 137 例
  • 严重不良事件：Part A: Alpelisib + Nab-paclitaxelEdit 42%；Part A: Placebo + Nab-paclitaxelEdit 30%；Part B1: Alpelisib + Nab-paclitaxelEdit 40%

可能原因

  • 申办方给出的原因归为「入组 / 运营」

编辑分析

入组缓慢。EPIK-B3 在 PIK3CA 突变或 PTEN 缺失的三阴性乳腺癌中测试 alpelisib+白蛋白紫杉醇，因持续入组缓慢而停止。生物标志物筛选让合格患者很少，这是精准治疗试验在小人群中的常见问题。

终止原因

入组 / 运营 · “The study was terminated following a strategic decision to halt recruitment due to persistently slow enrollment. This decision was not driven by any new or unexpected safety concerns.”
