DISCOVER NCT02842086

Study to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of Emtricitabine and Tenofovir Alafenamide (F/TAF) Fixed-Dose Combination Once Daily for Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis in Men and Transgender Women Who Have Sex With Men and Are At Risk of HIV-1 Infection

正常结束

  • 设计：随机，双盲，多组对比
  • 实际入组 5399 例
  • 严重不良事件：Descovy (DVY) 7%；Truvada (TVD) 7%

可能原因

  • 申办方给出的原因归为「目标已达成」

编辑分析

这不是失败。DISCOVER 是 F/TAF（Descovy）用于 HIV 暴露前预防的关键试验，早已证明不劣于 F/TDF 并获批。终止的原因是长期安全性数据已经足够，研究内不再需要继续随访，属于目标完成后的正常关闭。

终止原因

目标已达成 · “Following review of accumulated safety data from DISCOVER and real-world use of F/TAF, the sponsor determined that the long-term safety of F/TAF had been demonstrated and no further safety monitoring within the study was required.”
