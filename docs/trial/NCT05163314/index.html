编辑分析

疗效失败的连带终止。soticlestat（胆固醇 24-羟化酶抑制剂）在 Dravet 综合征和 Lennox-Gastaut 综合征的三期 SKYLINE 和 SKYWAY 结果为阴性，武田因此关闭这项补充研究。罕见癫痫适应症已有多种获批药物，新机制需要明显的疗效优势才有意义。

终止原因

疗效 / 无效性 · “Due to negative results from phase 3 SKYLINE and SKYWAY studies and unrelated to patient safety it has been determined that supplementary data from TAK-935-3003 study is no longer necessary. Therefore, Takeda has made a decision to close this study”
