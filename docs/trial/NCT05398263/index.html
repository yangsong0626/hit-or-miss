SUNRISE NCT05398263

Tezepelumab Efficacy and Safety in Reducing Oral Corticosteroid Use in Adults With Oral Corticosteroid Dependent Asthma

达到

  • 主要终点「Proportion of Subjects by Categorised Percent Reduction From」：Cumulative Odds Ratio 2.93（95% CI 1.43–6.03），p=0.003
  • 设计：随机，三盲，安慰剂对照
  • 实际入组 125 例
  • 严重不良事件：Tezepelumab 8%；Placebo 13%

可能原因

  • 置信区间贴近无效线，效应幅度有限，需看次要终点和亚组是否一致
  • 对照为安慰剂，证明了「有效」，但和现有疗法谁更好还需头对头数据

编辑分析

入组困难，背景是临床实践改变。SUNRISE 测试 tezepelumab 帮助重度哮喘患者减少口服激素，但生物制剂普及后，长期依赖口服激素的患者越来越少，试验招不到人。这其实反映了生物制剂的成功。

终止原因

入组 / 运营 · “The study was discontinued due to slow recruitment, resulting from evolving clinical practices that reduced the use of maintenance oral corticosteroids for severe asthma treatment. The termination decision was not related to safety concerns.”
