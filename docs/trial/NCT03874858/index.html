可能原因

终止原因

疗效 / 无效性 · “Sponsor decision: The primary endpoint of the core/TF1 was achieved, and the sample size of Extension/TFR2 was unlikely to yield meaningful results. No new safety signals were observed. For these reasons, continuing the study was not justified.”
