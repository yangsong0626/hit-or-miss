终止

  • 设计：非随机，四盲，多组对比

可能原因

  • 申办方给出的原因归为「疗效 / 无效性」
  • 试验在入组第一位受试者前撤回

终止原因

疗效 / 无效性 · “The study was discontinued due to evolving scientific \& feasibility considerations that could adversely affect recruitment, thereby reducing the likelihood of generating a sufficiently robust dataset to support reliable efficacy \& safety conclusions.”
