编辑分析

疗效失败。latozinemab（AL001，抗 sortilin 抗体，提升颗粒体蛋白水平）治疗 GRN 突变额颞叶痴呆，没有达到减缓临床进展的共同主要终点。生物标志物层面的改变没有转化为临床获益，是神经退行性疾病药物的典型困境。

终止原因

疗效 / 无效性 · “The trial did not meet the clinical co-primary endpoint of slowing FTD-GRN progression, as measured by the Clinical Dementia Rating® plus National Alzheimer's Coordinating Center Frontotemporal Lobar Degeneration Sum of Boxes (CDR® plus NACC FTLD-SB)”
