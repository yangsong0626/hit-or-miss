TRuST NCT02875548

A Study to Assess Long-term Safety of Tazemetostat in Adult Participants of All Ages With Any Disease Treated With Tazemetostat in a Previous Clinical Study

描述性

  • 主要终点「Number of Participants With Treatment-Emergent Adverse Event」：Tazemetostat <=600 mg BD 13.00 Participants；Tazemetostat 800 mg BD 39.00 Participants；Tazemetostat 1200 mg BD 3.00 Participants
  • 设计：开放标签，单臂无对照
  • 实际入组 58 例
  • 严重不良事件：Tazemetostat <=600 mg BD 31%；Tazemetostat 800 mg BD 51%；Tazemetostat 1200 mg BD 67%

可能原因

  • 单臂或无对照设计，注册库只登记了描述性数据，无法用统计检验判断成败，需要和历史对照或同类药物比较

终止原因

入组 / 运营 · “Sponsor decision; no additional participant enrollment was anticipated.”
