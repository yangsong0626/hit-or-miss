Evaluation of Gadopiclenol for Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) in Japanese Adults and Children

达到

  • 主要终点「Lesion Visualization on Paired Images: Border Delineation」：Mean Difference (Net) 0.06（95% CI -0.02–0.15），p=<0.0001
  • 主要终点「Lesion Visualization on Paired Images: Internal Morphology」：Mean Difference (Net) 0.06（5% CI -0.03–0.14），p=<0.0001
  • 设计：随机，四盲，多组对比
  • 实际入组 240 例
  • 严重不良事件：Adult Cohort: TEAEs Occurring During and or After 1%；Adult Cohort: TEAEs Occurring During and or After 2%；Pediatric Cohort: TEAEs Occurring During and or Af 0%

可能原因

  • 这是非劣效检验，达到意味着不比对照差，不代表更好
